## Status definitions

- **Research queue** — identified but not yet investigated deeply
- **Investigating** — active evidence gathering
- **Watch** — credible thesis worth monitoring
- **High-conviction research candidate** — unusually strong evidence within this research framework; still not a recommendation
- **Deprioritised** — currently less attractive or less differentiated
- **Rejected thesis** — evidence materially undermines the original thesis
