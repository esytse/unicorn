## Decision rule

Entry price is not a generic “cheap” price. For Gate E it must connect:
**entry valuation → March-2028 operating outcome → exit valuation → remaining-window return → catalyst timing → downside → alternative use of capital.**

Bands are deliberately approximate. Evidence can override price; a lower price does not rescue a broken thesis.

## 1. TAI-TECH — ACTION / starter eligible

### Claim
The existing earnings engine is strong enough that the AI-power transition can plausibly produce a portfolio-relevant 18-month outcome without requiring a software-like rerating.

### Evidence
- Jan–Aug 2026 revenue NT$5.605bn, +32.2% y/y.
- May–Aug growth accelerated to +37.8%, +57.4%, +63.3%, +53.1%.
- 2025 EPS NT$10.26; Q2 2026 EPS ~NT$3.81 and margins expanded.
- AI mix rose from ~7% in 2024 to ~10% in 2025 and ~12–13% in H1 2026.
- Current equity ~NT$23–24bn at ~NT$206.

### March-2028 bridge
Illustrative, not forecast:

| Case | Earnings basis by Mar-28 | Multiple | Implied share value | Driver |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Bear | EPS ~NT$12–14 | 14–16x | ~NT$170–225 | AI stalls; TLVR pricing weak; auto carries growth |
| Base | EPS ~NT$18–21 | 20–22x | ~NT$360–460 | company growth normalizes but AI/CSP mix reaches high-teens/20% |
| Bull | EPS ~NT$23–27 | 24–27x | ~NT$550–730 | CSP/ASIC/TLVR/next-gen power becomes company-transforming |

At NT$190–210, the base bridge is roughly 1.7–2.4x and the bull materially higher. The return depends primarily on **earnings growth**, with some rerating in the upper cases; it does not require a pure narrative multiple.

### Entry
**Starter eligible NT$190–210** while revenue/margin acceleration and AI-mix direction remain intact.  
**Preferred add zone <=NT$185** if no thesis deterioration.

### Size
**5% starter; 7.5% ceiling before AI reaches ~20% or named CSP/ASIC qualification becomes visible.**

### Catalysts
- monthly revenue through Q4 2026;
- next earnings/margin update;
- AI mix approaching 15–20%;
- CSP/ASIC or 800V production evidence.

### Add / trim / sell
- **Add:** <=NT$185 with thesis intact, or evidence-led after AI mix >=20% / named qualification.
- **Trim:** price >~NT$350 before EPS/AI mix catches up materially.
- **Sell/reassess:** AI mix stalls low-teens, TLVR price competition compresses margins, or architecture reduces external magnetics content.

### Gate-E conclusion
**ACTION: research-level 5% starter is eligible in the NT$190–210 band.** Current ~NT$206 is inside the band. Manual execution only.

- adjusted attributable profit HK$433.1m, +20.4%;

## 3. Namuga — WAIT / evidence-led entry

### Claim
Namuga has the most convex new programme, but entry must respect the binary gap between “mass-production award” and disclosed programme economics.

### Evidence
- dedicated robotics production line;
- PoC → technical verification → exclusive stereo-camera supply;
- initial supply expected early 2027; full-scale production expected 2028;
- profitable legacy base;
- current price KRW15,690 versus pre-announcement ~KRW13,880.

### March-2028 bridge
The decisive variable is robot-programme revenue, not a small change in the legacy multiple.

| Case | Programme state by Mar-28 | Earnings / valuation implication | Share-value direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | delays / immaterial initial orders | legacy earnings weaken; 8–10x type valuation | ~KRW9–13k risk zone |
| Base | initial programme reaches ~5–10% revenue with credible 2028 ramp | rerating toward ~15–20x on recovering earnings | ~KRW20–28k |
| Bull | programme becomes ~15–25%+ revenue with strong margin / additional OEM evidence | ~20–25x on materially higher earnings | ~KRW32–45k+ |

### Entry
Two distinct paths:
- **Price-led speculative:** <=KRW13–14k while award/timing remain intact.
- **Evidence-led:** up to ~KRW17k **after** first material order/revenue economics establish that the programme can affect consolidated earnings.

At KRW15,690 today, price is not sufficiently discounted to compensate for undisclosed programme economics.

### Size
Before economics: **max 4% speculative option**.  
After material order economics: ceiling can rise toward **7.5%** after refresh.

### Catalyst
First 2027 robot-vision order/revenue economics is the highest-information event.

### Gate-E conclusion
**WAIT.** Do not chase the announcement. Evidence can justify paying a higher price later.
