# Unicorn Potential — Discovery Funnel

**Status:** Active canonical discovery methodology  
**Backlog:** #235  
**Purpose:** identify listed companies where a structural bottleneck can plausibly create a **3x–10x equity outcome**, before Gate-E decides whether today's setup warrants capital.

## Why this layer exists

The broad ranked universe answers **what deserves research attention**. Gate E answers **what, if anything, is actionable at today's price and evidence**. Neither question is identical to **where could a genuine multibagger emerge?**

Unicorn Potential is therefore a separate funnel:

**world change → bottleneck migration → company capture → non-linear financial transmission → reverse-underwritten multibagger → evidence validation → Gate E**

A large high-quality beneficiary can rank highly in the broad universe while failing Unicorn Potential because its starting scale makes a 5x outcome implausible. Conversely, a lower-ranked, poorly understood small company can deserve urgent work if a credible 5x mechanism is emerging.

## Seven tests

| Test | Question | Promotion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Bottleneck migration | What becomes scarce if the underlying transition is 10x larger? | capacity, qualification, lead-time, IP, safety or physical constraints that are hard to add/substitute |
| Demand convexity | Does company-addressable demand grow faster than the end market? | rising content/unit, test steps, power/rack, components/system, attach rate or recurring usage |
| Company purity | If the thesis works, does it transform this company's numbers? | new exposure is or can become material to revenue, profit, FCF or asset value |
| Operating leverage | Can incremental demand produce disproportionate shareholder economics? | high incremental margins, royalties, service mix, utilization, fixed-cost absorption or pricing power |
| Under-recognition | Is the market still valuing an older/slower business? | segment inflection not reflected in consensus, classification, valuation or reported mix |
| Multibagger feasibility | Can a 3x/5x/10x outcome be built without heroic assumptions? | future revenue × margin × defensible multiple / dilution bridge supports the equity value |
| Evidence confidence | Are the key links directly evidenced and falsifiable? | primary/customer/technical evidence, dated checkpoints, explicit counter-case |

**Do not sum these into a single pseudo-precise score.** Treat them as gates. A fatal failure in shareholder capture, dilution, required market share, capital intensity or multibagger arithmetic can stop promotion even when the technology thesis is excellent.

## Reverse-underwriting template

For each serious candidate, start with **today's fully diluted equity value / enterprise value** and solve backwards.

| Outcome | Required future equity value | Required operating bridge | Evidence question |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1x** | current value | What does today's valuation already assume? | What must merely remain true? |
| **3x** | 3 × current value | revenue / EBITDA / FCF / royalty base needed at a defensible multiple | Can current customers/capacity/TAM support it? |
| **5x** | 5 × current value | same, including dilution and net debt/cash | Which assumptions are genuinely evidenced today? |
| **10x** | 10 × current value | same | Does this require implausible share, margins, capital or multiple? |

Every bridge should expose:
- starting market cap / EV and observation date;
- share count and plausible dilution;
- revenue or economic base required;
- sustainable margin / FCF conversion;
- terminal multiple appropriate to the business;
- implied market share / unit volume / capacity;
- capital required to reach that scale;
- time horizon;
- which parts are **FACT**, **INTERPRETATION**, **HYPOTHESIS** and **OPEN QUESTION**;
- strongest disconfirming evidence.

A 5x case is **not a price target or forecast**. It is a hurdle: *what must become true, and is that becoming more or less plausible?*

## Discovery signals: search for change, not “AI stocks”

The discovery process should prioritize evidence of an economic state change:

- orders/backlog growing far faster than the company or end market;
- capacity doubling, utilization approaching constraints or a new factory tied to named demand;
- first Tier-1 qualification, process-of-record status or new production socket;
- customer prepayment, take-or-pay, LTA or multi-year committed capacity;
- a formerly tiny segment reaching ~10–20%+ of company revenue;
- mix shift that raises gross/operating margin;
- unit cost falling rapidly with volume;
- first recurring royalty / service / software attach;
- management creating new segment disclosure because the exposure became material;
- customer diversification after qualification;
- competitor exit, long lead times, constrained specialty inputs;
- old-business valuation persisting while the new engine becomes financially visible.

These are **candidate-generation signals**, not automatic bullish evidence. Each must survive substitution, supply response, vertical integration, cyclicality, customer concentration, dilution and valuation tests.

## Three distinct outputs

### 1. Broad ranked universe
Continuous inventory of sufficiently evidenced listed companies. No cap. Answers: **where should research attention go?**

### 2. Unicorn Candidates
A deliberately smaller, variable set. Entry requires a plausible non-linear equity mechanism plus enough evidence to justify reverse-underwriting. There is **no target number**.

States:
- **POTENTIAL** — credible mechanism; reverse-underwrite incomplete.
- **UNDERWRITE** — 3x/5x/10x bridge is being tested.
- **VALIDATED POTENTIAL** — 3x/5x path survives current evidence, but this is still not a buy signal.
- **FAILED / PARKED** — required economics, scale, dilution or evidence make the multibagger mechanism implausible at the current setup.

### 3. Gate E
Capital-allocation layer. Answers: **does today's price/evidence/catalyst/downside justify a research-level ACTION?** A VALIDATED POTENTIAL candidate can still be NO ACTION because price already captures the upside.

## Promotion discipline

A company should enter **VALIDATED POTENTIAL** only when:
1. a 3x path survives conservative operating assumptions;
2. a 5x path is possible without requiring an obviously implausible market share, terminal multiple or financing structure;
3. at least one leading indicator is already observable;
4. the strongest counter-case is documented;
5. the bridge explicitly includes dilution/capital needs;
6. the thesis has dated falsification checkpoints.

A 10x path is valuable as a stress test, but **10x plausibility is not required**. The purpose is to find asymmetric companies without turning the repository into a collection of heroic bull cases.

## Relationship to the portfolio

Unicorn Potential prioritizes **discovery and underwriting**. It does not override Gate E, position ceilings, trigger discipline, evidence governance or manual brokerage execution.
