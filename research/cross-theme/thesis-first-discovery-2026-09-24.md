# Thesis-first discovery scan — 24 September 2026

**Method:** world/architecture change → scarce complement → bottleneck migration → company discovery → evidence of inflection → economic capture.

## Executive finding

The first restored thesis-first scan surfaced **two genuinely new research directions** outside the canonical 59-company inventory and one broader architecture shift worth mapping.

The strongest immediate signal is **AI point-of-load power delivery**: rising accelerator power density is making power-conversion architecture itself scarce, while Vicor has simultaneously disclosed near-capacity fabrication, two additional fab sites, a new VPD licence and a Q3 guidance increase tied to royalties.

The second is **humanoid supplier industrialisation**, where supplier awards and production-readiness are beginning to replace prototype-only evidence. Samhyun and Namuga are notable outside-universe leads, but current evidence is not yet sufficient for a full underwrite.

Liquid cooling remains structurally important, but the next discovery layer may be **components inside the cooling loop**—quick disconnects, manifolds/cold plates and connectors—rather than another broad cooling OEM.

## 1 — AI power density → point-of-load power architecture

**FACT — secondary:** TrendForce's 24 Sep AI-infrastructure bulletin says AI-server demand is extending lead times and producing price increases/capacity expansion in power semiconductors and thermal components, with higher embedded power-semiconductor value in future high-voltage architectures.

**HYPOTHESIS:** As accelerator racks become denser, scarcity migrates from merely securing grid/rack power toward converting and delivering very high current efficiently at package/board level. IP, power density, packaging and qualified multi-source architecture can capture economics even if upstream electricity is available.

### New lead — Vicor (NASDAQ: VICR)

**Primary evidence**
- 11 Sep: Vicor said ChiP Fab-1 utilization is approaching capacity and acquired sites for Fab-2 and Fab-3; the combined future footprint is nearly 1m sq ft and Fab-2 initial deployment has about a one-year lead time.
- 17 Sep: Vicor disclosed another non-exclusive VPD licence to a leading AI OEM.
- 21 Sep: Vicor raised Q3 sequential revenue-growth guidance from nearly 10% to **more than 20%**, explicitly because of royalties from the recently announced VPD licence.
- Management said four leading OEM/hyperscaler companies have secured licences to Vicor's VPD technology.

**INTERPRETATION:** architectural change → IP scarcity → licence adoption → royalty economics → capacity constraint/expansion. This is stronger than generic AI thematic association.

**Counter-case:** the equity has already repriced sharply; non-exclusive licensing enables multi-source supply; low royalty rates and alternative architectures may let industry adoption grow without proportionate Vicor shareholder capture.

**State:** **PROMOTE TO BOUNDED COMPANY UNDERWRITE.** Quantify licence/royalty economics, module revenue, capacity economics, share count/dilution and current valuation before any Unicorn/Gate-E conclusion.

## 2 — liquid cooling → component-level bottlenecks

**FACT — secondary:** TrendForce highlights rapid liquid-cooling capacity expansion and specifically cold plates/manifolds and fast-disconnect components.

**FACT — primary:** LOTES (TWSE:3533) markets liquid-cooling quick-disconnect products for hyperscale/enterprise data centres, AI/HPC racks and cold-plate/manifold cooling; it is also an NVIDIA MGX ecosystem participant.

**FACT — primary:** LG Electronics disclosed on 22 Sep that its 2.6 MW CDU qualified as NVIDIA DSX Ready, following 600 kW and 1 MW CDU qualifications. This supports the architecture shift, though LG is too diversified for this alone to be company-transforming.

**HYPOTHESIS:** At higher rack density, reliability, flow, leak-free connection and serviceability can shift scarcity toward apparently mundane cooling-loop components.

### New lead — LOTES (TWSE:3533)

**INTERPRETATION:** LOTES combines socket/interconnect exposure with liquid-cooling quick disconnects. The key question is company purity: are AI cooling products material enough to transform economics?

**Counter-case:** primary evidence confirms capability/ecosystem participation but not material orders, constrained capacity, pricing power or financial mix.

**State:** **EVIDENCE BUILD.** Seek customer/platform qualification, cooling-product revenue/order contribution, capacity and margin evidence.

**Coverage gap:** Auras was highlighted by secondary industry evidence for cold plates/manifolds, but this pass did not establish sufficiently strong primary evidence to promote it. Continue searching Taiwan cooling-component disclosures.

## 3 — humanoid industrialisation → supplier awards

The relevant state change is increasing use of **award, mass-production, sole-source, production readiness and accelerated capacity** rather than prototype/demo language.

### New lead — Samhyun (KOSDAQ:437730)

**FACT — broker evidence:** a 6 Sep Korea Investment note reports discussions with >20 robot companies and four humanoid programmes (two global, two Korean) progressing beyond spec-in into production-prototype supply contracts; mass-production terms were under discussion.

**Secondary corroboration:** recent industry mapping reports Samhyun accelerating its second actuator plant and planning substantial actuator/motor/controller/reducer capacity.

**INTERPRETATION:** the signal is automotive-grade manufacturing capability potentially crossing into awarded humanoid programmes.

**Counter-case:** customers remain unnamed; award visibility is not signed high-volume economics; planned capacity may outrun humanoid demand.

**State:** **EVIDENCE BUILD.** Require company/filing-grade confirmation of awards, capacity, expected robot revenue and economics.

### New lead — Namuga (KOSDAQ:190510)

**FACT — company-announcement reporting:** on 7 Sep Namuga announced it would exclusively supply a 3D stereo-camera solution for a next-generation humanoid programme at an unnamed large North American robot company, after PoC/technology verification, with mass production described as confirmed.

**INTERPRETATION:** perception modules may be a less crowded Physical-AI scarce-complement branch than reducers. A sole-source mass-production transition is exactly the state-change signal this method seeks.

**Counter-case:** customer identity, programme volumes, revenue contribution, duration and margins are not established.

**State:** **PROMOTE TO BOUNDED EVIDENCE PASS**, not yet an equity underwrite.

### Other actuator signal

SPG (KOSDAQ:058610) has its precision-reducer technology in KIMM's KAIROS standard humanoid drive module. Technically relevant, but currently weaker economic evidence than Samhyun's programme pipeline or Namuga's stated mass-production supply.

This scan does not displace Laifual, JEM, Weebit, Centrus, QNX or other existing names.

- **Vicor** is the strongest new lead because the state change is visible in primary economic evidence: licensing, royalties, raised guidance and capacity expansion.

## Negative / coverage-gap record

- Broad data-centre power/cooling incumbents are not automatically discoveries; many are already known and/or too diversified for the new bottleneck to transform company economics.
- Humanoid component capacity expansion alone is not scarcity proof; qualification, utilization, repeat orders and unit economics remain necessary.
- Tesla-chain supplier reports are noisy and often broker/supply-chain attributed. Do not promote unnamed-customer claims without stronger corroboration.
- HBM remains important, but this scan did not find a sufficiently strong *new* architecture/state change beyond existing JEM/SUSS/MJC/Camtek work to start there.
