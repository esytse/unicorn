# Networking and inference data-path capture

**Lifecycle:** CANONICAL discovery evidence
**As of:** 2026-10-09
**Issue:** #367
**Decision:** no new Gate-E entry; six listed screens, zero immediate capital promotions.

## Architecture before companies

**FACT:** NVIDIA's 31 May Rubin production release identifies production CPO Ethernet switching and a BlueField-4 STX storage layer; its September financial release explicitly supports both pluggable and co-packaged optics. Sources: [production release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Vera-Rubin-Ramps-Into-Full-Production-to-Power-Agentic-AI-Factories-Worldwide/default.aspx), [Q2 release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/). Retrieved 9 Oct; technical adoption, not supplier margin proof.

**INTERPRETATION:** scale-out bandwidth/power and inference context retrieval create separate constraints. CPO moves value toward lasers, optical packaging, yield/test and maintainability; it does not eliminate pluggables everywhere or short-reach copper/AECs. Context memory can move to tiered DRAM/flash/networking, but model compression, cache reuse and system integration can reduce content. Never count every architecture layer as simultaneously scarce.

**Strongest counter-evidence:** multiple qualified suppliers, platform capacity rights, open standards and customer bargaining can turn bandwidth demand into volume without scarce merchant margins. NVIDIA's nonexclusive Coherent commitment is demand evidence, not proof of exclusivity. Financial strength differs: fab/light IP suppliers still pay SBC; optical capacity expansion consumes cash and can overbuild. Any follow-up needs original debt/preferred/convertible terms and diluted cash earnings.

**Negative/context search:** production ecosystem spans listed NetApp/IBM and private VAST/DDN/WEKA/MinIO; no context-only public profit pool was isolated within this six-company bound. These are benchmarks, not seventh-through-tenth screens or substitutes for private access. Optical packaging/test can be researched if named production shortages and company-material orders appear; generic testing exposure is not enough.

**Output/gates:** no immediate deep-underwrite handoff is earned at current recognition. Retain Coherent and Credo as the two highest-information rescan candidates, not Buy instructions: require original customer concentration/cash/dilution data, nonexclusive merchant margins through architecture migration, and a valuation-sensitive March-2028 bridge superior to existing sleeves/cash. Hold/Trim/Sell for any eventual holding would depend on forward cash capture and priced growth, not entry cost. Structural confidence High; supplier capture Medium; new portfolio attractiveness Low pending full valuation. This screen closes the bounded discovery question, not every future company review.
