# Scarce-Complement Research Map

**Status:** canonical cross-theme coverage map  
**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Parent framework:** `research/cross-theme/scarce-complements.md`  
**Purpose:** show where Unicorn already has evidence, where exposures overlap, and which missing bottlenecks deserve research.

> **Abundant Intelligence → Scarce Complements → Bottleneck Migration → Economic Capture → Capital Allocation**

This map is a research-prioritisation surface, not a stock ranking. A bottleneck can be structurally important without producing supplier rents, and a company can have bottleneck exposure without clearing Gate-E valuation, catalyst or downside requirements.


## Evidence-chain calibration — 24 September 2026

**Claim -> evidence -> inference -> allocation implication:** this surface must distinguish evidence that a bottleneck or scarce complement exists from evidence that a specific listed company can capture durable incremental economics from it. A technically important layer can remain unattractive or uninvestable because competition, diversification, capital intensity, private-company capture or starting valuation absorbs the economics.

**Evidence quality / freshness:** prefer primary operating/customer/technical evidence for company capture and use secondary market/industry material mainly to map the landscape. Observation freshness is independent of document edit date; live ranking/allocation conclusions require refreshed company and valuation evidence at their governed trigger.

**Strongest counter-case:** bottlenecks migrate. Supply response, architectural substitution, vertical integration, qualification of alternatives or private-company capture can move the profit pool away from the initially identified scarce complement. Treat these as active falsifiers, not footnotes.

**Calibration boundary:** cross-theme ranking is a research/allocation prioritization device, not proof of expected return. Do not promote a company solely because its bottleneck scores highly. Company capture, financial materiality, valuation and portfolio correlation must survive their own evidence chain.

## What the remap says

### 1. We are over-covered in memory relative to independent bottlenecks

**FACT:** the current governed universe contains JEM, SUSS MicroTec, Micronics Japan and Weebit Nano, all linked to memory/data-movement economics.

**INTERPRETATION:** these are not identical businesses, but their return drivers can correlate through AI-memory capex and the semiconductor cycle. Additional memory names face a higher bar unless they expose a genuinely different bottleneck or materially superior asymmetry.

### 2. Physical AI research is structurally broad but public capture is uneven

Actuation and safety/runtime have identifiable public or near-public candidates. The potentially stronger feedback-data/fleet-learning moat is currently represented mainly by private benchmarks or economically diluted public companies.

**OPEN QUESTION:** can a listed company retain cross-customer learning rights and recurring economics without owning the whole robot stack?

### 3. Energy, power delivery and cooling provide independent return engines

These lanes are less correlated with the existing memory cluster and already have governed candidates. The key research task is no longer proving that AI uses power; it is determining exactly where capacity, qualification or speed-to-deployment remains constrained and who captures the rent.

### 4. The largest actionable research gaps are downstream of intelligence

The current universe is weakest in:
- scientific-AI execution / automated laboratories;
- independent simulation, evaluation and verification;
- sensing / physical-world data acquisition.

These are more bounded than a generic search for 'AI software' and fit the scarce-complements thesis directly.

### 5. Importance is not capture

Every lane must pass two separate tests:

1. **Bottleneck test:** does this input constrain useful output, and is supply difficult to expand/substitute?
2. **Capture test:** can a specific supplier retain pricing, margins, recurring revenue or switching costs?

Only after both pass should Gate-E test valuation, catalyst timing, downside and portfolio contribution.

## Portfolio-correlation implication

The research queue should prefer **independent bottleneck exposures** when evidence quality is comparable. New work should not add a fifth correlated memory expression merely because its standalone company quality is attractive.

This does not mechanically demote existing memory candidates. It changes the marginal-research hurdle: a new candidate should improve the portfolio opportunity set, not simply duplicate an already well-covered scarcity.

## Research priorities generated by this map

### P1 — Scientific-AI execution / automated labs
Test whether increasing AI-generated scientific hypotheses creates scarce physical experimental throughput and whether listed instrument, automation, consumables or lab-workflow suppliers can capture it.

### P2 — Simulation / evaluation / verification
Map merchant economics across robotics, autonomous systems and agentic workflows. Focus on recurring release-gating and proprietary evaluation data rather than generic software tooling.

### P2 — Sensing / physical-world data
Test which modalities remain qualification- or physics-constrained enough to support durable supplier rents, with emphasis on exposure material enough to move a listed company.

## Migration indicators

Future research should explicitly watch for:
- lead times and backlog normalising;
- capacity announcements that outrun demand;
- standardisation reducing switching costs;
- architectural substitution;
- customer insourcing;
- gross-margin/pricing deterioration despite demand growth;
- a new complement becoming the limiting factor;
- evidence that spend rises but supplier returns do not.

A lane should be downgraded when scarcity is relieved even if end-market demand remains strong.

## Current conclusion

**INTERPRETATION — Medium confidence:** Unicorn has good coverage of several first-order physical/infrastructure constraints, but the opportunity set is unbalanced toward memory and has meaningful gaps in the systems that turn cheap intelligence into verified physical or scientific outcomes.

The next discovery work should therefore be driven by **missing scarce complements**, not by adding companies to already crowded themes.
