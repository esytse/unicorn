# Fusion High-Voltage / Power Electronics — First-Pass Note

Fusion systems require bespoke high-voltage/current conversion for heating and magnet/plasma subsystems. Fusion for Energy's ITER work with Ampegon provides direct evidence: power supplies convert grid electricity into regulated high voltage/current for gyrotrons.

Technical necessity is therefore established, but the first pass did not identify a clean listed supplier whose fusion exposure is both differentiated and financially material. Generic power-electronics exposure should not be promoted without direct qualification/order evidence.
