# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Industry Evidence

The FIA 2026 supply-chain survey is used as evidence that private-fusion procurement and supplier investment are growing, not as a precise total-addressable-market forecast.

Reported figures cover participating companies/suppliers rather than the entire industry. The most useful signal for Unicorn is qualitative as well as quantitative: suppliers identify extreme-condition materials, heat management and fuel systems as persistent future challenges, while many are already investing in capacity.

This supports bottleneck research but also creates a falsification risk: capacity expansion may reduce scarcity before commercial volumes arrive.
