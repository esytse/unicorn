# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Bottleneck Migration

If fusion scales, bottlenecks are unlikely to remain static. Early scarcity may sit in HTS/magnets, specialist power, vacuum and prototype engineering; later constraints may migrate toward breeder/fuel-cycle systems, plasma-facing materials, heat extraction, maintenance throughput and conventional plant manufacturing.

Research should therefore avoid treating the 2026 bottleneck map as permanent. Company conviction must be tied to the current stage of industrialisation and updated when qualification/capacity shifts.
