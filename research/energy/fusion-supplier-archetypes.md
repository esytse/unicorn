# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Supplier Archetypes

1. **Pure enabling specialist:** potentially high upside, but often private and exposed to fusion timing.
2. **Diversified qualified supplier:** lower fusion sensitivity but stronger downside resilience; Furukawa/VAT pattern.
3. **Project engineer/integrator:** can win large contracts but repeatability/margins may be weak.
4. **Conventional balance-of-plant supplier:** benefits only when plants scale; usually already captured by energy thesis.
5. **Materials/process bottleneck:** potentially strongest moat if qualification and capacity are concentrated; evidence still incomplete.

Unicorn should generally prefer #2 or a demonstrably scarce #5 unless #1 becomes publicly investable with robust non-fusion/downside support.
