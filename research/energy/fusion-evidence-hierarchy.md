# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Evidence Hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. funded purchase orders/contracts and regulatory/company financial disclosure;
2. programme-primary qualification/procurement records (ITER, UKFE/STEP, F4E, etc.);
3. demonstrated technical operation with quantified conditions;
4. repeated commercial partnerships with defined deliverables;
5. industry surveys with disclosed methodology;
6. company strategic announcements/MOUs/investments;
7. developer target dates, market forecasts and projected LCOE.

Lower-level evidence can establish a question but should not by itself establish financial capture or portfolio promotion.
