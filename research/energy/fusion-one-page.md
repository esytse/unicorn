# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — One-Page Brief

**Question:** can we own fusion upside without betting on the winning reactor developer?

**Answer so far:** potentially, but the public-equity opportunity is earlier and narrower than the fusion narrative suggests.

**What is real:** fusion supply-chain spending is growing; major programmes are procuring technically difficult magnet, vacuum, power, robotics and diagnostic systems; HTS/integrated magnet systems have the strongest direct evidence.

**What is not proven:** that fusion revenue is currently large enough to drive most listed suppliers' earnings.

**Best new research lead:** Furukawa Electric / SuperPower — direct HTS supply and strategic fusion relationships plus diversified non-fusion businesses.

**Comparator:** VAT Group — direct ITER vacuum engineering with semiconductor-vacuum downside support, but no demonstrated fusion materiality.

**Action:** Gate-E underwrite Furukawa. Keep the rest event-driven. No portfolio/top-10 change from fusion alone.
