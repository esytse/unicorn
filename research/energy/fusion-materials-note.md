# Fusion Extreme Materials / Thermal / Fuel — First-Pass Note

The 2026 FIA supplier survey identifies materials for extreme conditions, heat management and fuel systems among the dominant long-term supply-chain concerns. UK STEP also identifies breeder blanket, fuel-cycle and fusion-hardened materials as explicit industrial needs.

This may ultimately contain the strongest scarcity moats, but the first pass did not establish a clean listed supplier with both direct fusion qualification and financially meaningful capture. Generic advanced-material capability is not enough.

Keep this layer parked until direct qualification, funded procurement or concentrated supplier evidence appears.
