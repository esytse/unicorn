# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Falsification Checklist

Before strengthening a fusion supplier thesis, ask:

- Is the claimed customer relationship actually funded procurement?
- Is the component required by the relevant architecture?
- Can another supplier qualify without prohibitive time/cost?
- Is capacity scarcity structural or just temporary?
- Does the supplier retain pricing/margin after capacity expansion?
- Is fusion revenue large enough to matter to the listed parent?
- Does the investment still work if commercial fusion is 2045–2055 rather than the developer target?
- Is the catalyst inside the portfolio window?
- Is the valuation already pricing the optionality?

A “no” to financial materiality or portfolio-window relevance normally prevents promotion regardless of technical importance.
