# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Monitoring Triggers

These are evidence triggers, not automatic buy/sell rules.

## Furukawa / SuperPower
- funded STEP/Tokamak/private-fusion HTS order with disclosed scale;
- new HTS manufacturing capacity with disclosed capex/output/timing;
- disclosure showing HTS becoming financially material to Furukawa;
- major qualified competitor capacity entry or price compression that weakens scarcity.

## VAT Group
- material private-fusion valve/vacuum order;
- repeated adoption across multiple fusion developers;
- explicit fusion revenue/order disclosure large enough to matter.

## Sector
- funded breeder blanket / tritium fuel-cycle procurement identifying concentrated suppliers;
- repeated qualification of a listed supplier in neutron-resistant materials or remote maintenance;
- supply-chain spending/capacity data showing a step-change from prototype procurement toward repeat production.

Routine fusion fundraising or developer target-date announcements alone do not fire a research trigger.
