# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Stage Assessment

**2026 stage:** transition from laboratory/component work toward larger integrated prototypes and pilot-system procurement, with substantial uncertainty before repeat commercial plant production.

This stage favours suppliers that can earn from prototype/pilot procurement **and** deploy the same capability elsewhere. It is less favourable to conventional balance-of-plant suppliers whose fusion revenue requires many plants, or to pure thematic exposure dependent on commercial electricity arriving on a specific developer schedule.
