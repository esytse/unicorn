# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Research Questions

## Supplier economics
- Who owns qualified capacity rather than merely relevant technology?
- What is the switching/qualification burden?
- Where is pricing power visible in orders, margins or backlog?

## Demand conversion
- Which programme requirements are funded and ordered?
- Which are forecasts, roadmaps or developer aspirations?
- When does prototype demand become repeat manufacturing?

## Portfolio relevance
- Can the opportunity change total-company earnings within 18 months?
- Does the company still work if fusion slips 10–20 years?
- Does it add independent asymmetry versus existing energy/Physical-AI exposure?

These questions govern all follow-up from #155.
