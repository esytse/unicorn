# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Technical vs Financial Relevance

A recurring failure mode is to infer investment relevance from technical necessity.

- **Technical relevance:** the component is required and difficult.
- **Commercial relevance:** the supplier is qualified and wins orders.
- **Financial relevance:** those orders materially change revenue, margins or cash flow.
- **Portfolio relevance:** the financial change plus valuation/catalyst/downside improves the portfolio objective.

The first-pass work establishes technical relevance for several layers and commercial relevance for selected suppliers. Financial and portfolio relevance remain the gating work.
