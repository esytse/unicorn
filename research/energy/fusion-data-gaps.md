# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Data Gaps

## Highest-value missing data

- Public-company fusion revenue/order disclosure.
- REBCO/HTS qualified capacity by supplier and expansion timeline.
- HTS tape pricing, gross-margin structure and customer concentration.
- Funded STEP procurement quantities/timing below the magnet-system prime level.
- Private-fusion supplier awards in vacuum, extreme materials, breeder/fuel-cycle and remote maintenance.
- Evidence of repeat product orders versus one-off engineering services.

These gaps explain why confidence in the physical bottleneck map is higher than confidence in public-company financial capture.
