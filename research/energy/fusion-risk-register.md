# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Risk Register

| Risk | Why it matters | Research response |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial fusion slips decades | destroys developer-timeline theses | require strong non-fusion economics |
| Supply capacity expands faster than demand | erodes scarcity/pricing | track capex, output and competitor qualification |
| Developers vertically integrate | removes supplier capture | prefer externally validated multi-customer suppliers |
| Prototype contracts do not repeat | one-off engineering ≠ product economics | require repeat orders / platform reuse |
| Fusion exposure is immaterial to listed parent | technically right, financially irrelevant | quantify total-company sensitivity |
| Architecture changes | component relevance can disappear | test cross-architecture applicability |
| Hype inflates valuation before earnings | poor asymmetry | apply Gate-E valuation/downside discipline |
| Government programme dependence | procurement/policy timing risk | separate public programme support from commercial demand |
