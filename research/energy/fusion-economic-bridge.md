# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Economic Bridge

The research bridge from technology to stock must be explicit:

**technical necessity → qualification/scarcity → funded orders → supplier share → revenue → incremental margin/FCF → total-company earnings sensitivity → valuation/catalyst → portfolio contribution.**

The first-pass fusion work establishes the left side of this bridge for several technologies. Furukawa research must now test the right side. No stock conclusion should skip those intermediate steps.
