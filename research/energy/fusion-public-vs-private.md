# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Public vs Private Signal

Private fusion companies are useful evidence for **where bottlenecks sit**, but are not automatically investable candidates for this repository's public-equity workflow.

CFS and Tokamak Energy currently provide strong evidence around integrated HTS magnet systems and supplier-market formation. Their technical/commercial activity should be used to identify public upstream suppliers and cross-market capabilities, not treated as public portfolio recommendations.

The public-company screen must independently prove company-level capture, valuation and catalyst relevance.
