# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — What Changed

Before this work, fusion was only a possible new energy bottleneck branch.

After the first pass:
- the branch is validated as real industrial research;
- HTS/integrated magnets are the strongest evidenced entry point;
- Furukawa/SuperPower is isolated for company-level research;
- VAT is retained as comparator;
- broad fusion does not change current portfolio/rankings;
- future work is bounded by explicit evidence triggers.
