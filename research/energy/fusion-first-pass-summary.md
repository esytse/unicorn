# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — First-Pass Decision Summary

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Issue:** #155

## Decision

Fusion enabling infrastructure is a valid structural-bottleneck research branch, but current evidence does not justify changing the active portfolio or top-10 ranking.

The first pass found direct evidence of industrial demand and qualification in HTS/superconducting systems, vacuum, remote handling, diagnostics and high-voltage power. The most attractive research structure is a supplier whose fusion capability is scarce but whose core business succeeds without commercial fusion.

## Follow-up

**Furukawa Electric / SuperPower** is the only new listed company promoted from broad discovery into a bounded Gate-E underwrite. Direct HTS supply and strategic relationships are evidenced; financial materiality, scarcity economics, capacity, competition, valuation and 18-month catalysts remain unresolved.

**VAT Group** is retained as a comparator/monitor. Its ITER vacuum-valve engineering is directly evidenced, but fusion materiality is not.

No other company is promoted from this pass.
