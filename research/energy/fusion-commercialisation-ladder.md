# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Commercialisation Ladder

Use this ladder when assessing supplier demand:

1. laboratory / component demonstration;
2. integrated prototype procurement;
3. funded pilot-plant system procurement;
4. first-of-a-kind net-electric plant;
5. repeat plant orders / standardized supply chain;
6. scaled commercial deployment.

Supplier revenue can begin at stages 1–3. Portfolio materiality usually requires evidence that spend is large/repeatable enough to matter to the listed parent; it does not require waiting for stage 6, but it also cannot be inferred from a stage-1 physics milestone.
