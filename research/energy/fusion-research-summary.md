# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Research Summary

Fusion now merits a bounded supplier-research lane because industrial procurement and difficult enabling systems are evidenced. The strongest first-pass bottleneck is HTS/integrated magnet systems; vacuum, remote handling, diagnostics, power and extreme materials are also technically real.

Public-equity capture remains much less developed than the technology story. Furukawa Electric / SuperPower is the first new company worth a bounded current underwrite; VAT Group is a useful cross-market comparator. No portfolio or top-10 change is made from the broad scan.
