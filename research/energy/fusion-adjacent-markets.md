# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Adjacent Markets

Preferred supplier resilience comes from capabilities already monetised in structural markets such as:

- grid/power transmission and electrification;
- semiconductor vacuum/process equipment;
- medical/scientific superconducting systems;
- fission/SMR and nuclear maintenance;
- aerospace/extreme-environment systems;
- industrial automation/remote handling.

Adjacent markets are downside support only when the same capability/capacity is genuinely transferable; superficial end-market overlap should not be counted.
