# AI power-generation hot-section component discovery — 25 September 2026

**Backlog:** #282, parent #261  
**Method:** AI power demand → turbine capacity shortage → hot-section manufacturing scarcity → qualified supplier → company materiality.

## World change

Gas-turbine demand has outrun manufacturing capacity. Rystad's Sep 2026 work says gas-turbine/reciprocating-engine orders hit ~100 GW last year, ~30% above OEM delivery capacity, with backlogs extending beyond the decade.

The bottleneck is migrating below turbine OEMs into:
**single-crystal / directionally-solidified blades and vanes → nickel superalloys → coatings/HIP → valves/controls → qualified repair/replacement capacity.**

The strongest validation is strategic behavior:
- GE Aerospace agreed to buy Consolidated Precision Products for ~$11.75bn, explicitly addressing mission-critical casting capacity.
- SpaceX is attempting to internalize turbine blade/vane production because of long turbine lead times.
- Howmet says demand/capital expansion is testing its ability to add equipment.
