# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Portfolio Fit

Even a strong supplier underwrite must be compared with the active portfolio on:

- remaining-window upside/downside;
- catalyst timing and probability;
- evidence confidence;
- permanent-loss risk;
- responsible position-size ceiling;
- correlation with existing energy/nuclear/Physical-AI exposures;
- best alternative use of capital.

A fusion supplier that merely duplicates existing energy-factor exposure should require clearly superior asymmetry to earn a place.
