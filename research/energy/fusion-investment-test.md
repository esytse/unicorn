# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Investment Test

For any listed fusion supplier, the underwrite must answer:

**Without fusion:** what earnings/cash flow/valuation support exists?

**With plausible funded fusion demand:** how much incremental revenue, margin and FCF can appear inside 18 months?

**With fusion delayed:** does the thesis survive?

**With capacity expansion:** does scarcity/pricing survive?

**Relative to current portfolio:** is the remaining-window asymmetry better enough to justify research attention and capital?

Only after these questions are answered should fusion exposure influence the active ranking.
