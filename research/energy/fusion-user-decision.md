# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Decision Usefulness

A future fusion alert/research output should answer:

- what changed in supplier evidence;
- whether it changes scarcity/capture/materiality;
- whether current valuation/catalyst/downside changes;
- whether portfolio ranking/allocation needs reassessment;
- what evidence would falsify the new view.

If it cannot answer those questions, it is informational rather than decision-useful and should normally stay quiet.
