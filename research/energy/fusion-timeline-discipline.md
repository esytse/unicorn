# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Timeline Discipline

Fusion research must distinguish:

- **scientific gain / plasma milestones**;
- **engineering gain and integrated plant operation**;
- **net electricity to the grid**;
- **reliable repeat operation**;
- **economic commercial deployment**.

A milestone in one category must not be silently translated into the next. Supplier revenue can occur well before commercial electricity, which is why enabling-infrastructure research can be valid even while plant timelines remain uncertain.
