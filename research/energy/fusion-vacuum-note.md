# Fusion Vacuum — First-Pass Note

Vacuum is a validated technical requirement rather than a speculative adjacency. VAT's ITER work demonstrates bespoke all-metal valve engineering for extreme temperature/radiation and very large isolation geometry.

However, VAT also illustrates the materiality problem: a world-class fusion-relevant product can be financially small beside an established semiconductor-vacuum franchise. The research signal is therefore **qualification quality and cross-market resilience**, not an automatic fusion earnings thesis.

Re-open VAT as active research only if private-fusion adoption/order disclosure becomes large enough to change the company underwrite.
