# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Catalyst Framework

A fusion supplier catalyst is portfolio-relevant only if it changes at least one of:

- funded order/backlog;
- qualified capacity/share;
- pricing/margin expectations;
- earnings/FCF estimates;
- probability/timing of repeat production;
- downside or thesis-break risk;
- valuation asymmetry.

Scientific headlines or developer fundraising that do not change a supplier underwrite are context, not catalysts.
