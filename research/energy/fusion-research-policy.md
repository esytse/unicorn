# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Research Policy

Fusion is allowed to create backlog only when a question can change:

- portfolio allocation/ranking;
- evidence confidence in a bottleneck;
- catalyst timing/probability;
- a thesis breaker;
- or the identification of a materially superior candidate.

Interesting science alone is not sufficient backlog justification. This keeps the lane compatible with `AGENTS.md` and `AUTOMATION.md`.
