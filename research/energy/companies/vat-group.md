## FACT

VAT Group states that it acted as an ITER development partner and created an ITER Valve Catalog for vacuum solutions capable of operating under extreme temperature and radiation conditions. The work included an all-metal sealed pendulum valve with a 1.6 m opening used to isolate tokamak and neutral-beam vacuum volumes.

VAT's established core market is high-vacuum technology for semiconductor, display and digital-technology manufacturing.
