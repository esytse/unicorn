# nVent — AI Energy Investment Capture

**Status:** Watch  
**Investment Capture Score:** **4.2 / 5**  
**Confidence:** Medium  
**Primary lanes:** electrical connection / protection; liquid cooling

## Current conclusion

**INTERPRETATION:** nVent is a smaller architecture-adjacent challenger with unusually strong current growth and direct exposure to two rising content pools: electrical connection/protection and liquid cooling.

## Evidence

**FACT:** Q2 2026 reported sales were **$1.5bn, +53%**, with **47% organic growth**.

**FACT:** adjusted EPS increased 69%; free cash flow rose to $167m from $74m.

**FACT:** management explicitly cited significant data-centre growth and announced another liquid-cooling manufacturing expansion.

## Why capture can persist

- rack/site density raises electrical connection, protection and thermal content;
- portfolio exposure is more concentrated than Eaton / Schneider;
- rapid new-product contribution suggests content expansion, not just underlying market growth;
- electrical + cooling mix provides some architecture resilience.

## Counter-evidence / risks

- OCP standardisation and multi-vendor cooling supply reduce proprietary lock-in;
- current growth includes portfolio transformation / new products and must be normalized;
- capacity expansion can reduce scarcity and pressure returns;
- precise data-centre revenue / customer concentration needs better disclosure.

## Thesis breakers

- organic growth normalizes without durable margin / FCF improvement;
- cooling capacity additions drive price competition;
- new products fail to sustain content share as standards mature;
- electrical connection/protection does not offset cooling commoditization.

## Gate-C question

Is the current earnings step-up durable enough to justify a specialist premium, or does normalization make nVent less attractive than the broader Vertiv / Eaton platforms?
