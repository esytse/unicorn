## Why this company surfaced

Furukawa Electric is a listed diversified industrial company with direct evidence that its SuperPower business supplies high-temperature superconducting (HTS) wire into fusion development. The company also serves infrastructure, automotive and electronics markets, making it a candidate for the Unicorn test: does a scarce fusion-enabling capability have attractive economics even if fusion commercialization is delayed?

## FACT

- In January 2023 Tokamak Energy announced that Furukawa Electric and its US subsidiary SuperPower were expected to supply more than several hundred kilometres of specialist HTS wire for ST80-HTS.
- Furukawa entered an investment agreement with Tokamak Energy in December 2023. In 2026 the companies expanded collaboration to explore UK HTS-tape R&D, prototyping, manufacturing processes and future production capability.
- Furukawa states that HTS applications extend beyond fusion into power transmission and medical devices.
- Furukawa invested in Japanese fusion company Starlight Engine in July 2026, adding a second direct fusion relationship.

## INTERPRETATION

The evidence establishes real fusion supply-chain exposure, but not financial materiality, pricing power or a Gate-E portfolio case. The key next question is whether Furukawa/SuperPower owns enough qualified capacity/IP/customer position for HTS growth to materially change Furukawa's earnings and valuation inside the remaining portfolio window.
