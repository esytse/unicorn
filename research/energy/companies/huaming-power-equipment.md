# Huaming Power Equipment — OLTC Investment Capture & Initial Gate C

**Status:** Investigating — near-Gate-C valuation, evidence-limited  
**Investment Capture Score:** **4.1 / 5**  
**Confidence:** Medium  
**Ticker:** SZSE 002270  
**Primary lane:** on-load tap changers (OLTCs) / transformer voltage regulation  
**Backlog:** #75

## Executive conclusion

**INTERPRETATION:** Huaming is the strongest new company surfaced by the energy second-order research. It has an unusually attractive combination of:

- high exposure to a qualified transformer subcomponent rather than generic electrical equipment;
- power-equipment gross margin above 60%;
- strong operating cash conversion and low balance-sheet leverage;
- overseas revenue growth above 40% in the core business;
- domestic high-voltage / UHV qualification moving upward;
- lifecycle inspection / maintenance / spares optionality;
- a roughly CNY17bn market capitalization rather than a $100bn-class rerated AI-infrastructure platform;
- a current valuation close to the research framework's 12% base-return hurdle.

But the evidence is **not yet sufficient for a formal `Watch` promotion or Gate-C pass**. The main gap is not demand or margins. It is whether Huaming's qualification and service moat can scale internationally against Maschinenfabrik Reinhausen (MR), especially in the highest-voltage / reliability-sensitive markets.

The initial normalized valuation screen at the **8 September 2026 CNY19.73 reference price** gives:

- bear: ~CNY18.1 / **-2.0% annualized**;
- base: ~CNY31.0 / **11.0% annualized**;
- bull: ~CNY46.3 / **21.9% annualized**;
- 25/50/25 weighted scenario CAGR: roughly **10.5%**;
- current base-case **12% return zone ~CNY19.0**.

This is unusually close to Gate C. The model deliberately excludes dividends from headline returns, although the current dividend yield is roughly 3%, because payout policy and future reinvestment are not assumed to remain constant.

**Current decision:** keep `Investigating`, but make Huaming the **highest-priority new energy company evidence gap**. Named repeat international qualification and durable service economics could justify a `Watch` promotion; deterioration in international margins / cash conversion or evidence that MR's capacity expansion commoditizes OLTC supply would weaken the case.

## 1. What the company sells

**FACT:** Huaming's core power-equipment business develops, manufactures and sells:

- on-load tap changers (OLTCs);
- de-energized / off-circuit tap changers;
- motor-drive mechanisms / controllers;
- filtration and related transformer accessories;
- online monitoring;
- inspection, maintenance, spares and lifecycle service.

**FACT:** Huaming describes the tap changer as a **key core transformer component** and the only component that operates / switches while the transformer is carrying load.

**FACT:** Direct customers are transformer manufacturers; ultimate users are utilities, grids, generators and industrial power users.

**FACT:** Products are highly customized by transformer manufacturer, voltage class and application. Huaming describes a small-batch / high-frequency production model with many variants and complex control requirements.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is materially different from generic transformer input exposure. The component is designed into a transformer, is reliability-critical and has enough moving / switching complexity to create qualification, field-history and service barriers.

## 2. Revenue sensitivity is unusually high

### H1 2026

**FACT:** Group revenue was **RMB1.281bn, +14.25% y/y**.

**FACT:** Core power-equipment revenue was **RMB1.099bn, +14.64%**, representing about **85.8% of group revenue**.

**FACT:** Core power-equipment gross margin was **60.83%**, +0.35 percentage points y/y.

**FACT:** Direct + indirect overseas core-business revenue reached **RMB449m, +44.59%**, exceeding 40% of power-equipment revenue. Huaming says overseas sales, order value and export activity reached record levels.

**FACT:** The H1 filing also says direct overseas / geographic international revenue was RMB274.6m with **58.50% gross margin**; the larger RMB449m figure includes indirect exports through Chinese transformer OEMs.

**INTERPRETATION:** The export distinction matters. The economic exposure to overseas transformer builds is already larger than direct-export accounting suggests, but not all ultimate customers / geographies are disclosed.

---

## 3. Economic capture is already visible

### Margin

A >60% core-business gross margin is exceptional for electrical hardware and materially above the transformer OEM / grid-equipment economics that originally surfaced the theme.

**INTERPRETATION:** This strongly suggests Huaming is capturing value through product qualification, customization, intellectual property / know-how and market structure — not merely passing through copper / steel inflation.

### Cash conversion

**FACT:** H1 2026 operating cash flow was **RMB458.9m, +44.41% y/y**.

**FACT:** Cash paid for fixed / intangible assets was about RMB43m, producing a simple H1 OCF-minus-capex proxy of roughly **RMB416m**.

**FACT:** TTM operating cash flow was about RMB745m and TTM free cash flow about RMB646m on third-party normalized financial data.

**FACT:** TTM FCF margin was around **25%**.

### Balance sheet

**FACT:** H1 asset/liability ratio fell from **39.09% to 32.71%**.

**FACT:** Current third-party ratios show net cash / negative net debt and ROIC around **22%**.

**INTERPRETATION:** Capital intensity is not currently absorbing the scarcity economics. This is a major advantage versus transformer factories, turbine capacity, Modine's cooling ramp and Centrus' enrichment buildout.

## 4. Qualification evidence

### Domestic / high-voltage evidence

**FACT:** Huaming says it is the leading company in China's tap-changer niche and that annual shipment volume ranks among the highest globally.

**FACT:** The company says it has strong leadership below **500 kV** in China, while imported brands retain stronger positions in 500 kV / UHV applications.

**FACT:** In 2025, the jointly developed **CHVT converter-transformer OLTC** entered batch operation on the ±800 kV Longdong–Shandong UHVDC project.

**FACT:** Huaming says it was the first domestic company to master UHV tap-changer manufacturing technology and is continuing to increase share in UHV / converter-transformer applications previously dominated by foreign suppliers.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is meaningful process-of-record evidence. The product has moved beyond prototype into batch grid operation at a highly demanding voltage class.

### Overseas evidence

**FACT:** Overseas / indirect-export revenue now exceeds 40% of the core business and is growing >40%.

**FACT:** Huaming has an Indonesian production base and Singapore regional service center, is building local supply / service in Turkey, and is expanding Europe while exploring North America and the Middle East.

**OPEN QUESTION:** Primary public disclosure does **not** yet provide enough named repeat overseas transformer-OEM / utility qualifications, especially at the highest voltage classes, to establish that Huaming has replicated its domestic moat globally.

This is the main evidence gap.

---

## 5. Competitive benchmark — Maschinenfabrik Reinhausen

MR is the right benchmark because it demonstrates what a mature global OLTC moat looks like.

**FACT:** MR describes itself as the world market leader in transformer tap-change operations and offers a broad vacuum / oil OLTC range across transformer applications.

**FACT:** MR currently performs **more than 6,000 OLTC maintenance operations per year** and has a dense global branch / service network with 24/7 support and long-term spare-parts capability.

**FACT:** MR's broader company footprint includes **57 locations in 28 countries** and 1,500 current patents.

**FACT:** In April 2026 MR said investment in its global capacity programme allowed it to stabilize committed OLTC delivery times at roughly **16 weeks**.

### Why this matters

**FALSIFICATION:** The 16-week delivery-time improvement means OLTCs should **not** be valued as a permanent unit-supply shortage. MR is adding capacity and can respond to demand.

**INTERPRETATION:** The durable thesis therefore shifts from 'not enough tap changers' to:

> **qualification + installed base + customization + service + cost / localization advantage.**

Huaming can still capture value if it gains qualified share from MR / imported products, but the moat cannot be underwritten purely from current transformer shortages.

---

## 6. Service economics — attractive but not mature overseas

**FACT:** Huaming provides original parts / original maintenance and is shifting from reactive service to active lifecycle marketing, with local and remote technical support.

**FACT:** OLTCs require different maintenance / inspection interventions based on switching count and years in service, with wear parts replaced where required.

**FACT:** Huaming is deploying online-monitoring systems and wants to transition from equipment manufacturer toward lifecycle service provider.

**COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** In a July 2026 investor-platform response, Huaming said the **overseas installed base remains relatively low and overseas maintenance revenue is still limited**. It has begun building a global lifecycle service network but expects further investment in local teams, spares and response capacity as overseas installed base expands.

**INTERPRETATION:** Service is credible upside, not current base-case earnings. MR's 6,000+ annual maintenance events show the long-run economic opportunity, but Huaming has not yet earned that installed-base annuity internationally.

## 8. Initial Gate-C valuation

### Reference point

**Reference date:** 8 September 2026  
**Share price:** **CNY19.73**  
**Market cap:** ~**CNY17.25bn**  
**Enterprise value:** ~**CNY17.0bn**  
**Trailing P/E:** ~24x  
**Forward P/E:** ~19.5x  
**P/FCF:** ~26.7x  
**Current dividend yield:** ~3.1%

Third-party consensus for FY2026 is roughly **CNY0.92–0.95 EPS**, with 2027 consensus around CNY1.11–1.14. Consensus 3-year EPS growth is materially above the base normalization used below.

### Modeling method

To stay consistent with the energy Gate-C work:

- use **CNY0.95 FY2026 EPS** as the forward baseline;
- grow normalized EPS from end-2026 through end-2030;
- apply a terminal multiple below or around Huaming's historical forward valuation range rather than preserve a peak rerating;
- calculate price-only annualized return from 8 Sep 2026 to end-2030 (~4.3 years);
- exclude dividends from headline CAGR;
- preserve a separate bear case for overseas / UHV share disappointment.

### Scenarios

| Scenario | 2027–2030 EPS CAGR | 2030 terminal P/E | 2030 EPS | 2030 price | Annualized return |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Bear** | **8%** | **14x** | ~CNY1.29 | ~**CNY18.1** | **-2.0%** |
| **Base** | **16%** | **18x** | ~CNY1.72 | ~**CNY31.0** | **11.0%** |
| **Bull** | **22%** | **22x** | ~CNY2.10 | ~**CNY46.3** | **21.9%** |

Simple 25/50/25 weighting of scenario CAGRs: **~10.5% annualized**.

These are research scenarios, not price targets.

### Why the base is not heroic

- FY2026 consensus EPS growth is roughly 20%+ depending on source;
- 2027 consensus EPS growth is around 20%;
- overseas core revenue is currently growing >40%;
- base assumes only **16% EPS CAGR after 2026**, allowing substantial normalization;
- the 18x terminal multiple is below Huaming's current ~19.5x forward P/E and below several recent historical forward multiples.

### Why the bear matters

The bear assumes:

- MR and other incumbents restore capacity / delivery performance;
- high-voltage overseas qualification progresses slowly;
- domestic UHV substitution does not scale as quickly as hoped;
- overseas margins normalize;
- terminal valuation compresses to 14x.

The resulting price is below today's price but does not imply catastrophic downside under the current earnings base.

Assuming an 18x end-2030 terminal P/E:

| Return hurdle | Required 2027–2030 EPS CAGR |
|---|---:|
| **10%** | ~**14.9%** |
| **12%** | ~**17.1%** |

**INTERPRETATION:** A 12% price-only return does not require an absurd earnings assumption: it requires roughly 17% EPS CAGR from the FY2026 baseline, close to but below current consensus expectations for the next several years.

The issue is **confidence in global durability**, not mathematical valuation stretch.

## 11. Thesis breakers

- high-voltage / overseas qualification remains limited to lower-value classes;
- direct and indirect export growth fails to translate into repeat named global customer wins;
- MR / other incumbents use restored capacity and service networks to prevent international share gains;
- overseas gross margin falls materially as Huaming invests in localization and service;
- receivables / working capital deteriorate despite revenue growth;
- UHV domestic substitution stalls or reliability problems emerge;
- service / online-monitoring remains immaterial despite installed-base growth;
- governance / investor-access / geopolitical constraints make the theoretical return unavailable or too risky for the intended investor.

---

## 12. Catalysts / evidence that would change status

### Promote toward `Watch`

Any combination of:

- named repeat transformer-OEM / utility qualifications in Europe, North America or Middle East;
- evidence of Huaming products in high-voltage / extra-high-voltage overseas service;
- sustained >30% overseas growth with ~55%+ core gross margins;
- material overseas service / spares growth and a larger installed-base annuity;
- independent evidence of durable share gains versus MR / other imported suppliers;
- share price entering / staying below the ~CNY19 12% zone while operating evidence remains intact.

### Deprioritize

- overseas orders slow sharply despite transformer demand;
- high-voltage qualification remains China-only;
- gross margin breaks below the historical high-value component economics;
- FCF conversion deteriorates structurally;
- competitive capacity reduces the economics before international scale is reached.

## Current decision

**Status remains `Investigating`.**  
**ICS: 4.1 / 5.**  
**Confidence: Medium.**

Huaming becomes the **primary new energy evidence monitor from the second-order programme**. It is not a high-conviction research candidate and does not yet pass strict Gate C because qualification confidence and investability are weaker than the arithmetic alone suggests.

The next evidence question is narrow:

> **Can we verify repeat, named international high-voltage qualification and enough service / installed-base economics to justify treating the current ~17% required EPS CAGR for a 12% return as durable rather than cyclical?**

Until that evidence appears, preserve the CNY19-ish valuation zone as a monitoring reference rather than an automatic buy threshold.
