# Mitsubishi Heavy Industries — AI Energy Investment Capture

**Status:** Watch / benchmark  
**Investment Capture Score:** **4.1 / 5**  
**Confidence:** Medium  
**Primary lane:** advanced gas turbines / power systems

## Current conclusion

**INTERPRETATION:** MHI is the critical third advanced-class turbine benchmark. Its presence confirms that the market is concentrated but not a simple GE Vernova / Siemens Energy duopoly. It also offers direct exposure to the turbine scarcity cycle, but broad defense / aerospace / industrial operations dilute AI-power sensitivity.

## Evidence

**FACT:** Q1 FY2026 group order intake was **¥2.022tn**, revenue **¥1.194tn**, and business profit **¥159.6bn**; MHI raised full-year guidance.

**FACT:** company guidance and contemporaneous industry reporting describe gas-turbine demand running far above historical levels, with MHI working to shorten lead times and expand output.

## Why capture can persist

- one of very few qualified advanced-class gas-turbine OEMs;
- long installed-base service economics;
- high engineering / metallurgy / qualification barriers;
- strong non-AI demand support from global power and industrial investment.

## Counter-evidence / risks

- conglomerate breadth substantially dilutes data-centre earnings sensitivity;
- defense/aerospace cycles can dominate valuation and capital allocation;
- capacity expansion can reduce turbine scarcity;
- company disclosures do not isolate AI-specific turbine economics cleanly.

## Thesis breakers

- turbine orders normalize before capacity expansion earns adequate returns;
- service attachment fails to grow with installed base;
- broader-group capital demands overwhelm Power Systems cash generation;
- turbine margin quality underperforms GE Vernova / Siemens Energy through the cycle.

## Gate-C question

Does MHI offer better normalized return than the cleaner Western turbine peers once conglomerate dilution and its different valuation framework are included?
