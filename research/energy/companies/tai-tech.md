## Why this company appeared

This company was not found by screening the existing universe. The path was:

**AI accelerator power density → point-of-load/VPD bottleneck → high-current magnetics/TLVR → TAI-TECH**

That matters because it preserves the original Unicorn thesis: follow a migrating physical constraint until it reaches a company small enough for the new demand engine to transform total economics.

## Evidence chain

### Product exposure

**FACT — company materials:** power inductors were **62.1% of 2025 revenue**. TAI-TECH's product set includes high-current power inductors and TLVR components, with competitors including Taiyo Yuden, Eaton, TDK, Murata and others.

**FACT — company product evidence:** TAI-TECH explicitly markets TLVR coupled inductors for AI GPU/ASIC rails and describes applications above 400A; its AI-server materials reference accelerator currents above 1,000A.

**FACT — reported management mix (secondary call memo):** AI was ~13% of 1H26 revenue and ~12% in Q2, up from ~10% in 2025 and ~7% in 2024. Management reportedly said AI and automotive were the fastest-growing end markets.

**FACT — secondary management reporting:** AI-server applications include DDR5 and TLVR; TLVR currently ships more heavily into NVIDIA, while management is prioritising CSP/ASIC applications where economics may be better.

### Financial inflection

**FACT — company monthly revenue:** Jan–Aug 2026 revenue was NT$5.605bn, **+32.2% y/y**. May–Aug monthly growth accelerated to +37.8%, +57.4%, +63.3% and +53.1% respectively.

**FACT — 2025 company presentation:** 2025 revenue NT$6.624bn (+20.3%), gross profit NT$1.744bn (+31%), gross margin 26.3% (+2.1ppt), operating profit NT$794m (+40%), parent net income NT$1.056bn (+41%), EPS NT$10.26.

**FACT — Q2 2026 market/company-data references:** gross margin ~30.8%, operating margin ~17.2%, Q2 EPS ~NT$3.81. These are materially above 2025 full-year margins.

**INTERPRETATION:** company-wide acceleration and margin expansion are real. AI is material but still a minority of revenue, so the evidence does not support attributing the whole inflection to AI.

## 3x / 5x feasibility

Using **NT$24bn** controlled starting equity:

- **3x:** NT$72bn
- **5x:** NT$120bn
- **10x:** NT$240bn

At a 25x mature earnings multiple:
- 3x requires ~**NT$2.88bn** annual net income / ~NT$24.7 EPS;
- 5x requires ~**NT$4.8bn** / ~NT$41.2 EPS;
- 10x requires ~**NT$9.6bn** / ~NT$82.5 EPS.

At 30x:
- 3x requires ~NT$2.4bn / ~NT$20.6 EPS;
- 5x ~NT$4.0bn / ~NT$34.4 EPS;
- 10x ~NT$8.0bn / ~NT$68.7 EPS.

Versus ~NT$1.24bn LTM net income, **3x requires roughly a doubling to 2.3x of earnings plus retention of a premium multiple**. That is demanding but not structurally absurd if AI/CSP/800V content becomes a major revenue/margin engine. 5x requires a much larger company transformation and is not currently evidenced.

## The most important counter-evidence

The thesis is not clean enough to call validated:

1. **AI is only ~12–13% of current revenue.** Automotive remains larger and has attractive margins, so current company growth is mixed-engine rather than a pure AI-power inflection.
2. **TLVR competition is substantial.** Management reportedly views NVIDIA TLVR pricing/margins as less attractive than hoped and is leaning toward CSP/ASIC applications.
3. **Order visibility appears short.** Secondary call notes suggest only roughly 2–3 months visibility versus longer visibility at larger peers.
4. **Architecture can migrate again.** If VPD/integrated voltage regulation moves magnetics inside the package, conventional board-level TLVR content could eventually face substitution.
5. **3x needs AI to become company-transforming**, not merely grow from 10% to 15% of revenue.

## Why it survives

The setup nevertheless passes the bounded Unicorn test better than Vicor at today's references:

- small starting equity (~NT$24bn);
- real earnings, cash-generative incumbent rather than pre-profit optionality;
- high-current power products already majority of product mix;
- AI mix rising from ~7% → 10% → 12–13%;
- company-wide revenue and margins accelerating;
- exposure extends beyond current NVIDIA TLVR into CSP/ASIC and reported 800V HVDC development.

The crucial thesis is therefore broader than **“NVIDIA needs TLVR.”**

It is:

> **As AI power architecture becomes more complex across GPU, ASIC, memory and 800V distribution, can TAI-TECH turn power-magnetics competence into a materially larger, higher-margin AI/CSP business before vertical integration commoditizes the layer?**

## Current conclusion

**Unicorn state: POTENTIAL — 3x is economically plausible but not validated; 5x is unvalidated; 10x unsupported.**

This is a better *original-thesis candidate* than Vicor at current valuations because the new bottleneck can still plausibly transform a ~NT$24bn company. But the evidence must show AI/CSP power content moving materially beyond today's ~12–13% revenue mix.
