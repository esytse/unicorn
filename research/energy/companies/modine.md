The operating evidence is unusually strong, but the current stock price still requires a successful margin/FCF normalization and a clean post-separation transition. The current signal is therefore **REASSESS / WAIT — not ACTION**.

## Why the setup is portfolio-relevant

**FACT:** FY2026 adjusted EBITDA was **$471m**. FY2027 guidance is **$650–680m adjusted EBITDA**, with sales growth of **20–35%**; the current FY2027 guide still includes Performance Technologies for the full fiscal year and will be refreshed after the separation closes.

**FACT:** Q1 FY2027 Data Centers sales were **$348.6m, +90% y/y**, driven primarily by higher hyperscale-customer sales in North America.

**FACT:** the company has said its expanded capacity provides a path toward roughly **$2bn of Data Centers revenue by FY2028**.

**FACT:** in May 2026 a strategic customer agreed that Modine would reserve / provide more than **$4bn of Airedale cooling supply capacity for calendar 2027–2029** and paid **$165m upfront** to support the associated capacity investment.

**INTERPRETATION:** the >$4bn agreement is unusually strong revealed-preference evidence for qualified capacity and schedule value, but it is not equivalent to $4bn of irrevocable booked revenue. The wording is a long-term capacity agreement and the upfront payment partly funds the buildout.

**FACT:** management says the largest increase in forward demand is being driven by **three major customers — two hyperscalers and one large neocloud customer**. A large share of near-term production is covered by firm purchase orders, while later years depend more heavily on long-term agreements and refreshed customer forecasts.

**INTERPRETATION:** this is strong demand quality, but customer concentration is material enough to cap position size until multi-customer conversion is better demonstrated.

## Performance Technologies separation / Modexus transition

**FACT:** Modine is spinning off Performance Technologies and combining it with Gentherm in a Reverse Morris Trust transaction. Modine shareholders are expected to own approximately **40% of the combined Gentherm/Performance Technologies company**, while Modine receives **$210m of cash**, subject to adjustment, to reduce debt.

**FACT:** the transaction was valued at approximately **$1.0bn** when signed in January 2026 and is structured to be generally tax-free for U.S. federal income-tax purposes.

**FACT:** Gentherm shareholders approved the transaction on **10 Sep 2026**. Modine now expects the transaction to close on **1 Oct 2026**, subject to customary closing conditions. After completion, continuing Modine is expected to rebrand as **Modexus Solutions** while retaining ticker **MOD**.

**INTERPRETATION:** the separation is a meaningful near-term catalyst because it leaves a cleaner Climate Solutions / data-centre cooling equity while distributing a stake in the combined Gentherm business. The current share price already reflects some of this simplification, so the transaction should not be counted as free incremental value. The correct post-close comparison is the combined value of continuing MOD plus the distributed Gentherm stake versus today’s pre-close MOD share price.

**OPEN QUESTION:** what continuing-company EBITDA and FCF guidance will Modine issue after the separation, and how much value will the market assign to the distributed Gentherm shares after deal completion?

## Margin normalization

**FACT:** Q1 FY2027 Data Centers gross profit was **$70.3m** and gross margin was **20.2%**, down about **960 bps** from 29.8% a year earlier.

**FACT:** Data Centers operating income was **$46.3m**, or about **13.3% operating margin**, versus $34.7m / 18.9% a year earlier. Adjusted EBITDA was **$51.7m, +27% y/y** despite much faster revenue growth.

**FACT:** the company attributes the Q1 pressure to rapid North-American capacity expansion, production disruption from supplier-capacity constraints, higher material costs and warranty effects. The 10-Q indicates roughly **$16m of higher material costs** and about **$4m of higher warranty expense** in the Data Centers segment.

**HYPOTHESIS / MANAGEMENT EXPECTATION:** management expects Data Centers profitability to recover sequentially as supply disruptions clear and fixed costs are absorbed, with segment EBITDA margin moving back toward roughly **19–20%** and potentially above 20% later in the year if execution holds.

**INTERPRETATION:** this is the key Gate-E proof point. Revenue growth is already visible; the stock needs evidence that growth converts to normalized EBITDA and FCF rather than merely consuming working capital and capex.

## Cash conversion and capital intensity

**FACT:** Q1 FY2027 operating cash flow was **$41.4m** and capital expenditure was **$46.4m**, implying a simple OCF-minus-capex free-cash-flow proxy of approximately **-$5m** for the quarter.

**FACT:** net debt was approximately **$432.9m** at quarter end and reported leverage remained modest at about **0.9x**. The separation is expected to provide $210m of cash to reduce debt.

**INTERPRETATION:** the balance sheet can support the current ramp, but the $165m customer payment and $210m transaction cash must not be capitalized as recurring operating cash flow. The decisive metric is normalized post-ramp FCF after customer-advance timing and elevated expansion capex are stripped out.

## March-2028 scenario framework

These are **illustrative scenario boundaries, not forecasts or price targets**. They value the economic package a current MOD shareholder should receive through the separation: continuing Modexus/MOD plus the distributed Gentherm stake.

### Bear — approximately $115–145 combined value

**HYPOTHESIS:** Data Centers revenue grows but margin recovery disappoints, customer concentration becomes a visible risk, FY2028 cooling revenue undershoots the ~$2bn path, post-separation FCF remains weak and valuation compresses as industry capacity expands. The distributed Gentherm stake provides some offset but does not prevent material downside.

### Base — approximately $225–270 combined value

**HYPOTHESIS:** the separation closes cleanly, continuing Climate Solutions converts the current capacity ramp into roughly mid/high-teens to ~20% normalized EBITDA margins, Data Centers moves toward the company’s FY2028 revenue path, Commercial HVAC remains healthy, leverage falls and the market values the cleaner company at a growth-industrial premium without assuming another major multiple expansion.

At the ~$179 reference this is roughly **26–51%** upside, useful but not sufficient on its own to satisfy the portfolio’s 2x hurdle.

### Upside — approximately $320–380 combined value

**HYPOTHESIS:** Data Centers approaches or exceeds the ~$2bn FY2028 path with ~20%+ EBITDA margins, new capacity is utilized quickly, FCF normalizes strongly, customer concentration does not impair pricing/returns, and the pure-play structure sustains a premium valuation. The distributed Gentherm stake retains meaningful value.

At the current reference this is approximately **79–112%** upside and is the first scenario that can make Modine a meaningful 2x-portfolio contributor.

### Stretch — approximately $440–520 combined value

**HYPOTHESIS:** Modine proves a durable multi-customer thermal-platform position, data-centre revenue and margins materially exceed the current FY2028 path, the market continues to capitalize long-duration AI cooling scarcity at a high multiple, and both continuing MOD and the distributed Gentherm stake execute well.

This requires exceptional execution and should not be used as the entry base case.

## Transaction rules

### Entry / reassess

- **Price-led reassessment:** approximately **$155–165** while the demand, separation and margin-recovery thesis remains intact.
- **Evidence-led reassessment above that range:** clean separation close plus updated continuing-company guidance showing materially stronger normalized EBITDA/FCF; Data Centers EBITDA margin recovery toward ~19–20%+; strong conversion of the $4bn capacity agreement; or evidence of broader repeat customer qualification.
- A price crossing alone is **not** an automatic Buy signal; refresh the underwrite first.

### Add

Add only after evidence improves without a compensating deterioration, especially:
- post-spin continuing-company EBITDA/FCF visibility improves;
- Data Centers margin recovers while revenue remains strong;
- customer concentration reduces or repeat qualification broadens;
- capacity ramp completes without another major funding cycle.

### Trim

Consider trimming if:
- price begins to capitalize the upside/stretch case before margin/FCF evidence arrives;
- data-centre growth remains strong but incremental margin/FCF deteriorates;
- customer concentration rises materially;
- the post-spin pure-play premium outruns normalized earnings.

### Sell / thesis break

Sell/reject the thesis if:
- Data Centers EBITDA margin fails to recover toward the high-teens / ~20% range after the current ramp;
- the >$4bn capacity agreement fails to convert into acceptable margins and cash returns;
- one major customer materially reduces/cancels committed demand and the gap is not replaced;
- normalized FCF remains weak after customer advances and elevated growth capex are removed;
- capacity expansion creates structural overcapacity or commoditization before the March-2028 window closes;
- post-separation execution materially impairs per-share value.

## Architecture resilience

**FACT:** the Data Centers business spans chillers, dry coolers, precision air handlers, CRAHs / fan walls, rear-door heat exchangers, coolant distribution units, immersion-related systems, modular data-centre solutions, controls, parts and service.

**INTERPRETATION:** the breadth is strategically useful because thermal value can migrate as rack density and coolant temperature change. Modine is less dependent on one cold-plate / CDU implementation than a narrow component supplier, although product mix and competitive intensity can still shift materially.

## Why capture can persist

- thermal capacity is physically mandatory for usable compute;
- customer pre-funding is unusually strong evidence of schedule value and qualification scarcity;
- Modine / Airedale spans facility-scale thermal systems and multiple cooling architectures;
- smaller company size gives data-centre growth meaningful total-company earnings sensitivity;
- current hyperscale / neocloud demand is supported by firm orders and multiyear capacity commitments rather than only industry forecasts;
- a normalized installed base can create parts / service / controls opportunities beyond equipment delivery.

## Counter-evidence / risks

- forward demand is concentrated among a few very large customers;
- Q1 revenue growth did not translate proportionally into EBITDA because expansion, supplier constraints and materials absorbed economics;
- current free cash flow is weak after capex;
- customer advances can flatter cash conversion if treated as recurring economics;
- aggressive industry capacity build can reduce scarcity and pricing after 2028;
- warm-water / dry-cooling and increasingly standardized interfaces can shift product mix and supplier bargaining power;
- additional growth beyond current committed capacity may require another capital cycle.
