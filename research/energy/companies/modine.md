The 17 Sep Reverse Morris Trust (RMT) adjustment is material enough to require explicit treatment, but the parties state that the mechanism is designed to preserve the negotiated relative equity values. The lower cash distribution should therefore **not** be treated mechanically as value leakage. The current signal remains **REASSESS / WAIT — not ACTION**.

## Why the setup is portfolio-relevant

**FACT:** FY2026 adjusted EBITDA was **$471m**. FY2027 guidance is **$650–680m adjusted EBITDA**, with sales growth of **20–35%**; the current FY2027 guide includes Performance Technologies for the full fiscal year and is expected to be refreshed after the separation.

**FACT:** Q1 FY2027 Data Centers sales were **$348.6m, +90% y/y**. The company has described a path toward roughly **$2bn of Data Centers revenue by FY2028**.

**FACT:** in May 2026 a strategic customer agreed that Modine would reserve/provide more than **$4bn of Airedale cooling supply capacity for calendar 2027–2029** and paid **$165m upfront** to support associated capacity investment.

**INTERPRETATION:** the >$4bn agreement is unusually strong revealed-preference evidence for qualified capacity and schedule value, but it is a long-term capacity agreement rather than $4bn of irrevocable booked revenue.

**FACT:** management has said the largest increase in forward demand is being driven by three major customers — two hyperscalers and one large neocloud customer.

**INTERPRETATION:** demand quality is strong, but customer concentration remains material enough to cap position size until multi-customer conversion is better demonstrated.

## Performance Technologies separation / Modexus transition

### Original structure

**FACT:** Modine is spinning off Performance Technologies and combining it with Gentherm in a Reverse Morris Trust. At signing on 29 Jan 2026, the transaction was valued at approximately **$1.0bn**; Modine was expected to receive **$210m cash**, and former SpinCo holders were expected to own about **40%** of the combined Gentherm/Performance Technologies company.

### 17 Sep 2026 adjustment

**FACT:** Gentherm and Modine disclosed on **17 Sep 2026** that changes in overlapping shareholder ownership caused by market trading require an exchange-ratio adjustment under the merger agreement. They expect Gentherm to issue approximately **2,902,466 additional shares** in the merger.

**FACT:** to offset the value of those additional shares, the pre-merger SpinCo cash distribution to Modine is expected to fall from **$210m to $159m**, while Gentherm will pay an aggregate special cash dividend of approximately **$58.35m**, or about **$1.90 per share**, to pre-closing Gentherm shareholders, conditioned on closing.

**FACT:** based on the expected exchange ratio and 31,230,226 fully diluted Gentherm shares as of 16 Sep, pre-closing Gentherm holders are expected to own approximately **56.4%** of the combined company and former SpinCo holders approximately **43.6%**, before overlapping ownership effects and subject to final calculations.

**FACT:** the joint release says the adjustment is **not intended to change the negotiated relative equity values** and is expected to have a neutral effect on transaction economics.

**INTERPRETATION:** the reduced $159m cash distribution is therefore not, by itself, evidence of $51m of value leakage to Modine shareholders. The adjustment exchanges part of the cash/debt-reduction leg for additional equity value in the combined company while preserving the negotiated relative economics by design. The relevant shareholder value package remains continuing MOD plus the distributed Gentherm stake.

**FACT:** the SpinCo distribution record date is **28 Sep 2026**; the distribution and merger remain expected on **1 Oct 2026**, subject to closing conditions. Modine common stock is expected to trade with due bills from 28 Sep through the expected closing date, with the ex-distribution date expected **2 Oct 2026**.

**FACT:** Modine shareholders will continue to hold the same number of MOD shares after closing, in addition to the Gentherm shares received through the transaction.

**OPEN QUESTION:** the final exchange ratio, final cash/dividend amounts, exact Gentherm shares received per MOD share and market value of the distributed Gentherm stake remain unknown until final closing calculations.

**OPEN QUESTION:** the lower cash distribution means Modine receives **$51m less cash for debt reduction** than the signing structure. Even if relative transaction economics are neutral, the continuing-company balance-sheet mix changes and should be reflected in the post-close leverage/FCF bridge.

### Gate-E implication

**INTERPRETATION:** the 17 Sep disclosure is a **REASSESS** catalyst, not a thesis break and not an ACTION. It resolves the earlier concern that the lower cash payment might automatically represent value leakage, but it does not create a new entry condition. The existing **$155–165** price-led reassessment remains unchanged pending final closing economics and post-separation guidance.

The next dated decision points are the **28 Sep record date**, **1 Oct expected close/final exchange economics**, and the first continuing-company guidance/margin/FCF update after separation.

## Margin normalization

**FACT:** Q1 FY2027 Data Centers gross profit was **$70.3m** and gross margin was **20.2%**, down from 29.8% a year earlier. Data Centers operating income was **$46.3m**, or about **13.3% operating margin**, versus 18.9% a year earlier; adjusted EBITDA was **$51.7m, +27% y/y** despite much faster revenue growth.

**FACT:** the company attributed pressure to rapid North-American capacity expansion, supplier constraints, higher material costs and warranty effects.

**HYPOTHESIS / MANAGEMENT EXPECTATION:** management expects profitability to recover as supply disruption clears and fixed costs are absorbed, with Data Centers EBITDA margin moving back toward roughly **19–20%** and potentially above 20% later in the year if execution holds.

**INTERPRETATION:** this remains the key Gate-E proof point. Revenue growth is already visible; the stock needs evidence that growth converts to normalized EBITDA and FCF rather than merely consuming working capital and capex.

## Cash conversion and capital intensity

**FACT:** Q1 FY2027 operating cash flow was **$41.4m** and capex **$46.4m**, implying a simple OCF-minus-capex proxy of approximately **-$5m** for the quarter.

**FACT:** net debt was approximately **$432.9m** at quarter end and leverage about **0.9x**.

**INTERPRETATION:** the $165m customer payment and transaction cash must not be capitalized as recurring operating cash flow. The decisive metric is normalized post-ramp FCF after customer-advance timing and elevated expansion capex are stripped out. Following the 17 Sep adjustment, the post-close leverage bridge should use the expected **$159m** SpinCo cash distribution rather than the original $210m, subject to final closing calculations.

## March-2028 scenario framework

These are **illustrative scenario boundaries, not forecasts or price targets**. They value the economic package a current MOD shareholder should receive through the separation: continuing MOD/Modexus plus the distributed Gentherm stake.

- **Bear — approximately $115–145 combined value.** Data Centers revenue grows but margin recovery disappoints, concentration becomes visible, FY2028 cooling revenue undershoots the ~$2bn path, post-separation FCF remains weak and valuation compresses.
- **Base — approximately $225–270 combined value.** Separation closes cleanly, Climate Solutions converts the ramp into mid/high-teens to ~20% normalized EBITDA margins, Data Centers moves toward the FY2028 revenue path and leverage falls.
- **Upside — approximately $320–380 combined value.** Data Centers approaches/exceeds the ~$2bn FY2028 path with ~20%+ EBITDA margins, new capacity is utilized quickly and FCF normalizes strongly.
- **Stretch — approximately $440–520 combined value.** Modine proves a durable multi-customer thermal-platform position and both continuing MOD and the distributed Gentherm stake execute exceptionally well.

**INTERPRETATION:** the 17 Sep exchange-ratio adjustment does not justify changing these scenario boundaries before final closing math because the parties explicitly designed the adjustment to preserve relative transaction economics. The post-close model should be rebuilt from the actual continuing MOD balance sheet plus the actual Gentherm shares distributed.

## Architecture resilience and risks

**FACT:** the Data Centers business spans chillers, dry coolers, precision air handlers, CRAHs/fan walls, rear-door heat exchangers, coolant distribution units, immersion-related systems, modular data-centre solutions, controls, parts and service.

**INTERPRETATION:** breadth reduces dependence on one cold-plate/CDU implementation, although product mix and competitive intensity can still shift materially.

Key risks remain customer concentration; margin pressure during rapid expansion; weak normalized FCF after capex; customer advances flattering cash conversion; industry capacity build reducing scarcity/pricing; cooling-architecture shifts; and another capital cycle beyond current committed capacity.
