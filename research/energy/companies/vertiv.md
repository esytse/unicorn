# Vertiv — AI Energy Investment Capture

**Status:** Watch  
**Investment Capture Score:** **4.4 / 5**  
**Confidence:** Medium-High  
**Primary lanes:** integrated electrical infrastructure; thermal chain; BTM / microgrid orchestration

## Current conclusion

**INTERPRETATION:** Vertiv is the highest-direct-sensitivity platform in the shortlist. It sits across electrical distribution, thermal management, controls and increasingly behind-the-meter power architecture, so it can capture value as the bottleneck migrates from grid connection to facility power to heat rejection.

## Evidence

**FACT:** Q2 2026 sales were **$3.274bn, +24% y/y**.

**FACT:** adjusted operating profit rose 51%; adjusted operating margin reached **22.6%, +410 bps**.

**FACT:** Q2 adjusted free cash flow was **$925m**, and the company ended the quarter in a net-cash position.

**FACT:** full-year 2026 guidance implies about **$14bn revenue** and ~31% organic growth at the midpoint.

**FACT:** acquisitions of UtilityInnovation Group, Strategic Thermal Labs and ThermoKey extend the platform into BTM/microgrid controls, chip-side cooling/validation and heat rejection.

## Why capture can persist

- direct AI/hyperscale demand sensitivity;
- power + thermal systems become harder to coordinate as rack/site density rises;
- customer value increasingly sits in integrated reference designs, testing and deployment rather than commodity components;
- service and controls extend economics beyond initial equipment;
- architecture breadth reduces dependence on one cooling or AC/DC implementation.

## Counter-evidence / risks

- rapid capacity expansion can reduce scarcity;
- OCP/open interfaces can commoditize cold plates, CDUs and some power sub-layers;
- acquisitions raise integration and capital-allocation risk;
- Vertiv is already an obvious AI-infrastructure winner and can be priced accordingly.

## Thesis breakers

- margin expansion reverses despite revenue growth;
- thermal/electrical standardization shifts value to lower-cost ODMs;
- BTM acquisitions fail to create incremental margin/service economics;
- customer concentration or project delays materially weaken cash conversion.

## Gate-C question

How much of the 2026 growth/margin step-up is durable normalized economics versus peak AI buildout, and what entry price leaves a 12–15% return after normalizing growth?
