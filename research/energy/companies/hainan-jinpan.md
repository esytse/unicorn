# Hainan Jinpan Smart Technology — AI Energy Investment Capture

**Status:** Investigating  
**Investment Capture Score:** **4.0 / 5**  
**Confidence:** Medium-Low  
**Primary lanes:** transformers; prefabricated data-centre power modules; emerging SST optionality

## Current conclusion

**INTERPRETATION:** Jinpan is one of the more interesting smaller listed challengers surfaced by the E2E work because AI/data-centre orders can materially change company economics and its product set spans transformers, MV/LV distribution and prefabricated power modules rather than a single component.

The evidence is strong enough to investigate, not strong enough to promote to Watch yet.

## Evidence

**FACT:** Reuters reports H1 2026 new data-centre orders more than **quadrupled year over year** and related backlog nearly tripled.

**FACT:** the company's H1 2026 report describes data-centre power cabins / modules covering MV switchgear, transformers, LV distribution, bus systems and intelligent monitoring.

**FACT:** the company is developing data-centre solid-state transformers and reports prototype / test progress in its H1 filing.

**FACT:** company investor communications reported total order backlog of roughly RMB10.7bn at June 30, 2026, with overseas backlog representing more than half.

## Why capture could persist

- smaller company size creates high earnings sensitivity;
- prefabricated integrated power modules benefit from speed-to-deployment;
- transformer / switchgear content is architecture-resilient;
- overseas order growth can expand addressable market;
- SST development provides optionality if 800-VDC / higher-efficiency architectures mature.

## Counter-evidence / risks

- qualification and customer depth outside China are less established than at global incumbents;
- Chinese electrical-equipment competition can be intense and price-led;
- current order growth can attract rapid capacity expansion;
- SST commercial adoption is early and should not be capitalized as base-case earnings;
- investor access, governance, disclosure quality and geopolitical constraints require separate Gate-C treatment.

## Thesis breakers

- overseas backlog fails to convert to cash / margin;
- data-centre share proves project-driven rather than repeatable;
- transformer/module margins erode as competitors add capacity;
- major hyperscaler qualification remains limited;
- SST remains a prototype without commercial wins.

## Gate-C question

Is Jinpan genuinely a lower-cost / faster-deployment structural winner with global customer qualification, or mainly a high-growth domestic equipment vendor benefiting from the same temporary shortage?
