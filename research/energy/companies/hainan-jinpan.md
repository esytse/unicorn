# Hainan Jinpan Smart Technology — Gate-E AI-Power Underwrite

**Ticker:** SSE STAR: 688676  
**Status:** Investigating  
**Investment Capture Score:** **4.0 / 5**  
**Confidence:** Medium-Low  
**Primary lanes:** transformers; prefabricated data-centre power modules; emerging SST/HVDC optionality  
**Last substantive update:** 2026-09-10  
**Backlog:** #130

## Gate-E conclusion

**CONCLUSION:** Jinpan is a genuine AI-power operating-capture candidate, not merely a thematic transformer name. H1 2026 data-centre and overseas order growth is large enough to change group economics, the company has an established international footprint, and a new Virginia production base improves North-American delivery capability. But at the **10 September 2026 close of about CNY65.51**, the stock already discounts a significant earnings ramp while H1 cash conversion remains negative and named hyperscaler/customer verification remains incomplete.

**Gate-E portfolio role:** `Independent AI-power catalyst candidate / pre-cash-conversion watch`.  
**Current alert state:** **REASSESS — not ACTION**.  
**Research status:** remains `Investigating`.  
**Confidence:** Medium on demand/backlog evidence; Medium-Low on per-share March-2028 return because cash conversion, named global qualification and valuation remain unresolved.

Jinpan improves the **portfolio architecture** because its main risk driver is AI-power/electrical infrastructure rather than HBM or semiconductor commercialization. It does not yet solve the £80k objective because a 2x outcome from the current price requires an upside-to-stretch earnings/multiple combination rather than the base case.

## 1. Current capitalization and valuation

**FACT:** Jinpan closed at approximately **CNY65.51 on 10 September 2026**, versus CNY67.46 on 9 September and CNY65.74 on 8 September.

**FACT:** H1 2026 attributable net profit was **RMB302.7m** and basic/diluted EPS was **RMB0.66**. This implies a working share-count denominator of roughly **459m shares**.

**INTERPRETATION:** At CNY65.51, the implied equity value is therefore approximately **RMB30.1bn**.

**FACT:** A current 20-analyst consensus compilation shows average EPS estimates of approximately **RMB1.97 for 2026, RMB2.74 for 2027 and RMB3.54 for 2028**.

On that basis, the current price is approximately:

- **33.3x** 2026 consensus EPS;
- **23.9x** 2027 consensus EPS;
- **18.5x** 2028 consensus EPS.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is not an obviously cheap transformer stock. The valuation already assumes strong 2027–2028 earnings conversion. Gate E therefore requires evidence that the backlog converts with acceptable margin and cash rather than simply extrapolating order growth.

---

## 2. Demand and backlog — genuinely strong

**FACT:** H1 2026 total new sales orders were **RMB7.493bn, +69.31% y/y**.

**FACT:** Data-centre new orders were **RMB3.869bn, +336.25%**, data-centre revenue was **RMB1.265bn, +122.57%**, and period-end data-centre backlog was **RMB5.092bn, +179.07%**.

**FACT:** Overseas new orders were **RMB4.190bn, +270.69%**, overseas revenue was **RMB1.481bn, +68.30%**, and overseas backlog was **RMB6.239bn, +122.65%**, equal to **58.32% of total backlog**.

**FACT:** Management says the overseas order mix is concentrated mainly in AIDC data centres. It also says orders span dry and liquid transformers, switchgear and box substations, so the opportunity is broader than one product.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is unusually strong forward-demand evidence. The critical Gate-E question is no longer whether AI infrastructure demand exists; it is **how much of the order surge becomes durable revenue, margin and free cash flow by March 2028**.

---

## 3. Global qualification — stronger, but still not clean enough

**FACT:** Jinpan says it has served data-centre projects for many years and has completed more than one hundred international/domestic AIDC projects. Older company materials name domestic customers including Baidu, Huawei, Alibaba, China Mobile, China Telecom and China Unicom.

**FACT:** In a 2 September 2026 investor response, management said customers cover leading global data-centre and energy/power enterprises and that long-term relationships exist with international customers. The company did **not** confirm the investor's specific claim that its US subsidiary directly supplies multiple named US-listed technology giants.

**FACT:** Jinpan opened another production base in **Wytheville, Virginia** during H1 2026. It also operates or has described production/operating capability across the United States, Mexico and Malaysia.

**INTERPRETATION:** The evidence supports real North-American/international qualification, but **named hyperscaler attribution remains below the repo's FACT threshold**. This limits how aggressively the market's AIDC narrative should be capitalized.

**OPEN QUESTION:** Which current North-American AIDC customers account for the rapid order growth, and how repeatable are orders across campuses/projects?

---

## 4. Cash conversion — the main financial falsification

**FACT:** H1 2026 revenue was **RMB3.556bn (+12.73%)** and attributable net profit was **RMB302.7m (+14.23%)**.

**FACT:** Operating cash flow was **-RMB23.8m**, versus **+RMB234.8m** a year earlier.

**FACT:** Cash paid for property, plant, equipment and intangibles was about **RMB129m**, giving a simple OCF-minus-capex proxy around **-RMB153m**.

**FACT:** Inventory increased from roughly **RMB2.59bn at December 2025 to RMB3.24bn at June 2026**; accounts receivable rose from roughly RMB2.37bn to RMB2.56bn; short-term borrowings rose from about RMB492m to RMB938m.

**INTERPRETATION:** The negative cash flow can plausibly reflect working-capital build for a much larger backlog, but it still falsifies any shortcut from order growth to economic capture. A high-quality Gate-E rerating requires at least partial reversal as deliveries convert through H2 2026 and 2027.

---

## 5. Capacity and product optionality

**FACT:** The Virginia expansion strengthens localized US delivery. Management says orders are accepted only after capacity assessment.

**FACT:** The Tongxiang data-centre power-module / VPI-transformer digital-factory infrastructure was completed in H1 2026 and line installation/commissioning was under way.

**FACT:** Jinpan's first **13.8kV SST prototype adapted for the North-American grid** completed internal testing and was sent overseas for testing in H1 2026. The company also reports work on HVDC/data-centre power architecture.

**INTERPRETATION:** Local production and broader power-module capability are valuable for speed-to-power. SST/HVDC should remain **optionality only** until commercial orders/qualification emerge; the March-2028 base case is conventional transformer/electrical backlog conversion.

## 6. March-2028 scenario boundaries

These are **illustrative decision boundaries, not forecasts or price targets**. They use 2027/2028 earnings evidence and deliberately vary both earnings and valuation because the current stock is already priced for growth.

| Scenario | Illustrative March-2028 boundary | Approx return from CNY65.51 | What has to be true |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Bear | **CNY40–48** | -39% to -27% | backlog slows, working capital stays heavy, margins compress and/or global qualification disappoints |
| Base | **CNY75–90** | +14% to +37% | 2027 consensus-like earnings convert, backlog remains healthy, cash conversion normalizes only partially |
| Upside | **CNY115–135** | +76% to +106% | 2028 EPS reaches roughly high-consensus range, AIDC/overseas mix remains strong, cash conversion improves and premium growth multiple persists |
| Stretch | **CNY160–190** | +144% to +190% | earnings materially exceed current consensus, named/repeat global AIDC wins de-risk qualification, margins/cash strengthen and market sustains a premium multiple |

**INTERPRETATION:** A **2x** outcome from today's price is possible only toward the upper end of the upside case or in the stretch case. The base case is not sufficient for the aggressive portfolio hurdle.

**HYPOTHESIS:** A fresh price-led reassessment becomes more attractive around **CNY54–57**, provided backlog, overseas qualification, margin and balance-sheet evidence remain intact. This is a new Gate-E condition derived from the current 18-month scenario surface and is **not** the historical ~CNY59 Gate-D normalized-return zone.

At ~CNY55, the midpoint upside boundary (~CNY125) would imply ~2.27x, providing more room for execution error than at CNY65.5.

## 8. Transaction rules

### Entry / research-level Buy condition
Do **not** buy merely because the historical Gate-D ~CNY59 zone is reached.

Reassess for an entry when either:

- **price-led:** shares reach roughly **CNY54–57** while backlog, customer/qualification, margins and balance-sheet evidence remain intact; or
- **evidence-led:** positive/normalizing operating cash flow plus durable AIDC/overseas backlog conversion and materially stronger named/repeat global qualification raise the evidence-supported upside enough that roughly **60%+ remaining-window upside** is credible without requiring the stretch case.

### Initial size
If a current entry condition is confirmed: **~5–7%** of Gate-E capital.

### Add
Increase only after evidence improves, especially:
- positive cash conversion after backlog deliveries;
- repeat global/AIDC order evidence;
- margin durability;
- valuation still leaves attractive remaining-window upside.

### Maximum size
- **~8%** before cash conversion and qualification evidence strengthen;
- **~10–12%** after repeat global qualification plus durable operating-cash/earnings conversion.

### Trim
Trim if valuation rerates materially faster than earnings/cash evidence, if a major catalyst becomes fully priced before realization, or if Jinpan no longer offers better forward asymmetry than alternative independent return engines.

### Sell / thesis break
Sell or materially reduce the research position if:
- overseas/data-centre backlog fails to convert to acceptable margins/cash;
- working-capital absorption remains structurally high through scaled deliveries;
- named/repeat global qualification remains weak while valuation continues to price global AIDC capture;
- competition/capacity expansion materially compresses transformer/module margins;
- tariffs, geopolitics, A-share access or governance materially impair per-share economics;
- debt/dilution/capital intensity rises enough to destroy the remaining-window case.

## 9. Portfolio comparison

**Versus JEM/SUSS:** Jinpan offers materially better thematic diversification. Its evidence is lower quality on named customers and cash conversion, but the return driver is AI electrical infrastructure rather than the HBM equipment cycle.

**Versus Weebit:** Jinpan has much stronger current revenue/backlog and earnings evidence, but less extreme architectural optionality.

**Versus Laifual:** both are smaller Asian growth specialists with customer/qualification risk. Jinpan has stronger current profitability and backlog scale; Laifual has a purer Physical-AI growth vector but is earlier in profit/cash conversion.

**CONCLUSION:** Jinpan deserves a place on the **conditional portfolio candidate surface**, but not an immediate ACTION at CNY65.5. It is more useful to the portfolio than another memory-correlated name because it can become an independent return engine if cash conversion and qualification improve.
