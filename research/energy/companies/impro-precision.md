## Evidence correction

The #282 lead was framed as possible gas-turbine hot-section exposure. Better evidence changes that interpretation.

Impro's AI-data-centre exposure is currently better supported through **high-horsepower engines used in distributed power generation** and **liquid-cooling components**. Its separately reported energy end market has historically included oil/gas activity. Do not equate generic “energy” growth with turbine blade/vane revenue without direct attribution.

## Financial base

2026 interim results:
- H1 revenue HK$3.018bn, +23.2% y/y;
- attributable profit HK$421.0m, +21.6%;
- adjusted attributable profit HK$433.1m, +20.4%;
- EPS HK$0.222.

FY25 research tied AI data-centre demand to high-horsepower engines:
- high-horsepower engine sales +43.3% y/y;
- 22.1% of FY25 sales, the largest sub-sector end market;
- new projects and deeper processing/full-finishing expected to support further growth;
- Mexico SLP Phase II sand-casting capacity scheduled for mass production in 2026.

Liquid-cooling demand also contributed to strong diversified-industrials growth.

## Corrected thesis

**AI data-centre buildout → distributed generation + liquid cooling → higher precision-manufacturing content → new project mass production + deeper processing → possible mix/margin uplift.**

This is a broader AI-infrastructure manufacturing thesis, not yet a proven turbine-hot-section scarcity thesis.

## Why it remains interesting

AI-related distributed-power exposure was already >20% of FY25 sales through the high-horsepower-engine sub-sector. Impro is profitable, has manufacturing scale and is adding capacity. That makes the transition company-material rather than thematic decoration.

## Missing evidence

Before full reverse-underwrite:
- H1/FY26 current high-horsepower-engine and cooling mix;
- AI-specific backlog/customer wins;
- incremental margins;
- Mexico SLP ramp evidence;
- current valuation and earnings bridge;
- durability after capacity additions.

## Classification

**POTENTIAL / EVIDENCE BUILD.**

Promote only if current segment evidence plus valuation shows the AI-power/cooling engine can create asymmetric consolidated earnings growth.

The earlier “energy acceleration = turbine hot-section” inference is rejected without source-level attribution.

### Primary evidence

This supports three AI-infrastructure vectors. It does **not** support treating all energy revenue as turbine hot-section exposure.
