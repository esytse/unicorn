## Evidence correction

The #282 lead was framed as possible gas-turbine hot-section exposure. Better evidence changes that interpretation.

Impro's AI-data-centre exposure is currently better supported through **high-horsepower engines used in distributed power generation** and **liquid-cooling components**. Its separately reported energy end market has historically included oil/gas activity. Do not equate generic “energy” growth with turbine blade/vane revenue without direct attribution.
