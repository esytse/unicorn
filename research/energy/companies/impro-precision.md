## Evidence correction

The #282 lead was framed as possible gas-turbine hot-section exposure. Better evidence changes that interpretation.

Impro's AI-data-centre exposure is currently better supported through **high-horsepower engines used in distributed power generation** and **liquid-cooling components**. Its separately reported energy end market has historically included oil/gas activity. Do not equate generic “energy” growth with turbine blade/vane revenue without direct attribution.

- attributable profit HK$421.0m, +21.6%;

FY25 research tied AI data-centre demand to high-horsepower engines:

## Corrected thesis

**AI data-centre buildout → distributed generation + liquid cooling → higher precision-manufacturing content → new project mass production + deeper processing → possible mix/margin uplift.**

This is a broader AI-infrastructure manufacturing thesis, not yet a proven turbine-hot-section scarcity thesis.

## Why it remains interesting

AI-related distributed-power exposure was already >20% of FY25 sales through the high-horsepower-engine sub-sector. Impro is profitable, has manufacturing scale and is adding capacity. That makes the transition company-material rather than thematic decoration.
