# GE Vernova — AI Energy Investment Capture

**Status:** Watch  
**Investment Capture Score:** **4.4 / 5**  
**Confidence:** Medium-High  
**Primary lanes:** large gas turbines / long-term service; grid electrification

## Current conclusion

**INTERPRETATION:** GE Vernova currently offers the cleanest combination of validated physical scarcity and merchant economic capture in the energy programme. Gas-turbine slot reservations, backlog growth, margin expansion, cash generation and long-lived service economics are all visible in current results.

It is not a pure AI company. That is partly a strength: AI/data-centre demand accelerates a cycle also supported by grid reliability, replacement generation, electrification and industrial demand.

## Evidence

**FACT:** Q2 2026 orders were $24.2bn, +88% organically.

**FACT:** backlog increased $13bn sequentially and total backlog reached $176bn.

**FACT:** Gas Power equipment backlog / slot reservation agreements increased to **116 GW**, with at least 125 GW expected by year-end 2026.

**FACT:** Q2 revenue was $11.1bn and adjusted EBITDA margin reached **11.3%**, up 340 bps organically.

**FACT:** GE Vernova reported strong cash generation and increasing 2026 guidance.

## Why capture can persist

- advanced-class gas-turbine OEM supply is concentrated;
- hot-section manufacturing and qualification are slow to expand;
- customer slot reservations make scarcity visible before revenue recognition;
- each installed turbine creates long-duration parts / service economics;
- Electrification provides exposure to transformers / grid equipment if generation architecture changes.

## Counter-evidence / risks

- turbine capacity expansion can normalize lead times later in the decade;
- gas pipeline / permitting constraints can delay booked projects;
- wind remains a lower-quality business inside the group;
- AI is not separately disclosed enough to isolate exact earnings sensitivity;
- the stock is already a widely recognized AI-power beneficiary, so valuation can dominate future returns.

## Thesis breakers

- materially weaker reservation conversion;
- service attachment / margins fail to rise with installed base;
- capacity expansion collapses turbine pricing before backlog converts;
- Electrification demand slows as transmission / data-centre projects are screened down;
- normalized FCF fails to support the valuation used in Gate C.

## Gate-C question

What turbine/service + Electrification earnings profile is already embedded in the current price, and what normalized margin / terminal multiple still provides a 12–15% expected return?
