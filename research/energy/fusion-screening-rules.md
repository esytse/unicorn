# Fusion Supplier Screening Rules

Apply these rules to follow-up work from #155.

1. **Necessity:** prove the component/system is technically required by a funded fusion programme or credible architecture.
2. **Qualification/scarcity:** establish why supply is difficult to substitute, manufacture or qualify.
3. **Capture:** distinguish technical relevance from company-level revenue, margin, pricing power and cash conversion.
4. **Delay resilience:** prefer companies whose non-fusion business supports the thesis if fusion slips 10–20 years.
5. **18-month relevance:** require a plausible catalyst/evidence path inside the active portfolio mandate before promotion.
6. **Capacity response:** model whether announced capacity expansion destroys scarcity economics.
7. **No double counting:** conventional grid/balance-of-plant exposure already captured in energy research does not receive a second valuation premium merely because a future customer may be a fusion plant.
8. **Evidence hierarchy:** funded contracts/orders and technical qualification outrank MOUs, strategic investments, developer forecasts and thematic claims.
