# Fusion Supplier Company Template

## FACT — direct fusion evidence
Contracts, orders, qualification, delivered hardware, named customers/programmes.

## FACT — non-fusion business
Revenue base, adjacent markets, segment economics, capacity and capex.

## INTERPRETATION — scarcity/capture
Why the supplier can or cannot retain pricing/share/margin.

## HYPOTHESIS — fusion upside
Incremental demand scenarios without assuming developer target dates are facts.

## Gate-E
Current valuation, 18-month catalysts, downside/permanent-loss risk, evidence confidence, responsible sizing and marginal portfolio contribution.

## Falsification
Capacity expansion, competitor qualification, vertical integration, programme delay/cancellation, commoditisation, financial immateriality.

## Decision
Research / monitor / parked / portfolio candidate — with explicit evidence gate.
