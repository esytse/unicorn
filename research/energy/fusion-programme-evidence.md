# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Programme Evidence

Programme-primary evidence used in the first pass includes:

- ITER technical documentation for remote handling, diagnostics and superconducting systems;
- Fusion for Energy supplier/procurement evidence for remote handling and high-voltage gyrotron power supplies;
- UK Government / UK Fusion Energy STEP investment prospectus for required system-partner and manufacturing capabilities.

These sources establish **what must be engineered and procured**. They do not by themselves establish which listed supplier will earn attractive returns; company-level work remains separate.
