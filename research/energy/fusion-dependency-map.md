# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Dependency Map

Commercial plant success depends on more than plasma physics. Supplier research should track dependencies across:

- confinement/magnet or driver system;
- heating/pulsed power;
- vacuum;
- plasma-facing/extreme materials;
- fuel/tritium cycle where applicable;
- heat extraction / conversion;
- diagnostics/control;
- remote maintenance;
- grid/balance-of-plant;
- repeatable manufacturing and qualification.

Failure or delay in any serial dependency can defer plant economics even when another subsystem succeeds. This supports the picks-and-shovels approach but also argues against valuing distant volume demand too early.
