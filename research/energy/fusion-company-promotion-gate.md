# Fusion Company Promotion Gate

A fusion-adjacent listed company should not enter `watchlist.md` or the active top-10 merely because it appears in a fusion supply chain.

Promotion requires evidence for all of:

1. **Direct relevance** — funded order, qualification or technically specific supplier relationship.
2. **Scarcity** — difficult-to-substitute capability, manufacturing process, IP, qualification or installed capacity.
3. **Capture** — plausible revenue/margin/cash benefit at company level.
4. **Resilience** — acceptable thesis if commercial fusion slips by 10–20 years.
5. **Window** — a credible evidence/catalyst path inside the active 18-month mandate.
6. **Relative merit** — better marginal portfolio contribution than the capital/research alternative after downside, correlation and position-size constraints.

Failure on #3 or #5 normally means **monitor**, not active research promotion.
