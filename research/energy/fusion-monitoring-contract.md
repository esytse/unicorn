# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Monitoring Contract

Fusion monitoring should be **event-driven and quiet**.

Notify/reassess only for material events such as funded supplier awards, major qualification milestones, capacity changes affecting scarcity, financial disclosure showing materiality, or a thesis-breaking technical/programme change.

Do not generate portfolio alerts for routine fusion fundraising, speculative commercialization dates, generic government support or repeated media enthusiasm without a supplier-level consequence.
