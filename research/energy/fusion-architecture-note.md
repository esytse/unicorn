# Fusion Architecture Neutrality Note

This research lane deliberately avoids selecting a winning fusion architecture.

Magnetic tokamaks, stellarators, magnetic mirrors and non-magnetic/inertial approaches do not share every component. A supplier should therefore receive an architecture-diversification benefit only when there is direct evidence that its technology is reusable across more than one approach.

Examples from the first pass:
- CFS HTS technology is being used/licensed beyond its own tokamak programme to Realta's magnetic mirror and Type One's stellarator work, supporting cross-architecture magnet relevance.
- Generic grid/balance-of-plant exposure is architecture-neutral but is already captured in the broader energy thesis and should not be double counted.

Architecture neutrality is a risk-control principle, not an assumption that every enabling technology applies to every fusion design.
