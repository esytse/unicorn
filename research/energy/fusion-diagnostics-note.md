# Fusion Diagnostics / Controls — First-Pass Note

ITER plans roughly 60 diagnostic instruments measuring 101 parameters under conditions substantially harsher than current machines, including far higher neutron fluence and much longer pulse duration. This establishes a genuine hardened instrumentation/control problem.

The investable challenge is supplier concentration and materiality. Scientific-instrument companies may possess relevant capability, but generic analytical-instrument exposure is insufficient. Promote only after direct fusion qualification/orders and company-level economics are evidenced.
