# Fusion Grid / Balance-of-Plant — First-Pass Note

A future fusion power plant still requires conventional electrical and, depending on architecture, thermal balance-of-plant equipment. That does not create a new thesis for Siemens Energy, Eaton, nVent, Vertiv or other existing energy names by itself.

Fusion demand should enter those company models only when funded projects become large enough to affect backlog/earnings. Until then, assigning a separate fusion premium would double-count optionality already embedded in the broader energy thesis.
