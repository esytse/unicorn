# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Scope Boundary

**In scope:** investable enabling technologies, supplier qualification/scarcity, current adjacent-market economics, funded procurement, capacity, margins, catalysts and downside.

**Out of scope unless separately commissioned:** choosing the best fusion physics approach, predicting the date of commercial fusion, valuing private fusion developers, or constructing a thematic fusion basket.

The research objective is supplier capture under uncertainty, not conviction about when fusion succeeds.
