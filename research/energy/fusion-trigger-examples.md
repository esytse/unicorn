# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Trigger Examples

**Would trigger reassessment:**
- SuperPower/Furukawa announces a large funded multi-year HTS order.
- STEP discloses funded tape quantities/supplier awards.
- A second/third major fusion developer qualifies the same listed supplier.
- A competitor commissions large qualified HTS capacity, changing scarcity.
- VAT discloses a material private-fusion order.

**Would not trigger by itself:**
- a fusion startup raises another funding round;
- a developer moves its target power-plant date;
- a politician announces general fusion support;
- a scientific plasma record without supplier economics.
