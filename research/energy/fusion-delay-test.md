# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Delay Test

For every supplier candidate, rerun the thesis assuming meaningful commercial fusion deployment slips to **2045–2055**.

If the investment case collapses because current valuation requires fusion volume before then, the supplier fails the resilience test unless there is a separate near-term contracted demand/catalyst that justifies the position.

This is deliberately harsher than developer timelines and protects the portfolio from paying today for distant optionality.
