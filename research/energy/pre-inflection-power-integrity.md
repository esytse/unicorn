# Pre-inflection AI power-integrity discovery — 25 September 2026

**Backlog:** #259  
**Objective:** search the left-hand side of the pattern observed in Vicor/AP Memory/TAI-TECH:
**qualification → first production → 5–15% revenue → company acceleration → market recognition.**

## Architecture signal

Infineon's March 2026 quad-phase TLVR module integrates power stages, TLVR inductors and decoupling capacitors in a 9×10×5 mm package, exceeds 2 A/mm² and supports both lateral and vertical power delivery. This is important falsification as well as validation: component scarcity is real, but integration can move value from standalone suppliers into modules.

The search therefore prioritises suppliers with **qualified proprietary magnetics/materials or a transition into integrated AI power components**, rather than generic passive exposure.

## Candidate 1 — Shenzhen Sunlord Electronics (SZSE:002138)

### State-change evidence

**Secondary current evidence:** September broker research reports 1H26 revenue of RMB3.86bn (+19.7% y/y) and says TLVR inductor orders are scaling rapidly, while new tantalum-capacitor products are progressing through customer qualification for AI servers/eSSD/ADAS.

**Architecture fit:** the company has both magnetic/passive and power-management exposure, so TLVR plus high-density capacitors could capture multiple BOM layers.

### Why it is interesting

The state change appears earlier than AP Memory's: qualification/order scaling is visible, but the AI power products are not yet obviously dominant in company financials.

### Why it is not promoted yet

Primary company-grade evidence reviewed in this pass is insufficient to quantify:
- TLVR revenue/order value;
- named platform/customer qualification;
- capacity constraint;
- incremental margin;
- current AI-power share of total revenue.

**State: EVIDENCE BUILD.** High information value, but no company underwrite until primary disclosures establish materiality.

## Candidate 2 — Hengdian Group DMEGC Magnetics (SZSE:002056)

### State-change evidence

On 22 Sep the company responded to investors that:
- chip inductors are already in **scaled mass production** and supplied in batches to leading domestic customers;
- overseas customers are being expanded, with some samples already submitted;
- **TLVR magnetic cores are supplied in batches to leading domestic and overseas customers**.

### Why it is interesting

This is exactly the qualification → production transition the discovery framework seeks, and the evidence is unusually current.

### Why it is probably not a Unicorn setup yet

DMEGC is a diversified magnetic materials / photovoltaic / lithium-battery company. 1H26 revenue was reported around RMB12.34bn. Even if TLVR cores grow rapidly, the new bottleneck must become large enough to transform consolidated economics.

**State: EVIDENCE BUILD / COMPARATOR.** Track AI magnetic-component revenue and margin; do not promote on product qualification alone.

## Candidate 3 — Chilisin / Yageo power-magnetics chain

Industry evidence continues to identify TLVR/high-current inductors as a growing AI-server component category, but large diversified passive-component groups have a high materiality hurdle. They are useful competitors for TAI-TECH and smaller candidates rather than automatic Unicorn discoveries.

**State: COMPETITIVE MAP, not promoted.**

## Candidate 4 — integrated module incumbents (Infineon)

Infineon's TDM24745T is direct evidence that TLVR magnetics, decoupling and power stages can be integrated into a compact module. It validates the bottleneck while creating substitution risk for standalone component vendors.

**State: ARCHITECTURE COMPARATOR.**

## What this iteration changes

## Falsifiers to carry forward

- Integrated power modules can internalize magnetics/capacitors and reduce standalone content.
- High unit growth does not imply pricing power; TLVR can become competitive quickly.
- A technically scarce component is not an investment bottleneck if it remains immaterial to consolidated earnings.
- The best early signal is **qualification plus first meaningful financial contribution**, not capacity announcements alone.
