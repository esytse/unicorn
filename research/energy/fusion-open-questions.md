# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Open Questions

These questions are deliberately bounded. New GitHub issues should be created only when answering them can materially affect portfolio allocation, ranking, evidence confidence, catalyst timing/probability or a thesis breaker.

## Immediate

- Furukawa/SuperPower: what are HTS revenue, capacity, margins, market share, qualified competitors and credible 18-month catalysts?
- Does STEP/Tokamak demand translate into funded tape procurement at a scale material to Furukawa?

## Evidence-triggered

- Which public suppliers dominate neutron/radiation-resistant materials, breeder-blanket/fuel-cycle hardware or extreme heat-management systems after qualification rather than merely possessing relevant materials expertise?
- Are any listed remote-handling/robotics suppliers building reusable fusion product platforms rather than one-off engineering contracts?
- Has VAT Group converted ITER expertise into material private-fusion orders?

## Park unless evidence changes

- Developer-level fusion winners.
- Long-dated fusion LCOE forecasts.
- Generic grid/balance-of-plant beneficiaries already represented in the energy thesis.
