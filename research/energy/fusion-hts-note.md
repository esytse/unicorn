# Fusion HTS — Why It Leads the First-Pass Queue

HTS leads the fusion enabling-infrastructure queue because the evidence chain is unusually complete:

1. multiple magnetic-confinement developers explicitly identify high-field HTS magnets as enabling technology;
2. integrated systems require conductor, magnet engineering, cryogenics, current leads, power, controls and quench protection;
3. UK STEP has already awarded a major magnet-system partnership;
4. Furukawa/SuperPower has direct wire-supply and strategic relationship evidence;
5. HTS has plausible non-fusion applications in power, medical/scientific and other industrial systems.

The missing link is **economics**: capacity, qualified competition, pricing, margin, Furukawa segment materiality and catalysts. That is why HTS advances to company research rather than portfolio promotion.
