# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Private Supplier-Market Signal

CFS's external HTS magnet relationships with Realta Fusion and Type One Energy matter because they show that enabling technology can be sold/licensed across developer boundaries rather than remaining purely vertically integrated.

That supports the picks-and-shovels hypothesis. It does **not** prove that upstream public suppliers capture attractive economics; the Furukawa work must test where value accrues between HTS material, tape, cable, integrated magnet manufacturing and developer-owned IP.
