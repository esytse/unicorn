# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Key Numbers

- **$538m:** reported 2025 supply-chain spend by participating private fusion companies in FIA 2026 survey.
- **+24%:** reported year-on-year growth in that spend.
- **$681m:** respondents' projected 2026 spend.
- **75%:** surveyed suppliers reporting capacity investment for fusion in the prior year.
- **£70m:** Tokamak Energy STEP Magnet Systems Partner contract through March 2029.
- **13.7 T:** peak field reported in Tokamak Energy's 2026 Demo4 integrated HTS campaign.
- **Several hundred km:** HTS wire Furukawa/SuperPower were expected to supply for Tokamak Energy ST80-HTS under the 2023 announcement.
- **€60m:** F4E remote-handling framework value across specialist suppliers.

These figures establish industrial activity and technical scale; they do not by themselves establish listed-company earnings materiality.
