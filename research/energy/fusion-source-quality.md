# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Source Quality

The first-pass evidence intentionally leans on programme-primary and company-primary sources for technical qualification/contracts, with the FIA survey for industry breadth and the Financial Times for independent commercialization-risk framing.

Limitations:
- company sources can overstate strategic significance;
- programme requirements do not guarantee supplier economics;
- FIA survey coverage is partial rather than the entire industry;
- developer timelines/cost estimates remain highly uncertain.

Therefore confidence is **Medium** on the bottleneck map and lower on public-company financial capture.
