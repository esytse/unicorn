# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Economic Risk

A supplier can be essential to fusion and still be a poor stock exposure if:

- the parent company is too large for fusion to matter;
- contracts are low-margin engineering work;
- capacity additions require heavy capex/working capital;
- customers vertically integrate;
- pricing falls as the supply chain matures;
- valuation already capitalizes distant fusion growth.

Future work must therefore measure capture, not just necessity.
