# Fusion Supplier Decision Tree

**Direct funded/qualified fusion evidence?**  
No → park.  
Yes → continue.

**Scarce/difficult-to-substitute capability?**  
No → monitor only.  
Yes → continue.

**Strong non-fusion economics if fusion slips?**  
No → require exceptional asymmetry/downside evidence.  
Yes → continue.

**Can fusion/adjacent catalyst affect company economics inside 18 months?**  
No → monitor.  
Yes → Gate-E underwrite.

**Does valuation/downside/marginal portfolio contribution beat alternatives?**  
Only then can the company enter active ranking/portfolio consideration.
