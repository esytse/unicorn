# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Priority Rationale

The broad fusion lane remains **P2** because it can surface new asymmetric suppliers but does not currently represent an immediate portfolio decision.

The Furukawa follow-up should also begin as **P2** unless fresh price/order/earnings evidence creates a direct 18-month decision consequence. It must not displace active P0/P1 work merely because fusion is topical.

This priority follows the repository rule that queue order is subordinate to marginal portfolio contribution.
