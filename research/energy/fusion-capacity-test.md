# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Capacity Test

Scarcity must be tested dynamically.

For HTS and other specialist layers, track:
- current qualified output;
- announced capacity additions and commissioning dates;
- capex per unit of capacity;
- qualification time for new entrants;
- customer prepayments/commitments;
- price/margin response as capacity arrives.

A bottleneck can be technically difficult yet still become a poor investment if supply expands before demand converts.
