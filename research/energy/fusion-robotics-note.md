# Fusion Remote Maintenance / Robotics — First-Pass Note

This is a genuine Physical-AI/robotics overlap, but the first-pass supplier evidence is fragmented across engineering consortia and specialist firms. The investable question is whether a listed company develops a reusable radiation-hardened maintenance platform with repeat economics across fusion and fission/nuclear decommissioning, rather than one-off project engineering.
