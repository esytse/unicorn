# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Questions Answered

**Is fusion industrial enough to research suppliers?** Yes.

**Can we avoid choosing a reactor developer?** Yes in principle, by focusing on enabling suppliers, but architecture specificity still matters.

**Where is evidence strongest today?** HTS/integrated magnet systems, with validated needs also in vacuum, remote maintenance and diagnostics.

**Is there already a public-company winner?** Not established.

**Which new public name deserves deeper work?** Furukawa Electric / SuperPower.

**Does fusion change the portfolio today?** No.
