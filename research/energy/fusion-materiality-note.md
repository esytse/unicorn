# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Materiality Discipline

The 2026 FIA survey reports hundreds of millions of dollars of annual private-fusion supply-chain spending across participating companies. That is meaningful evidence of industrialisation, but it is not automatically meaningful to the earnings of a large listed industrial supplier.

For each public candidate, future work must separate:

- **sector spend** from **supplier addressable spend**;
- addressable spend from **qualified share**;
- qualified share from **recognized revenue**;
- revenue from **incremental margin and free cash flow**;
- long-dated opportunity from **18-month earnings/catalyst relevance**.

A technically critical component can still be an immaterial investment exposure. This distinction is the central gating test for the fusion lane.
