# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Moat Test

A fusion supplier moat should be evidenced through one or more of:

- long qualification cycles;
- proprietary process/IP;
- scarce manufacturing know-how;
- extreme-environment reliability data;
- difficult materials/jointing/integration;
- installed qualified capacity;
- multi-programme customer validation;
- switching costs created by system requalification.

“Advanced technology” or “fusion exposure” alone is not a moat.
