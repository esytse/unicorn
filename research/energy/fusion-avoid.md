# Fusion Enabling Infrastructure — Avoid

- Do not build a long list of companies with the word “fusion” in presentations.
- Do not score private developer timelines as if they were contracted demand.
- Do not promote generic robotics/materials/power companies without direct qualification evidence.
- Do not infer current business exposure from decade-old contracts without checking ownership/product changes.
- Do not let a compelling technology story outrank current portfolio decision work.
- Do not create duplicate company theses when an existing canonical file can be extended.
