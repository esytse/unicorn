## Record one observation per decision and checkpoint

For each `checkpoints/<ID>-03.json` (and 06/12/18), append a matching `performance/<ID>-03.json` using [the template](PERFORMANCE.template.json). Preserve the T0 `candidate_id` as a stable **security** identifier, add a permanent **issuer** identifier and lifecycle ACTIVE/DELISTED/ACQUIRED/RESTRUCTURED. Never exclude delisted, acquired or restructured observations. Document each corporate action with date, source and total-return treatment (cash proceeds, replacement shares, distributions, rights, split and FX where relevant). The supplement and checkpoint become immutable after merge; corrections are linked additional records governed by a separate proposal, never a silent rewrite. CI validates join, source fields, arithmetic and append-only bytes. Human review must verify provenance and actual full-series coverage.

`return_series.status=COMPLETE` requires sourced, same-currency **total-return wealth indices**, including dividends and corporate actions, from the T0 executable observation through the checkpoint actual observation date. Record all trading-session observations for an honest peak-to-trough maximum drawdown; an excerpt with missing intervening troughs is insufficient in production. The script checks positive ordered points, common benchmark start/end, and recalculates raw return, benchmark return, simple benchmark-adjusted return (equity minus benchmark), and maximum drawdown against the checkpoint fields. `source`, `coverage`, `currency`, reinvestment and post-delisting policies must be explicit. It cannot independently certify a provider's coverage, currency conversion or corporate-action accuracy. If a reliable complete adjusted series is missing, set status and all four return/drawdown fields to `DATA-LIMITED` with a reason. Never plug an unadjusted close or assume that a delisted security returned zero.

`events` records the **first** dated, sourced material economic evidence, market-recognition/repricing evidence and thesis breaker, each separately; the derived scorecard reports days from T0. Market recognition needs a predeclared T0 recognition criterion and a recoverable price/valuation source, not a later narrative of why a stock moved. Missing events yield DATA-LIMITED timing, not an invented time to success or a claim that the event never occurs. `economic_capture` and `transformation` use YES/NO/DATA-LIMITED independently from architecture and per-share outcomes; evaluate them against the T0 bridge. Checkpoint narrative remains authoritative for mechanism and caveats, and reviewers must resolve inconsistencies with the supplemental labels. A future event cannot be backfilled as if known at T0.

Opportunity cost uses the same observation window and currency: benchmark adjustment comes from the checkpoint; cash requires sourced return plus matching start/end; a competing Unicorn candidate requires a candidate already registered at the subject's T0, sourced adjusted return, same window, currency and checkpoint horizon. No hypothetical best later winner is chosen retrospectively. Missing comparators remain DATA-LIMITED. `python3 scripts/performance.py` reports raw/benchmark/adjusted return, drawdown, event timing, capture outcomes, lifecycle and opportunity cost for every checkpoint. It returns `[]` before the first prospective observation; that is not performance evidence.

`cohort_metrics` accepts **one methodology version and one horizon**. It keeps every registered candidate, including REJECT and failed/delisted names. Precision is PROMOTE economic converters divided by PROMOTEs with known outcomes; recall is promoted converters divided by all converters in a genuinely complete registered universe; transformation capture rate is promoted transforming suppliers divided by all transforming suppliers in that same complete universe. The function returns DATA-LIMITED for missing outcomes, insufficient denominators (reporting convention at least 10 relevant observations), incomplete universe, or mixed versions/horizons. Completeness must be declared and independently audited from pre-T0 discovery/negative-search records; passing `complete_universe=True` in code alone does not prove coverage. Do not interpret a computed fraction as investment alpha. Benchmark-adjusted total return, drawdown, missing data and duration must also be compared before any shareholder claim.
