# AP Memory — silicon-capacitor power-integrity underwrite

**Ticker:** TWSE:6531  
**Discovery origin:** #253 point-of-load/package-power-integrity scan  
**State:** **POTENTIAL / LATE DISCOVERY — business inflection validated, starting valuation already reflects substantial success**  
**Boundary:** research conclusion only; no brokerage action.

## Thesis path

**AI package power density → power integrity moves inside package → silicon capacitors become qualified scarce complement → AP Memory S-SiCap**

This is the intended original-thesis path: architecture first, company second.

## Evidence of company transformation

**FACT — primary:** AP Memory's Jan–Aug 2026 revenue reached **NT$6.599bn, +109.1% y/y**. July and August revenue were each above NT$1bn and grew **+144.6%** and **+150.8%** y/y.

**FACT — primary:** S-SiCap Gen4 raises capacitance density >50%, targets embedded-substrate packaging and was scheduled for progressive mass production in 2026. AP Memory's S-SiCap Interposer completed customer packaging/reliability validation and entered four-reticle mass production at end-Q3 2025.

**FACT — secondary management reporting:** Q2 S-SiCap revenue was about **NT$638m, +273% y/y**, reaching roughly **27% of Q2 revenue**; IoTRAM remained ~71%. Management commentary reported in September expects S-SiCap to grow several-fold in 2027 and potentially exceed IoTRAM.

**FACT — Q2 financial reporting:** Q2 revenue was NT$2.363bn versus NT$1.329bn a year earlier; net income NT$681m versus a loss a year earlier; EPS NT$4.18.

**INTERPRETATION:** Unlike many early discovery candidates, the new engine is already financially material. Silicon capacitors have crossed from R&D/qualification into a quarter of company revenue while total company revenue has roughly doubled.

## Industry validation / competition

**FACT — Samsung Electro-Mechanics primary disclosure:** Samsung signed a **KRW1.5tn** silicon-capacitor supply contract for 2027–28 with a global large-scale customer and describes strict qualification/high entry barriers.

This validates the market but is also the strongest counter-case: AP Memory is not alone, and larger integrated competitors can invest heavily and bundle capacitors with package substrates/MLCCs.

## 3x / 5x feasibility

Using **NT$160bn** controlled equity:

- 3x → **NT$480bn**
- 5x → **NT$800bn**
- 10x → **NT$1.6tn**

At 30x mature earnings:
- 3x requires **NT$16bn** annual net income;
- 5x **NT$26.7bn**;
- 10x **NT$53.3bn**.

At 40x:
- 3x requires **NT$12bn**;
- 5x **NT$20bn**;
- 10x **NT$40bn**.

Versus ~NT$2.83bn TTM net income, 3x requires roughly **4–6x earnings expansion** while retaining a premium multiple. The operating inflection is exceptional, but the equity has already repriced dramatically.

## Conclusion

AP Memory is one of the clearest examples found so far of the original thesis being **fundamentally correct but discovered after the market recognized the inflection**.

The business passes:
- bottleneck migration;
- difficult qualification;
- company purity/materiality;
- rapid revenue growth;
- operating leverage/profit conversion.

The current equity setup does not yet pass a non-heroic 3x bridge from ~NT$160bn.

**Unicorn state: POTENTIAL / LATE DISCOVERY — 3x unvalidated; 5x/10x unsupported at current evidence/valuation.**

This does not make the discovery useless. It gives us a high-quality pattern to search for **earlier**:
> customer validation → first mass production → new segment reaches 5–15% of revenue → before the market capitalizes the full power-integrity transition.

## What to monitor

Upgrade if:
- S-SiCap 2027 orders/contracted capacity imply a credible path to >NT$10–12bn annual earnings;
- customer/platform diversification materially reduces concentration;
- new manufacturing economics support very high incremental margins;
- valuation compresses materially while the S-SiCap trajectory remains intact.

Downgrade if:
- Samsung/other competitors compress pricing;
- S-SiCap growth slows after current projects;
- capacity/capital requirements erode incremental returns;
- customer qualification fails to broaden.
