## Snapshot

**FACT:** Onto Innovation (NYSE: ONTO) provides inspection, metrology and lithography/process-control systems used across advanced nodes and advanced packaging. Its AI-memory exposure includes HBM, 2.5D logic packaging, chiplets, panel-level packaging and related yield-control steps.

**FACT:** A leading HBM manufacturer selected Onto's Dragonfly G5 for HBM4 after onsite competitive evaluation, with double-digit commitments for both Dragonfly G5 and 3Di systems. Onto also disclosed a >$240m volume-purchase agreement with a leading HBM manufacturer extending through 2027.

**FACT:** Q2-26 revenue was a record $343.1m, +35.3% y/y, with record advanced-node and advanced-packaging revenue. Backlog exceeded $1bn for the first time. Non-GAAP gross margin was 57.0% and non-GAAP operating margin 30.0%.

## Why it may matter

**HYPOTHESIS:** Advanced packaging creates an architecture-resilient process-control bottleneck because larger packages, denser interconnects, HBM stacking and hybrid bonding all raise the economic value of detecting defects and controlling dimensions before expensive integration steps.

**CONCLUSION:** Onto converts the validated 4.7/5 advanced-packaging/process-control bottleneck into strong financial capture with unusually good qualification evidence and attractive operating economics. **Investment Capture Score: 4.0/5.** Status moves from `Investigating` to `Watch`. Its main constraint is that the stock has already rerated to a ~$13bn market cap, while competition remains strong.

## Evidence

### HBM4 qualification

**FACT:** In March 2026 Onto said a leading HBM manufacturer completed evaluation and selected Dragonfly G5 for HBM4 2D inspection. The customer committed to double-digit numbers of Dragonfly G5 and 3Di systems, with the new platforms displacing a previous tool of record.

**FACT:** Onto's 2025 year-end results disclosed a new volume-purchase agreement estimated above $240m with a leading HBM manufacturer for Dragonfly 2D inspection and 3D bump metrology through 2027.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is stronger evidence than generic AI exposure because it shows production qualification, competitive displacement and multi-year spend.

### Architecture resilience

**FACT:** Dragonfly G5 targets defects down to about 150nm and is designed for multiple future process generations. Onto also markets Firefly for advanced IC substrates and panel-level packaging and 3Di for 3D metrology.

**INTERPRETATION:** Onto is attractive because the process-control requirement survives changes from current micro-bump/RDL packaging toward panel-level and hybrid-bond approaches, although the exact tool mix can change.

### Financial capture

**FACT:** Q2-26 revenue reached $343.1m, +35.3% y/y. GAAP gross margin was 53.4%; non-GAAP gross margin 57.0%; non-GAAP operating margin 30.0%.

**FACT:** Six-month revenue was $635.1m, +22.1% y/y. Q3 guidance was $380m–$400m revenue with non-GAAP operating margin 31.5%–32.5%.

**FACT:** Backlog exceeded $1bn for the first time, and management said customer multi-year investment plans support demand into 2027.

## Evidence against the thesis

- KLA, Camtek and Applied Materials can contest high-value inspection/metrology steps.
- A large HBM volume agreement increases customer concentration and can make revenue lumpy.
- Semiconductor-capex pull-forward can inflate backlog during unusually strong AI build-outs.
- Some inspection steps can consolidate as process yields mature.
- The market cap has risen sharply, reducing asymmetry.
- Onto also has non-AP businesses, so the HBM thesis does not flow one-for-one into total earnings.

## Catalysts

- conversion of the >$240m HBM VPA and >$1bn backlog into 2027 revenue;
- additional HBM4/HBM4E process-of-record wins;
- Dragonfly/3Di share gains versus Camtek/KLA/Applied;
- panel-level packaging and hybrid-bond wins that prove architecture durability;
- sustained 30%+ non-GAAP operating margin.

## Thesis breakers

1. The leading HBM customer's follow-on orders slow materially after the current VPA.
2. Dragonfly G5 loses process-of-record share to Camtek/KLA/Applied.
3. Advanced-packaging growth falls below broader WFE growth despite rising AI packaging complexity.
4. Non-GAAP gross margin falls materially below the mid-50s as AP mix increases.
5. Valuation remains elevated while backlog/revenue growth normalises sharply.

## Open questions

1. What exact percentage of revenue is HBM versus 2.5D logic versus other advanced packaging?
2. How concentrated is the >$1bn backlog by customer?
3. What service/recurring revenue is attached to the installed base?
4. How much multi-tool content does Onto win per HBM/2.5D line relative to Camtek?
5. Does panel-level packaging create incremental Firefly/Dragonfly content or substitute existing tools?
