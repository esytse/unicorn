## Snapshot

**FACT:** Montage Technology (SSE: 688008; HKEX: 6809) is a fabless semiconductor company whose core franchise is memory-interface silicon for server and client DIMMs. Its portfolio spans DDR5 RCD, MRCD/MDB, CKD, PMIC/SPD/temperature-sensor products, PCIe/CXL retimers and CXL memory-expander controllers.

**FACT:** Montage's 2025 annual report reported RMB5.139bn of interconnect-chip sales, up 53.4% year over year, with 65.6% gross margin. Its filing cites Frost & Sullivan data putting Montage at 36.8% of the global memory-interconnect-chip market by 2024 revenue; the top three vendors represented 93.4%.

**FACT:** For 1H26 the company reported RMB3.335bn revenue, up 26.7% year over year, and RMB1.997bn attributable net profit, up 72.3%. Revenue less reported cost of sales implies a gross margin of roughly 65%, consistent with the economics of the interconnect franchise.

## Why it may matter

**HYPOTHESIS:** As DDR5 speeds rise and MRDIMM becomes more important, specialist interface silicon can increase content per memory module while remaining difficult to substitute because it must meet JEDEC specifications and qualify across DRAM vendors, CPU platforms and server OEMs.

**CONCLUSION:** The company strongly captures the validated 4.4/5 interface bottleneck, but the shares already price in substantial growth. **Investment Capture Score: 3.8/5.** Montage moves from `Investigating` to `Watch`. The limiting factor is valuation/size rather than technical franchise quality.

## Evidence

### Core franchise and concentration

**FACT:** Montage's 2025 filing says the global memory-interconnect market was highly concentrated and that Montage ranked first by 2024 revenue share at 36.8%.

**FACT:** The same filing says Montage is one of three major suppliers capable of providing full-spectrum DDR2-to-DDR5 memory-interface solutions and one of two companies able to supply both Gen 1 and Gen 2 DDR5 MRCD/MDB products.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is a real oligopoly with meaningful qualification/IP barriers, not a generic semiconductor exposure.

### MRDIMM content

**FACT:** Rambus's current MRDIMM 12,800 architecture uses one MRCD plus ten MDB chips per module. Montage markets the same core categories and supports MRDIMM/MCRDIMM platforms.

**FACT:** Renesas announced Gen 3 MRDIMM chipsets up to 16,000 MT/s in July 2026, confirming both the bandwidth trajectory and strong competition.

**INTERPRETATION:** MRDIMM can create substantial content-per-module growth, but Montage must keep pace with Rambus and Renesas rather than merely benefit from market growth.

### CXL and adjacent products

**FACT:** In July 2026 Montage announced trial production of a CXL 3.2 Memory eXpander Controller supporting PCIe 6.x/CXL 3.2 up to 64 GT/s with dual DDR5-8000 controllers.

**FACT:** The company also demonstrated live multi-host dynamic-capacity memory pooling using its CXL 3.2 silicon.

**INTERPRETATION:** CXL is credible optionality but should not be used to justify the current valuation until merchant revenue becomes material. The broader CXL-controller market includes Astera Labs, Microchip, Marvell and others.

### Financial evidence

**FACT:** 1H26 revenue was RMB3.335bn, +26.7% y/y; attributable net profit was RMB1.997bn, +72.3% y/y; operating cash flow was RMB1.328bn.

**Negative evidence:** Net-profit growth is much faster than revenue growth, so investors should separate operating-business economics from investment income, tax and other non-operating contributors when modelling sustainable earnings.

## Evidence against the thesis

- Rambus and Renesas have competitive DDR5/MRDIMM roadmaps, including 12,800–16,000 MT/s products.
- Higher-speed conventional RDIMM can delay MRDIMM adoption in some platforms.
- AMD is supporting multiple future memory approaches, including MRDIMM and SOCAMM2, so value can migrate between interface architectures.
- CXL is not yet a mandatory memory tier and has a broader merchant supplier base.
- The current valuation embeds substantial growth and margin durability.
- Fabless manufacturing creates foundry/supply-chain and geopolitical exposure.

## Catalysts

- broad JEDEC-standard MRDIMM adoption across Intel and AMD server platforms;
- financial disclosure showing MRCD/MDB/CKD/retimer revenue becoming material;
- CXL MXC moving from trial production to meaningful volume revenue;
- sustained interconnect gross margin near the mid-60s through a memory downcycle;
- valuation compression without deterioration in the franchise.

## Thesis breakers

1. Memory-interconnect share falls materially as Rambus/Renesas gain platforms.
2. MRDIMM adoption remains niche while conventional RDIMM/SOCAMM2 take the growth.
3. Interconnect gross margin falls materially below the historic mid-60s level.
4. New-product revenue remains immaterial despite high R&D spending.
5. Valuation remains >~50x forward earnings while growth normalises toward the low-20% range.

## Open questions

1. What percentage of 2026 revenue is RCD versus MRCD/MDB versus retimers and other new products?
2. What is Montage's share specifically in Gen 2/Gen 3 MRDIMM rather than the broader interface market?
3. How much of 1H26 profit growth is operating versus non-operating?
4. Which AMD platforms will qualify Montage MRDIMM products and on what timing?
5. What is the first credible volume-revenue milestone for CXL MXC?
