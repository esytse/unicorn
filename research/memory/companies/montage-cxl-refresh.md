# Montage Technology — CXL 3.2 controller thesis refresh

**Ticker:** STAR 688008 / HKEX 6809  
**Origin:** #278, parent #261  
**State:** **FAILED / PARKED for Unicorn at current valuation; CXL thesis materially strengthened**  
**Boundary:** research conclusion only; no brokerage action.

## What changed

The old Montage thesis centered on DDR5/MRDIMM interface chips. The 2026 state change is more interesting:

**AI memory-capacity pressure → CXL expansion/pooling → specialized MXC controller → Samsung/SK hynix product integration → 2027 commercialization window.**

### Primary evidence

On 31 Jul Montage announced industry-first trial production of its **CXL 3.2 MXC**:
- PCIe 6.x / CXL 3.2, 64 GT/s;
- dual DDR5 controllers up to 8000 MT/s;
- supports AIC/EDSFF expansion, pooling and tiered memory;
- ships with SDK, analysis and validation tooling;
- **already integrated into next-generation CXL products by Samsung and SK hynix, with initial validation successfully completed.**

This is substantially stronger than a roadmap claim.

## Existing business is already accelerating

**Q1 2026 primary report:**
- revenue RMB1.461bn, +19.5%;
- parent net profit RMB847m, +61.3%;
- interconnect revenue RMB1.417bn, +24.4%;
- combined MRCD/MDB + PCIe Retimer + CKD + CXL MXC revenue RMB269m, +93.8%;
- those four newer products reached **19% of interconnect revenue**;
- interconnect gross margin 71.5%.

**H1 2026:** revenue ~RMB3.335bn (+26.7%) and parent net profit ~RMB1.997bn (+72.3%).

The new-product cluster has already crossed the 5–15% discovery threshold, although CXL itself is not separately disclosed.

## 3x / 5x hurdle

Use RMB273bn equity:

- 3x → **RMB819bn**
- 5x → **RMB1.365tn**
- 10x → **RMB2.73tn**

At 30x earnings:
- 3x needs ~RMB27.3bn NI;
- 5x ~RMB45.5bn;
- 10x ~RMB91bn.

At 40x:
- 3x ~RMB20.5bn;
- 5x ~RMB34.1bn;
- 10x ~RMB68.3bn.

Versus TTM NI ~RMB3.07bn, even 3x requires roughly **6.7–8.9x earnings growth** depending on terminal multiple.

That is not impossible over a long horizon, but it is far beyond what current CXL qualification evidence validates.

## CXL materiality

The correct positive observation is:
- Samsung/SK hynix validation materially reduces technical/adoption uncertainty;
- 2027 commercialization can create a new revenue leg;
- Montage already has high-margin memory-interface economics and customer relationships;
- newer interconnect products are growing rapidly.

The correct negative observation is:
- CXL revenue is not separately disclosed;
- the new-product RMB269m bucket contains four products, so CXL materiality cannot be isolated;
- current valuation already capitalizes substantial AI-memory success;
- Astera/Marvell and proprietary fabrics remain competitive alternatives.

## Unicorn Potential conclusion

**FAILED / PARKED at current valuation for the Unicorn screen.**

This is a stronger company thesis than before but a weaker asymmetric setup.

- **3x:** not validated from current evidence/valuation.
- **5x:** unsupported.
- **10x:** unsupported.

This is a useful thesis-first lesson: discovery can correctly identify a major bottleneck and a real winner **after the equity has already moved beyond the desired asymmetry window**.

## Reopen trigger

Reopen Unicorn status only if:
- CXL controller revenue becomes separately material and implies a much larger earnings bridge than currently visible;
- a major valuation reset occurs while Samsung/SK hynix/other qualifications remain intact;
- CXL controller socket share becomes dominant with durable pricing power;
- another new interconnect engine materially expands the earnings ceiling.

Otherwise keep Montage as a high-quality architecture/company comparator rather than force it into the candidate funnel.
