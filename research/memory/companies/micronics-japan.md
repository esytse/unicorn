# Micronics Japan (MJC)

**Research stream:** AI memory — HBM / memory probe cards  
**Ticker:** TSE 6871  
**Status:** Investigating  
**Gate-E portfolio role:** Watch / correlated HBM quality candidate  
**Confidence:** Medium-High on operating / memory-probe capture; Medium-Low on current March-2028 deployability  
**Last substantive update:** 2026-09-13

## Executive conclusion

**INTERPRETATION:** Micronics Japan remains the **strongest operating/technical franchise** in the Japanese memory-probe-card comparison, but the current 18-month Gate-E underwrite does **not** support a research-level `ACTION` at the latest verified **11 September 2026 close of ¥12,680**.

The quality case is unusually strong:

- H1 2026 sales were **¥49.206bn (+48.6% y/y)** and operating profit **¥15.557bn (+105.5%)**, an operating margin of about **31.6%**;
- full-year guidance was raised to **¥103.8bn revenue / ¥31.4bn operating profit / ¥23.0bn net income / ¥593.31 EPS**;
- HBM is explicitly cited by management as a driver of DRAM probe-card demand, and MJC is developing technology for next-generation HBM;
- probe cards were about **97.7% of FY2025 sales**, giving unusually high operating sensitivity to memory probe-card demand;
- H1 operating cash flow remained positive through heavy investment, with a simple OCF-minus-capex proxy around **¥4.7bn**.

The main Gate-E constraints are valuation, concentration and correlation:

- FY2025 Samsung Electronics plus Micron Memory Taiwan represented roughly **71% of group sales**;
- public evidence still does not quantify MJC's HBM4/HBM4E production share by customer or generation;
- the latest verified close implies roughly **21x guided FY2026 EPS**, so much of the current HBM earnings inflection is already capitalized;
- a plausible base case does not approach the portfolio's 2x objective from today's price; the first scenario that produces roughly 60%+ remaining-window upside requires materially stronger HBM share/earnings evidence;
- MJC adds more memory-cycle / HBM correlation to JEM and SUSS, so it must offer clearly superior asymmetry before it displaces tactical cash or an independent return engine.

**Current Gate-E decision:** **REASSESS / WAIT — not ACTION.** MJC should remain the quality benchmark and live event/price monitor. A price-led reassessment becomes materially more attractive around **¥10,000–10,800** while the operating/HBM thesis remains intact. Evidence-led reassessment can occur above that range if credible HBM4/HBM4E production/share evidence, customer diversification and cash conversion raise the March-2028 upside case enough to justify the correlation and concentration risk.

### Why MJC is not preferred to JEM at the current price

**FACT:** MJC has stronger direct HBM evidence, higher probe-card purity, stronger current cash conversion and a better internally supported capacity profile than JEM.

**FACT:** JEM is materially smaller and cheaper on current guided earnings, while its principal evidence gap is exact HBM4/HBM4E production share and cash conversion.

**INTERPRETATION:** At equal valuation, MJC would be the preferred operating business. Under the March-2028 mandate, however, **JEM currently offers greater stock-level asymmetry**, while MJC's larger market value and higher starting multiple require more earnings proof. MJC also does not solve the portfolio's independent-return-engine gap because it is correlated with the same HBM/memory cycle.

### SUSS comparison

**INTERPRETATION:** SUSS and MJC express different parts of the HBM stack—advanced backend bonding versus wafer-level probe/testing—but both depend on continued HBM capacity and complexity growth. MJC currently has stronger operating margins and cleaner direct earnings conversion; SUSS has more visible 2027 backlog and potentially greater rerating sensitivity if HBM process-of-record evidence improves. At current governed conditions, neither justifies forcing capital: JEM remains the more asymmetric memory candidate, while tactical cash / independent sleeves retain higher marginal portfolio value.

## 1. What the company sells

**FACT:** Micronics Japan develops probe cards and semiconductor test equipment.

**FACT:** In FY2025, the probe-card business generated approximately **¥68.525bn**, around **97.7% of group sales**.

**FACT:** MJC describes itself as holding the world's leading / top share in memory probe cards.

**INTERPRETATION:** MJC is unusually close to a pure listed exposure to wafer-level memory probe-card demand. If HBM test complexity increases content and qualification value, the effect can flow directly into group economics rather than being diluted across a broad equipment portfolio.

## 2. Technology and HBM relevance

### U-Probe platform

**FACT:** MJC's proprietary U-Probe combines MEMS micro-cantilever probe technology with thin-film multilayer wiring.

**FACT:** MJC positions the platform for DRAM and Flash memory and supports large-area / one-touchdown testing on 12-inch wafers.

**FACT:** Published product information describes configurations around **2,500 DUT and roughly 200,000 pins on one card**, illustrating the extreme parallelism required in advanced memory wafer testing.

**INTERPRETATION:** Increasing HBM I/O complexity, die value and known-good-die requirements should raise the value of high-parallelism, stable-contact probe technology. The defensible layer is not simply pin count: it includes MEMS process control, wiring density, planarity/contact repeatability, thermal behavior, qualification and repair/replacement know-how.

### HBM evidence

**FACT:** Management says strong HBM demand drove DRAM probe-card sales and expanded Aomori production capacity contributed to record probe-card sales.

**FACT:** MJC sustainability / technology materials say the company is developing new technologies for **next-generation HBM** and expanding production capability in Aomori and South Korea.

**FACT:** MJC and Advantest announced a strategic partnership in February 2025 to develop next-generation semiconductor test solutions; Advantest also invested in MJC.

**INTERPRETATION:** The combination of direct HBM demand commentary, memory-probe purity and strategic test-system collaboration is stronger operating evidence than a generic AI linkage. The unresolved Gate-E proof is exact HBM4/HBM4E generation/customer production share.

## 4. Customer concentration — central Gate-E risk

**FACT:** FY2025 major-customer disclosures show:

- **Samsung Electronics:** approximately **¥35.007bn**, about **49.9%** of group sales;
- **Micron Memory Taiwan:** approximately **¥14.990bn**, about **21.4%**.

Together these two customers represented roughly **71% of FY2025 group revenue**.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is simultaneously strong qualification evidence and the largest company-specific risk. A share loss or sourcing/architecture change at Samsung can materially change group earnings even if HBM demand remains healthy.

**OPEN QUESTION:** How much of Samsung and Micron revenue is specifically HBM, and what production share does MJC hold by HBM generation?

## 5. Capacity, capex and cash conversion

**FACT:** MJC's FV26 plan contemplated approximately **¥48bn of capital expenditure** and **¥22bn of R&D** over 2023–2026.

**FACT:** Additional Aomori capacity is already contributing to sales, rather than being entirely post-horizon optionality.

**FACT:** H1 2026 operating cash flow was approximately **¥12.17bn** and capital expenditure approximately **¥7.65bn**, leaving a simple H1 free-cash-flow proxy around **¥4.66bn**.

**FACT:** Working capital expanded: receivables rose by roughly **¥4.4bn** and inventory by roughly **¥2.5bn**, with work-in-process a large inventory component.

**INTERPRETATION:** Cash conversion is a meaningful quality advantage versus JEM's current expansion profile, but H1 FCF is not enough evidence to assume structurally high FCF through a memory-cycle reversal. Working-capital and capex normalization remain key monitoring variables.

## 7. Transaction rules — research level only

### Entry / reassessment

Two paths can make MJC deployable; neither authorizes brokerage execution.

**Price-led reassessment:**
- share price enters approximately **¥10,000–10,800**;
- FY2026 guidance / the earnings bridge remains intact;
- no material Samsung/Micron share loss or deterioration in HBM evidence;
- margins, cash conversion and balance-sheet evidence remain consistent with the current thesis.

At that point, refresh the underwrite rather than buying mechanically. If the refreshed evidence still supports the upside case, an initial position can become competitive.

**Evidence-led reassessment above the range:**
- credible HBM4/HBM4E production/share evidence materially increases confidence in durable HBM capture; and/or
- customer diversification reduces the Samsung/Micron concentration risk; and/or
- normalized FCF evidence strengthens materially;
- the refreshed March-2028 case supports at least roughly **60% remaining-window upside** with tolerable downside and better marginal portfolio contribution than tactical cash / JEM / independent candidates.

### Sizing if an entry condition is confirmed

- **Initial research size:** approximately **5–7%**.
- **Maximum before stronger diversification/HBM4 proof:** approximately **8–10%**.
- **Maximum after credible HBM4/HBM4E share + customer diversification + durable FCF evidence:** approximately **10–12%**.

The ceiling is lower than a similarly strong independent-return engine because MJC adds memory/HBM correlation to JEM and SUSS.

### Add

Add only when at least one improves without compensating deterioration:

- HBM4/HBM4E production/share evidence;
- customer diversification / lower concentration;
- normalized FCF and working-capital conversion;
- earnings visibility through March 2028;
- valuation / remaining-window asymmetry.

### Trim

Trim/reduce if:

- the share price rerates materially faster than earnings/evidence and documented remaining-window upside falls below competing uses of capital;
- Samsung/Micron concentration rises further without corresponding evidence quality;
- HBM/process evidence weakens while the multiple remains premium;
- the portfolio becomes excessively correlated to the memory/HBM cycle.

### Sell / thesis break

Exit/reject the Gate-E thesis if credible evidence shows any of the following materially:

1. HBM4/HBM4E production share is materially weaker than MJC's legacy DRAM position;
2. Samsung or Micron qualification/share loss materially reduces revenue or margin;
3. current ~30% operating margins prove peak-cycle and normalize materially below the mid-20s without compensating volume/share gains;
4. inventory/receivables and capex absorb cash persistently as demand slows;
5. capacity expansion runs into a memory-capex digestion cycle and returns deteriorate;
6. alternative probe architectures / competitors erode MJC's memory-probe qualification advantage.

## 8. Bottleneck direction and portfolio fit

**Bottleneck direction:** **Strengthening near term / uncertain structurally.** HBM stack value, pin density and known-good-die economics support more demanding wafer-level testing. The principal structural threats are customer multi-sourcing, probe-card capacity additions, architecture change and eventual normalization of HBM capex intensity.

**Best alternative use of capital today:** tactical cash, JEM at its governed price/evidence condition, or independent-return-engine candidates such as Modine/Jinpan when their own conditions improve. MJC's superior business quality does not currently compensate for the starting valuation and portfolio correlation.

**Gate-E alert state on 13 September 2026:** **REASSESS / WAIT — not ACTION.**
