## Snapshot

**FACT:** Weebit Nano is a semiconductor-IP company licensing embedded ReRAM to foundries, IDMs and product companies. Its commercial model is licensing + NRE/technology-transfer revenue followed by royalties when customers reach volume production.

**FACT:** FY26 revenue was **A$15.3m**, up from A$4.4m in FY25, but revenue remained licensing/NRE rather than material recurring royalties. Cash at 30 June 2026 was **A$168.3m** after roughly A$102m of 2026 equity funding. Ordinary shares rose to about **240.2m** at year-end, with additional unlisted options and share rights creating further dilution potential.

**FACT:** Commercial adoption is real. Weebit has qualified foundry platforms at SkyWater and DB HiTek, Tier-1 IDM licences with onsemi and Texas Instruments, multiple product-customer agreements, and **three customer chip designs taped out by July 2026**. At least one product prototype has returned functional silicon. Product qualification after tape-out can take **12–18 months**.

**CONCLUSION:** The key risk has migrated from basic technology viability toward **commercialization speed, royalty economics and dilution**. The decisive Gate-E question is whether meaningful mass-production royalties appear soon enough to alter per-share economics by March 2028.

**Gate-E portfolio role:** **Speculative option / pre-royalty watch**.  
**Current research signal:** **NO ACTION** at the September 2026 reference price.  
**Research watchlist:** remains **Watch**.

## 1. Commercialization waterfall

### Stage 1 — foundry / IDM licensing

**Passed.**

- SkyWater licensed Weebit ReRAM in 2021 and has a qualified 130nm offering.
- DB HiTek licensed the technology in 2023 and completed JEDEC-based qualification on its 130nm BCD process in late 2025 / early 2026.
- onsemi licensed Weebit ReRAM in January 2025 for its 65nm Treo BCD platform. Test chips produced in onsemi's production fab were functional; qualification remained underway in 2026.
- Texas Instruments licensed Weebit ReRAM in December 2025 for integration into advanced process nodes, including IP licensing, technology transfer, design and qualification.

**INTERPRETATION:** Tier-1 IDM adoption materially reduces the probability that Weebit is merely a research-stage technology vendor. It does not yet prove high-volume production economics.

### Stage 2 — product design-in and tape-out

**Passed for multiple customers.**

**FACT:** Two product customers had taped out by May 2026, including Overlord Labs at DB HiTek. By July 2026 Weebit reported **three customer chip designs taped out to date**. One returned prototype was already functional and running software.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is stronger than a licence pipeline because customers have committed engineering effort and silicon. The remaining steps are qualification, product launch, sustained wafer starts and royalty collection.

### Stage 3 — qualification

**In progress.**

- DB HiTek platform qualification is complete.
- onsemi qualification was targeted for completion during 2026.
- product-level qualification after tape-out can take 12–18 months.
- TI technology transfer / qualification remains a multi-step program.

### Stage 4 — mass production

**Not yet demonstrated in reviewed public evidence.**

The company has said it hopes its first product customer reaches mass production in **CY27**.

### Stage 5 — recurring royalties

**Not yet demonstrated materially.**

This remains the investment-critical milestone. The March-2028 portfolio case should not capitalize a broad TAM before this stage is visible.

## 2. Why ReRAM may matter

**FACT:** Embedded flash becomes harder and more expensive to integrate at smaller process nodes. Weebit's ReRAM is a back-end-of-line memory technology using fab-compatible materials, which can reduce integration complexity relative to embedded flash.

**FACT:** Weebit has demonstrated technology at advanced nodes including 28nm and 22nm FD-SOI development silicon, while currently qualified public foundry offerings remain at 130nm. TI and onsemi provide a path into more advanced industrial process platforms.

**FACT:** Weebit was selected for a Korean government-supported compute-in-memory program with DB HiTek in 2026 and has expanded its Systems and AI activity.

**CONCLUSION:** AI / compute-in-memory is **upside optionality, not the base case**. The nearer commercial case is embedded NVM replacement in analog, power, automotive, industrial, security and embedded-processing applications.

---

## 3. Competition and substitutes

**FACT:** Weebit's FY26 disclosures acknowledge that major foundries including TSMC and UMC have qualified proprietary ReRAM. Other alternatives include MRAM, FRAM, PCM, OTP/MTP and improved flash.

**INTERPRETATION:** Weebit's moat is not ownership of ReRAM as a category. The durable advantage, if it develops, will come from independent IP, qualification know-how, process-transfer recipes, design modules, algorithms and a growing installed base of foundry/IDM/customer programs.

**THESIS RISK:** A large foundry can choose captive ReRAM or another embedded-NVM solution, limiting third-party royalty capture even if ReRAM adoption itself grows.

## 6. March-2028 Gate-E scenarios

These are **scenario boundaries, not forecasts or price targets**. They are designed to test what commercialization outcome must exist by the portfolio horizon.

| Scenario | March-2028 operating state | Illustrative equity-value logic | Approx. per-share boundary* |
|---|---|---|---:|
| **Bear** | no meaningful royalties; tape-outs/qualification slip; revenue remains mostly licences/NRE; cash continues to burn | ~A$320–450m equity | **A$1.25–1.75** |
| **Base** | first production program begins; royalties visible but still small; annualized revenue run-rate roughly A$40–60m | ~A$750m–1.05bn equity | **A$2.9–4.1** |
| **Upside** | at least 2 meaningful production programs; royalty mix becomes material; annualized revenue run-rate roughly A$80–100m | ~A$1.35–1.8bn equity | **A$5.2–7.0** |
| **Stretch** | multiple production programs + strong royalty scaling and/or a commercially credible AI option; annualized revenue run-rate ~A$130–160m+ | ~A$2.2–3.0bn equity | **A$8.5–11.5** |

\*Uses ~255–260m diluted planning shares. The valuation ranges deliberately widen because royalty gross margin and terminal multiple are not yet observable.

### What this means from ~A$3.43

- **Bear:** roughly 50–65% downside.
- **Base:** approximately flat to modest upside.
- **Upside:** roughly 1.5–2.0x.
- **Stretch:** roughly 2.5–3.4x.

**INTERPRETATION:** Weebit has genuine portfolio-level upside asymmetry, but the probability distribution is much wider than JEM or SUSS. A 2x outcome is possible without the most extreme stretch case, but it requires **actual royalty conversion**, not merely further licensing announcements.

## 7. Gate-E action framework

### Current state

**NO ACTION** at ~A$3.4 on current evidence.

The technology/customer bridge is sufficiently de-risked to remain a serious Top-10 candidate, but the current valuation does not provide enough margin for a pre-royalty business under the 18-month mandate.

### Price-led reassessment

A price-led reassessment becomes more interesting around **A$2.5–2.7** only if:

- no material qualification failure appears;
- onsemi/TI programs remain on track;
- at least the existing three customer tape-outs continue toward qualification;
- cash remains sufficient without another unexpectedly large equity raise;
- no new technical reliability issue appears.

This is a **new Gate-E evidence/return zone**, not a reuse of any historical long-duration valuation threshold.

### Evidence-led reassessment

Reassess at any price when one of the following occurs:

1. first meaningful production royalty is disclosed;
2. a product customer enters mass production;
3. a second production customer is credibly scheduled inside the portfolio window;
4. onsemi qualification completes and a production product/program is identified;
5. TI reaches a material silicon/qualification/production milestone;
6. royalty revenue becomes large enough to model gross-margin and operating-leverage economics;
7. a materially dilutive financing changes the per-share case.

### Entry

A research-level initial position becomes supportable only after either:

- the price-led reassessment zone is reached with the commercialization thesis intact; **or**
- a fresh evidence-led underwrite shows a credible path to at least roughly **60% remaining-window upside** with acceptable dilution and a clearly defined downside case.

### Research sizing if confirmed

- **Initial:** ~3–4% of the £40k research portfolio.
- **Maximum before proven recurring royalties:** ~5%.
- **Maximum after meaningful recurring royalties + repeat production adoption:** ~7–8%.

The smaller size versus JEM/SUSS reflects binary commercialization and dilution risk.

### Add

Add only when production/royalty evidence improves faster than valuation or dilution risk.

### Trim

Trim if price rerates sharply on licensing/tape-out announcements without corresponding production/royalty evidence, or if the position becomes too large relative to the still-binary commercial proof.

### Sell / avoid

Reduce or reject if mass-production timing slips beyond the March-2028 relevance window, another large financing materially erodes per-share upside, or product qualification fails.

## 9. Thesis breakers

1. No meaningful production royalties by early 2028 despite multiple 2026 tape-outs.
2. onsemi or TI qualification / production programs stall materially.
3. Product-customer tape-outs fail qualification or never enter volume production.
4. Captive foundry ReRAM / MRAM / another NVM wins the relevant process slots and limits independent-IP adoption.
5. Another large equity raise occurs without a commensurate improvement in per-share commercialization value.
6. Stock-based compensation / dilution remains large relative to new recurring revenue.
7. A technical reliability, endurance, yield or manufacturability problem appears at a production customer.
8. AI compute-in-memory remains research-only while valuation increasingly assumes a material AI platform.
