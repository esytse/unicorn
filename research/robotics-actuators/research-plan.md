## Gate-B evidence standard

A company needs direct evidence for several of:

- named production/customer relationship;
- actual mass production or signed multi-year supply;
- component qualification / market share / process advantage;
- revenue or backlog sensitivity;
- attractive/defensible gross or operating margins;
- FCF / working-capital quality;
- manageable capex;
- installed-base/service recurrence;
- architecture resilience;
- enough total-company exposure for the bottleneck to matter.

Do not promote on conference booths, anonymous supply-chain rumors or top-down humanoid forecasts.

---

## Gate-B scorecard

Score 1–5 across:

- bottleneck linkage;
- production/customer proof;
- share / qualification moat;
- architecture durability;
- total-company sensitivity;
- pricing / margins;
- service / recurrence;
- FCF / capital intensity;
- customer concentration;
- competitive / vertical-integration risk.

A high Gate-B score is not yet a buy signal.

## Re-open Gate A only on a structural trigger

- major OEM adopts materially new joint architecture;
- production lifetime/yield data changes the qualification hierarchy;
- new screw/reducer manufacturing process changes supply elasticity;
- joint-module interfaces standardize enough to change switching costs;
- new production evidence makes a currently rejected/common component structurally scarce.
