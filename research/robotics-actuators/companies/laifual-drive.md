## Snapshot

**FACT:** Zhejiang Laifual Drive is a Chinese precision-transmission manufacturer whose core products are harmonic reducers and related precision components. It also sells joint modules, robotic arms and automated workstations, giving it potential to move upward from a component supplier toward integrated motion modules.

**FACT:** H1 2026 revenue was **RMB142.2m, +80.1% y/y**. Harmonic reducers / precision components contributed **RMB119.4m**, reducer shipments reached **239.5k units, +112%**, and reducer gross margin rose to **29.6% from 20.7%**. **FACT:** H1 designed capacity was 270k units against 256.9k production, about **95.1% utilization**. By July 2026 designed capacity had reached 64.8k units/month, approximately 778k annualized. Management says humanoid customers are progressing from validation toward **bulk procurement**.

**FACT:** Top-five customers represented **55.8%** of H1 revenue and the largest customer 17.2%. This is meaningful concentration despite rapid growth.

**CONCLUSION:** Laifual has crossed from a prospectus-era humanoid narrative into **real operating capture**: volume, small-reducer mix, unit cost, utilization, gross margin and adjusted profitability are all moving in the right direction. The remaining Gate-E problem is whether those gains convert into sufficient cash earnings per share by March 2028 without price competition, architecture substitution or capacity-driven capital needs absorbing the economics.

## 1. What the company actually sells

### Harmonic reducers / precision components

**FACT:** This is the dominant economic engine, representing about 84% of H1 2026 revenue. Harmonic reducers are used in industrial/collaborative robots and increasingly in humanoid joints where compact size, low backlash and high precision matter.

### Joint modules

**FACT:** Laifual also integrates reducers with motors, encoders, bearings and other joint components. This can raise content per robot but also changes the competitive set from reducer specialists toward integrated actuator suppliers.

**INTERPRETATION:** Joint modules are upside only until revenue, margins and repeat customer adoption are disclosed at meaningful scale. Do not assume integration automatically earns higher margins.

### Robotic arms / automated workstations

These diversify downstream applications but should not be confused with the core precision-transmission bottleneck. They can improve customer intimacy and application learning while also adding systems-integration complexity.
