# Humanoid safety / force-control / certification discovery — 25 September 2026

**Backlog:** #266, parent #261  
**Method:** industrial deployment change → safety/force-control constraint → supplier qualification → company materiality.

## World change

Humanoids are moving into real manufacturing environments. As deployment shifts from fenced demos to shared workspaces and contact-rich manipulation, **force control, collision detection, safe torque limitation and functional-safety validation** become deployment constraints rather than optional features.

## Bottleneck map

**joint torque/force sensing → tactile/contact sensing → safety controller/runtime → validated stop/limit behavior → system certification**

A good Unicorn candidate must capture a hard-to-substitute layer **and** be small/pure enough for humanoid adoption to transform company economics.

### ATI Industrial Automation / Novanta

ATI is a leading force/torque sensing reference for robotics, but ownership inside Novanta raises the consolidated-materiality hurdle. This is an architecture comparator rather than a clean pre-inflection small-cap discovery.

### Robotiq / private and other private specialists

Private specialists validate demand but do not enter the listed-equity universe.

### Korean/Japanese component suppliers

The scan found numerous robotics component claims but insufficient primary evidence of a new humanoid-specific qualification → mass-production transition in a small listed force/torque sensor supplier. Do not infer scarcity from generic robotics exposure.

**Result: NEGATIVE SCAN for a new listed force/torque candidate at current evidence quality.**

### Pilz / SICK / large industrial safety vendors

Industrial safety incumbents validate the deployment constraint but are either private/family-owned or too diversified/large for humanoid adoption alone to create the desired company transformation.

## Certification / validation

Certification and safety-validation work is likely to expand as humanoids enter factories, but large testing/certification groups fail the purity/materiality test. No small listed specialist with a sufficiently strong new humanoid qualification signal was established in this pass.

## Key falsifier

Humanoid OEMs can implement force estimation through motor current, encoders and software rather than dedicated force/torque sensors at every joint. Safety value may therefore accrue to **integrated actuator/control stacks** instead of standalone sensor vendors.

This makes the bottleneck real at system level but does not guarantee a standalone sensor equity winner.

## Discovery conclusion

**No new company promoted.**

This is a useful negative result:
- safety/force control is structurally important;
- the listed opportunity currently appears captured by known names, diversified incumbents or private specialists;
- evidence does not support adding a weaker company simply to fill the category;
- integration may move the economic bottleneck back toward actuator/control suppliers.
