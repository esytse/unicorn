## Decision

**REASSESS / WAIT — do not promote to a new Gate-E ACTION at ~US$8.3–8.5.**

FY27 Q2 materially strengthens the QNX business thesis: record revenue, faster EBITDA growth, a raised FY27 outlook, royalty-backlog conversion and the first Alloy Kore win. The investment bottleneck has shifted from proving QNX quality to proving enough valuation asymmetry at BlackBerry's current equity value.

## What changed

- BlackBerry Q2 FY27 revenue: **US$163.3m**, +26% y/y.
- QNX revenue: **US$80.3m**, +27% y/y; gross margin **87%**; adjusted EBITDA **US$29m**, +41% y/y.
- FY27 QNX guidance: **US$315–325m revenue / US$95–105m adjusted EBITDA**.
- Coretura selected Alloy Kore in QNX's largest design win; company disclosure says it adds **>US$100m** to royalty backlog.
- Management says the roughly **US$950m royalty backlog** has begun converting as programmes enter production.
- Secure Communications remains the offset: FY27 segment guidance was reduced and DBNRR is 91%.
- Q2 Licensing revenue of **US$22.1m** is unusually high; management indicated roughly US$6m/quarter for Q3/Q4. Do not annualise Q2.

## Backlog interpretation

Royalty backlog is economically important but is **not contracted cash or guaranteed revenue**. It depends on customer production volumes, timing and programme survival. Treat it as a forward design-win indicator and conversion pool, not receivables.

The stronger evidence is the combination of:
1. backlog growth;
2. development licences/design wins;
3. actual royalty conversion;
4. rising QNX margin.

That sequence supports the thesis that QNX has a high-incremental-margin royalty flywheel.

## Alloy Kore / content-per-machine

The Coretura award validates Alloy Kore as a pre-integrated safety-certified foundation rather than only a collection of RTOS/hypervisor components. This creates a plausible route to greater software content per vehicle/machine.

**Do not model an undisclosed per-vehicle uplift.** Until BlackBerry discloses pricing/content or enough comparable wins exist, capture Alloy Kore through scenario revenue growth and margin rather than invented unit economics.

## BlackBerry SOTP framework

At roughly US$8.3–8.5 and ~587m basic shares, equity value is approximately **US$4.9–5.0bn**. With roughly US$250m net cash/investments, enterprise value is around **US$4.6–4.8bn**.

A useful SOTP must keep three businesses separate:

- **QNX:** premium multiple justified only by sustained growth, royalty conversion and margin expansion.
- **Secure Communications:** lower multiple until retention/growth improves; 91% DBNRR and reduced FY27 guidance prevent a premium growth multiple.
- **Licensing:** high-margin but lumpy; normalize rather than capitalize Q2's exceptional run rate.
