# Physical-AI simulation / validation / fleet-feedback discovery — 25 September 2026

**Backlog:** #272, parent #261  
**Method:** robot deployment scaling → sim-to-real/validation scarcity → productization → company materiality.

## World change

Physical AI cannot be validated like ordinary software. A robot policy must survive geometry, physics, hardware variation and rare real-world conditions.

Fresh 2026 evidence shows the stack becoming explicit:
- NEURA + Dassault connect virtual twins with real-world robot learning.
- ABB RobotStudio HyperReality uses NVIDIA Omniverse to train/validate in physics-accurate twins and close sim-to-real.
- SKAI Intelligence + ABB are validating synthetic-data infrastructure against ABB robotic workstations.
- Wandelbots + Tech Mahindra are taking Physical-AI deployment infrastructure to global manufacturers.
- Grid Dynamics says commercial Physical-AI programs now span humanoid intralogistics, autonomous construction equipment and manufacturing policy-control platforms, with simulation/validation as a core layer.

The bottleneck is:
**train in simulation → validate against physical behavior → deploy → observe fleet → feed failures/edge cases back into simulation → revalidate.**

## Stack

**digital twin / world model → synthetic data → policy training → hardware-in-loop / virtual controller → validation harness → deployment → telemetry/fleet feedback → edge-case replay → continuous improvement**

## New public lead — Grid Dynamics (NASDAQ: GDYN)

### State change

Grid Dynamics' Q2 2026 reporting is unusually relevant:
- AI-related revenue reportedly reached ~30.7% of total revenue.
- Physical-AI work moved from capability-building to first commercial engagements.
- Programs include autonomous driving/loading/excavation for a major construction/mining-equipment manufacturer, humanoid intralogistics for a life-sciences company, and manufacturing policy-control.
- It acquired the Ekumen robotics engineering team in May, adding ROS/simulation/validation depth.
- It describes a multi-year physical-AI program from its first commercial engagement.
- Its platform combines simulation, policy composition, control-test sandboxing, deployment and digital-twin monitoring.
- It announced a strategic partnership with Doosan Robotics, creating a hardware/distribution channel.

This is a genuine **services company → Physical-AI platform/engineering engine** state change.

### Why it is interesting

Unlike giant simulation incumbents, GDYN is small enough for Physical AI to become company-material. The company already reports AI at ~31% of revenue, while Physical-AI remuneration is described as still early. If robotics becomes a meaningful share of that AI engine, the transition could affect total growth/mix.

### Why it is not promoted yet

The evidence does not separately disclose:
- Physical-AI revenue;
- platform/software vs engineering-services revenue;
- gross margin of robotics work;
- recurring licence/usage economics;
- customer concentration;
- backlog attributable to Physical AI.

The core risk is that scarce expertise produces **consulting revenue**, not scalable software economics.

**State: PROMOTE TO BOUNDED COMPANY UNDERWRITE.**

The state change and company materiality are sufficient to justify one bounded pass.

5. Physical-AI revenue can be thematically impressive but financially immaterial.
