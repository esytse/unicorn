# Raspberry Pi Holdings (LSE:RPI)

**Research stream:** Physical AI / edge compute
**Status:** Active challenger; scenario valuation complete, consensus reset pending
**Confidence:** Medium
**Last substantive update:** 2026-09-24

## Snapshot
Raspberry Pi sells low-cost computing platforms, silicon and accessories. Its commercial mix has shifted toward industrial/OEM use; management estimates industrial applications at roughly 75% of end markets in H1 2026.

## Why it may matter
**HYPOTHESIS:** Raspberry Pi may capture a durable layer of edge/physical-AI growth through low-cost compute, a large software/community ecosystem, long product support and OEM design-ins. The investment question is whether those advantages produce enough earnings growth to justify a valuation that has already rerated sharply.

## Evidence
### Product / technology
- Raspberry Pi 5 ecosystem includes AI HAT+ 2 for local LLM/VLM inference.
- Capabilities span silicon IP, boards, software, application engineering and community.
- Cyber Resilience Act preparation has been designed into newer hardware/software.

### Customers / adoption
- H1 shipments 4.2m (+17%); direct shipments +26%; backlog doubled to 2.6m.
- 1,300+ active OEM relationships; industrial applications ~75% of estimated end markets.
- Board-to-Board initiative: >50 C-suite engagements and 26 identified projects, with increasing defence interest.

### Commercial / financial
- FY25 revenue $323.2m; gross profit $77.8m.
- H1 2026 revenue $256.9m (+90%); gross profit $59.4m (+79%); adjusted EBITDA $40.3m (+108%); PBT $19.6m (+216%).
- Gross margin 23% vs 25%; ASP/board +42% to $65.9, largely reflecting memory-cost pass-through.
- Pre-results consensus: FY26 EBITDA $57.8m; FY27 $56.8m. Management now expects FY26 above prior consensus.
- Strategic lower-cost memory inventory materially helped H1 economics; management says exceptional unit economics have moderated.

### Competitive position
- Ecosystem, documentation, availability and OEM support differentiate versus commodity SBC alternatives.
- NVIDIA Jetson/industrial vendors are stronger in high-end edge AI; Raspberry Pi competes on cost, ecosystem and broad embedded deployment.

### Strongest disconfirming evidence
Revenue +90% on units +17% reveals major ASP/memory-cycle distortion. Management says exceptional H1 economics moderated. High-end edge-AI alternatives offer much more compute.

### Valuation sensitivity

The official consensus still reflects 1 Sep estimates: FY26 adjusted EBITDA $57.8m and FY27 $56.8m. H1 alone delivered $40.3m and management now expects FY26 above that old consensus, so the old FY26/FY27 estimates are stale rather than a usable base case.

At 755p, approximate equity value is ~£1.3bn. Using ~175m shares and a working GBP/USD rate of 1.34 gives an equity/enterprise-value scale of roughly $1.75-1.8bn before future inventory financing. The following is a **hurdle matrix, not a target**:

| Normalized FY27/28 EBITDA | Exit EV/EBITDA | Indicative equity-equivalent price* | Read-through |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| $65m | 15x | ~416p | normalization/bear case: today's price is vulnerable |
| $90m | 20x | ~768p | roughly supports the current 755p area |
| $120m | 25x | ~1,279p | requires a strong OEM/edge-AI growth + premium-multiple outcome |

*Mechanical EV-to-equity approximation using ~175m shares and 1.34 GBP/USD; excludes future net debt/cash changes and is deliberately not a price target.

A second near-term hurdle is FY26 EBITDA. $60m/$65m/$70m/$75m implies H2 EBITDA of only $19.7m/$24.7m/$29.7m/$34.7m after H1's $40.3m. That makes a FY26 beat likely on current guidance, but it does **not** solve the FY27 normalization question because memory-driven ASP/unit economics are already moderating.

**Gate-E interpretation:** 755p approximately requires a normalized ~$90m EBITDA business at a ~20x multiple to look conventionally supported. Until revised FY27/FY28 estimates or H2 evidence demonstrate that earnings bridge, RPI remains REASSESS/WATCH rather than ACTION.

### Return bridge
Near-term earnings upside: backlog/OEM volume. Rerating upside requires sustained 2027+ growth after memory normalization.

## Interpretation
The evidence supports a genuine industrial/OEM platform transition, not merely a hobbyist story. It does not yet establish asymmetric equity upside because H1 includes temporary memory advantages and the shares have repriced sharply.

## Risks and substitutes
DRAM/component cycle; gross-margin normalization; inventory funding; NVIDIA Jetson/Advantech/other SBC competition; slow OEM conversion; premium valuation.

## Catalysts
Post-results estimate revisions; H2 backlog conversion; large OEM/defence wins; quantified edge-AI adoption.

## Thesis breakers
Backlog fails to convert; industrial/OEM share stalls; normalized margins fail valuation hurdle; ecosystem fails to retain design wins.
