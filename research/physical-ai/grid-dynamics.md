# Grid Dynamics — Physical-AI productization underwrite

**Ticker:** NASDAQ: GDYN  
**Discovery origin:** #272, parent #261  
**State:** **POTENTIAL / EVIDENCE BUILD — attractive small-cap exposure, but services economics currently dominate**  
**Boundary:** research conclusion only; no brokerage action.

## Thesis

Physical AI creates a scarce integration/validation layer:
**simulation + ROS/control + hardware variability + deployment + telemetry + continuous validation.**

Grid Dynamics is attempting to productize this layer through GAIN while retaining Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) teams for implementation.

The investment question is whether this becomes a repeatable platform-led economic engine or remains high-skill consulting.

## Current company scale

**FACT — Q2 2026:** revenue **$108.2m**, +7% y/y; non-GAAP EBITDA **$14.7m**, 13.6% margin.

**FACT — 30 Jun 2026:** 4,838 personnel.

**FACT — 24 Sep 2026:** GDYN share price **$7.54**.

**Secondary current reference:** market cap around **$625m** at $7.70 on 23 Sep.

Annualizing Q2 revenue mechanically gives ~$433m, so the current equity value is roughly **1.4–1.5x** that revenue run-rate before adjusting for cash/debt. This is not a high-multiple software valuation.

## Evidence of state change

### AI is already company-material

Management reporting indicates AI-related revenue is ~30.7% of total revenue. This means the broader AI transition has already crossed the desired 5–15% discovery threshold.

### Physical AI is earlier

Q2 commentary says:
- first commercial Physical-AI engagements are now active;
- construction/mining autonomy work has expanded into a multi-year program;
- a life-sciences customer is piloting humanoid intralogistics;
- Doosan Robotics partnership creates a global cobot channel;
- Ekumen adds >13 years of robotics engineering / ROS depth;
- the GAIN Physical AI platform includes simulation, policy/control testing, deployment, fleet management, telemetry and continuous model updates.

The company's own product page explicitly combines a platform with **Forward Deployed Engineers embedded in the customer's environment**.

## Productization test

### Evidence for scalable capture

- reusable GAIN platform components;
- simulation/digital-twin workflows;
- fleet telemetry and continuous model updates;
- cloud/ROS/NVIDIA integration;
- hardware-channel partnerships;
- multi-year customer programs.

### Evidence against software-like capture

- company still describes itself as a digital engineering / IT services provider;
- 4,838 personnel against ~$433m annualized revenue underscores human-delivery intensity;
- FDE positioning is explicitly central to the offering;
- Q2 Physical-AI remuneration was described as still to come;
- no separate Physical-AI revenue, ARR, platform bookings or software gross margin is disclosed.

**Interpretation:** the bottleneck is real and GDYN can monetize it, but today's evidence supports a **high-value engineering-services model with reusable IP**, not yet a proven recurring-software model.

## 3x / 5x feasibility

Use **$625m** controlled equity reference:

- 3x → **$1.875bn**
- 5x → **$3.125bn**
- 10x → **$6.25bn**

At a future 20x EBITDA:
- 3x requires ~$94m EBITDA;
- 5x ~$156m;
- 10x ~$313m.

At 15x:
- 3x ~$125m;
- 5x ~$208m;
- 10x ~$417m.

Current Q2 non-GAAP EBITDA annualizes mechanically to ~$59m.

Therefore:
- **3x:** plausible if revenue compounds strongly and EBITDA roughly doubles while the market awards a high-quality engineering/platform multiple.
- **5x:** requires a substantially larger and/or more software-like business; unvalidated.
- **10x:** unsupported.

Another way to view 3x: at a 20% EBITDA margin, ~$94m EBITDA requires roughly **$470m revenue**, close to the current annualized revenue base. But a 20x EBITDA multiple on a services-heavy company would require much stronger growth/quality than currently proven. At a more services-like 12x, $1.875bn requires ~$156m EBITDA; at 18% margin that implies ~$868m revenue.

The key variable is therefore **economic quality/multiple**, not merely thematic demand.

## Why this is interesting

1. **Small starting equity** around $625m.
2. **Existing profitable scale** rather than pre-revenue optionality.
3. **AI already material** at ~31% of revenue.
4. **Physical AI still early**, creating a second possible mix shift.
5. **Valuation does not assume software economics** today.
6. **Clear falsifiable transition:** services → reusable platform/FDE hybrid → recurring/partner-scaled economics.

## Why it can fail

1. Physical AI remains project-based engineering.
2. Hiring must scale roughly with revenue.
3. NVIDIA/ABB/Dassault/robot OEM platforms capture most software value.
4. Customer programs stay bespoke and slow to replicate.
5. FDE economics improve delivery but not gross-margin/recurrence.
6. AI revenue growth elsewhere obscures weak robotics monetization.

## Unicorn Potential conclusion

**POTENTIAL / EVIDENCE BUILD**

- **3x:** economically plausible, not validated.
- **5x:** unvalidated; needs clear platform/recurring economics or very strong services compounding.
- **10x:** unsupported.

This is **not as clean an original-thesis setup as Namuga** because the scarce complement is engineering capability and software integration rather than a qualified physical component, and labour can dilute operating leverage.

It is nevertheless a valuable #261 discovery because the starting valuation is modest and Physical AI is entering commercial programs before separate revenue is visible.

## Promotion triggers

Move toward UNDERWRITE if:
- Physical-AI revenue/bookings become separately disclosed and material;
- GAIN platform revenue/ARR or recurring usage is disclosed;
- robotics partnerships produce repeatable deployments without proportional headcount;
- EBITDA margin expands while Physical-AI mix rises;
- multiple hardware partners distribute the platform;
- a second/third large multi-year Physical-AI program is disclosed.

Park/downgrade if:
- Physical-AI growth requires near-linear headcount;
- no recurring/platform economics emerge;
- robotics remains immaterial after 2027;
- EBITDA margins stagnate despite AI mix growth.
