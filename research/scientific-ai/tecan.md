# Tecan — Scientific-AI Execution / Data-Driven Lab Screen

**Status:** Gate-B company screen  
**Backlog:** #176

**Confidence:** Medium on strategic fit; Low-Medium on AI-specific financial materiality

## Why Tecan is in this screen

The scientific-AI execution work in #171 identified the validated experimental system — physical handling, trusted measurement, orchestration, provenance and exception recovery — as a plausible scarce complement if AI increases the rate at which candidate hypotheses enter wet-lab validation.

Tecan is a useful listed test because its exposure is more automation-pure than Bruker's: liquid handling and robotic work cells, a large installed instrument fleet, scheduling/orchestration software, service/consumables and an instrument-data layer in Introspect.

The question is not whether Tecan uses AI. The question is whether AI-driven science increases lab throughput and whether Tecan can capture that increase through instruments, recurring consumables/service and software/data economics.

## Current operating evidence

**FACT:** H1 2026 group sales were CHF427.5m, down 2.7% in reported Swiss francs but up 3.4% in local currencies. Order entry was CHF444.3m, up 3.0% in local currencies, for 1.04x book-to-bill. Adjusted EBITDA was CHF64.5m and margin 15.1%.

**FACT:** Life Sciences Business sales were CHF179.6m, up 3.1% in local currencies. Recurring revenue from services, consumables and reagents increased to **64.4% of segment sales**, versus 62.1% a year earlier. Liquid-handling instrument revenue recovered in Q2 after prior declines.

**FACT:** Tecan maintained FY2026 guidance for low-single-digit local-currency sales growth and 15.5–16.5% adjusted EBITDA margin. Its 2028 ambition remains CHF1bn sales and 20% adjusted EBITDA margin.

**FACT:** H1 operating cash flow fell to CHF17.0m from CHF60.0m and cash conversion to 36.5%, primarily from working-capital changes. Net liquidity was CHF73.5m at June 2026.

**INTERPRETATION:** Tecan already has a meaningful recurring economic layer around its automation installed base. That is stronger capture evidence than an AI feature alone, but current group growth remains modest and does not establish AI as a material earnings driver.

## Introspect / agentic-AI evidence

**FACT:** Tecan says Introspect continuously reads instrument performance, utilization and quality data and can surface recommended actions. Its API is positioned for error-diagnosis, error-prevention, run-rescue and inventory-management agents.

**FACT:** Tecan reported more than **1,000 instruments connected to Introspect** across large pharma, biotech and clinical laboratories. Published case-study aggregates cite up to 64% higher utilization, 12% lower error rates and 26% lower non-value-added time.

**FACT:** In June 2026 Tecan announced agentic-AI integration using NVIDIA BioNeMo Agent Toolkit. Early access is available; the disclosed use cases remain operational optimization rather than autonomous scientific reasoning.

**FACT:** Tecan's 2026 capital-markets material reports aggregated connected-instrument trends of roughly +2% annual run-success rate, +6% value-added time and +14% active run time versus tip consumption.

**FACT:** Tecan acquired Wako Automation in December 2025, bringing Director scheduling software and related modules in-house, complementing FlowPilot robotic-workcell orchestration.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is a credible closed-loop operational architecture: instruments generate telemetry; software identifies utilization/error patterns; agents can recommend or eventually execute interventions; service can diagnose remotely; improved throughput can drive more consumable use. The feedback loop is closer to fleet operations than to a generic AI add-on.

**LIMITATION:** Tecan does not disclose Introspect revenue, software ARR, attach rate, paid conversion, retention, agentic-AI pricing or incremental orders attributable to AI. The >1,000 connected instruments demonstrate deployment, not yet financial materiality.

## Valuation / 18-month bridge

**FACT:** Tecan closed at CHF218.40 on 22 September 2026. With 12.322m shares outstanding at 30 June, that implies an equity value of roughly **CHF2.69bn** before subsequent buyback changes.

**FACT:** At June 2026 Tecan reported CHF73.5m net liquidity. On that simplified basis, enterprise value is roughly **CHF2.62bn**.

**INTERPRETATION:** Against annualized H1 adjusted EBITDA of ~CHF129m, the simplified EV/EBITDA reference is about **20x**. Against management's 2028 ambition of CHF200m adjusted EBITDA (CHF1bn sales × 20% margin), the same current EV is about **13x** before allowing for cash generation, buybacks or execution risk.

This is not a cheap proof-of-concept valuation. A compelling Gate-E case therefore needs evidence that AI/data-driven automation materially improves the probability or magnitude of the 2028 growth/margin bridge, rather than merely decorating the existing instrument cycle.

No price-led ACTION condition is established from this Gate-B screen.

Tecan currently has better evidence of recurring installed-base economics and fleet telemetry. Bruker currently has the better direct evidence that AI is influencing bookings.

Neither clears Gate-E because neither discloses enough AI-linked revenue, margin or order materiality to show that scientific AI can move consolidated earnings inside the active portfolio window.

## What would promote Tecan

A fresh underwrite is warranted if one or more of these becomes visible:

1. paid Introspect / agentic-AI attach, ARR or recurring software revenue;
2. named customer deployments showing AI-driven automation expansion rather than pilot use;
3. evidence that AI-powered labs are increasing instrument/work-cell orders or consumable pull-through;
4. measurable improvement in Life Sciences growth/margins linked to digital/automation mix;
5. material multi-instrument / third-party orchestration that proves Tecan can capture the workflow layer rather than only its own hardware fleet.

## Thesis breakers / downgrade tests

- agentic AI remains a free feature with no effect on instrument/service/consumable economics;
- customers use vendor-neutral orchestration while instrument telemetry remains siloed;
- AI reduces physical experiment volume rather than increasing validation throughput;
- Life Sciences instrument recovery stalls and recurring mix reflects weak hardware sales rather than stronger attach;
- the 2028 margin bridge depends primarily on restructuring rather than differentiated automation capture.

## Conclusion

**WATCH / evidence-building; no Gate-E promotion and no Top-10 displacement.**

Tecan strengthens the scientific-AI scarce-complement thesis because it demonstrates a real installed automation fleet, high recurring Life Sciences revenue mix, connected telemetry and a credible path from monitoring to agentic intervention. It is a cleaner automation exposure than Bruker.

The missing evidence is financial: AI-specific revenue, paid attach, incremental orders, software economics and consolidated earnings sensitivity are not disclosed. At the current valuation reference, the portfolio does not need to pay ahead of that proof.
