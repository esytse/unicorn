## 2. Japan Electronic Materials — Buy below ~¥5,600

### Existing normalized return

The canonical JEM underwrite uses FY Mar-2027 guided EPS, 10% normalized subsequent EPS growth and a 15x terminal P/E to end-2030. The implied terminal value remains roughly **¥9.1k**.

At the 9 Sep ~¥6,640 reference, that gives only about **7.7% annualized price return**. Existing monitoring zones remain approximately:

- ~10% return: **~¥6,100**;
- ~12% return: **~¥5,640**;
- ~15% return: **~¥5,040**.

### Amagasaki shareholder-capital hurdle

The new Amagasaki factory is approximately **¥12.5bn**, with roughly **¥12.2bn** of public-equity proceeds intended to fund it.

For that new shareholder capital alone to earn:

| Required return on new equity | Incremental annual net income required | Incremental annual revenue required at current ~18.4% guided net margin |
|---:|---:|---:|
| **10%** | ~¥1.22bn | ~¥6.6bn |
| **12%** | ~¥1.46bn | ~¥8.0bn |
| **15%** | ~¥1.83bn | ~¥9.9bn |

**INTERPRETATION:** the factory hurdle is achievable relative to JEM's current ~¥36.4bn revenue base, but not trivial. The investment case increasingly depends on whether Amagasaki is genuinely high-return HBM/advanced-memory capacity rather than ordinary cyclical probe-card volume.

**Decision:** **Buy below ~¥5,600; wait at the current price unless HBM socket/share evidence raises normalized earnings.**

## 4. Weebit Nano — Small speculative position only

### Why a normal earnings valuation is still inappropriate

FY26 revenue is only **A$15.3m** and remains licensing/NRE rather than recurring production royalties. At A$3.43 and ~240.2m FY26 shares, equity value is approximately **A$824m**.

The key question is therefore the commercialization ramp required to make today's option value worthwhile.

### Reverse 2x / 3x hurdle

Assume illustratively:

- five-year horizon;
- **20% cumulative share-count increase** from further dilution;
- mature royalty-platform valuation of **15x revenue**.

Then approximately:

| Desired share-price outcome | Future revenue required | CAGR from FY26 A$15.3m |
|---:|---:|---:|
| **2x** | ~A$132m | **~54%/yr** |
| **3x** | ~A$198m | **~67%/yr** |
| **5x** | ~A$330m | **~85%/yr** |

At a 10x terminal revenue multiple, the required revenues are substantially higher.

**INTERPRETATION:** royalties can create exceptional incremental margins, so the revenue hurdle is not impossible. But first production royalties are essential, and additional licensing announcements alone do not justify a higher capital status.

**Decision:** **Small speculative position only** until first meaningful recurring royalty revenue and repeat production adoption are visible.

## 5. Laifual Drive — Wait despite the operating upgrade

H1 2026 materially improves operating confidence: revenue +80%, reducer shipments +112%, reducer gross margin ~29.6%, adjusted EBITDA positive and humanoid customers moving toward bulk procurement.

The stock-price hurdle is nevertheless much more demanding than the growth headline suggests.

### Reverse 2x hurdle

Using approximately:

- HK$58 share reference;
- ~104.1m shares;
- ~RMB284m H1-annualized revenue base;
- approximate HKD/RMB translation only for the scenario;
- five-year horizon.

For a **2x equity outcome** at a future **30x P/E**:

- at **15% net margin**, required revenue is roughly **RMB2.4bn**, or about **54% annual revenue CAGR**;
- at **12% net margin**, required revenue is roughly **RMB3.1bn**, or about **61% CAGR**.

A 3x outcome requires materially higher growth again.

### Stress-test base case

A deliberately strong illustration of **40% annual revenue growth for five years, 12% mature net margin and 25x terminal P/E** produces a rough value **below the current share price**.

**INTERPRETATION:** Laifual deserves its high unicorn-research rank because the operating base can transform rapidly. It does **not** currently deserve a conventional buy conclusion because the stock already assumes an extraordinary scale-up.

**Decision:** **Wait for evidence / valuation catch-up.** A venture-style small position would be a separate risk choice, not a Gate-D conventional allocation conclusion.

## 6. BlackBerry / QNX — Wait for 24 Sep; provisional zone ~US$6.7

### Pre-Q2 SOTP

Using FY27 guidance midpoints:

- QNX revenue **~US$303.5m**; adjusted EBITDA **~US$80m**;
- Secure Communications revenue **~US$275m**; EBITDA **~US$61m**;
- Licensing EBITDA **~US$25m**;
- corporate/unallocated drag **~US$37m**;
- simple net liquidity **~US$226m**.

A basic SOTP assigning 12x Secure Communications EBITDA, 10x Licensing EBITDA, capitalizing corporate drag at 10x and adding net liquidity leaves approximately **US$3.8bn** of the current ~US$4.6bn equity value attributable to QNX — around **12.5x FY27 QNX revenue**.

### Conservative five-year base

Assume:

- QNX revenue grows **15% annually** for five years;
- terminal QNX value is **10x revenue** rather than today's implied premium;
- non-QNX net SOTP remains roughly **US$0.8bn**.

This gives future BlackBerry equity value of roughly **US$6.9bn**. For that outcome to deliver 12% annualized from today, current equity value would need to be roughly **US$3.9bn**, or around **US$6.7/share** on ~586m shares.

If QNX instead retains a ~12x revenue multiple while growing 15%, today's price can support roughly the 12% hurdle. That dependence on terminal multiple is the problem.

**Decision:** **Wait for 24 Sep results; provisional conservative buy-below zone ~US$6.7.** Refresh immediately after Q2 guidance and any non-auto/robotics disclosure.

## 7. Remaining five — decisions already sufficiently constrained

### Micronics Japan — Buy below ~¥9,300

MJC is the stronger operating probe-card business but the existing normalized model places the ~12% base-return zone around **¥9,270**, versus ~¥12,710 now. Samsung + Micron Taiwan represented ~71% of FY2025 sales, increasing the downside of paying a peak multiple.

**Decision:** **Buy below ~¥9,300 / wait at current price.**

### FORT Robotics — Wait for S-4

At the announced transaction reference the operating business is already valued around **48x 2025 revenue**, and at the current NTWO premium the rough price-scaled multiple is higher. Actual recurring software economics, redemptions, warrants, earnouts and final share count remain unresolved.

**Decision:** **Wait for evidence / transaction.** Do not assign a buy zone until the S-4 allows a fully diluted capitalization and revenue-quality rebuild.

### Centrus Energy — Wait for funded capacity

Customer-prepayment evidence from X-energy and Radiant strengthens the commercial financing path, but the project remains multi-billion-dollar and the current equity value already carries substantial policy/capacity optionality. The existing event-driven base-value framework has an approximate **US$147 12% zone**, below the ~US$185.5 reference.

**Decision:** **Wait for funded-capacity evidence.** Reassess when the full commercial expansion capital stack is visible or valuation enters the existing zone without thesis deterioration.

### Hainan Jinpan — Buy below ~CNY59

AIDC orders/revenue/backlog and overseas orders are accelerating sharply, but named global qualification and operating-cash-flow conversion remain below the evidence quality of the best memory candidates. Existing normalized work places the ~12% zone around **CNY59**, versus ~CNY68 now.

**Decision:** **Buy below ~CNY59 / wait now.**

### Harmonic Drive Systems — Avoid at current valuation

HDS remains an important technical benchmark, but roughly **75x forward P/E** plus increasing China competition and actuator-architecture alternatives leave an unattractive conventional return setup.

**Decision:** **Avoid at current valuation / wait for major price reset or materially larger quantified humanoid profit.**
