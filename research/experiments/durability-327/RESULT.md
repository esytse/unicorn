# #327 durability and shareholder-capture experiment

**Status:** HISTORICAL experiment, 2026-09-25. **Inputs:** frozen [#261 T0 predictions](../../backtests/261/PREDICTIONS.md), [outcomes](../../backtests/261/OUTCOMES.md), [failure ledger](../../backtests/261/FAILURES.md), and underlying dated case packets. **Rule boundary:** no v1 prediction, outcome, ranking, portfolio action or live methodology changed. This is hypothesis generation on a selected historical sample, not a prospective validation or alpha estimate.

## Design and denominators

`python3 scripts/durability_experiment.py` joins all **63 candidate-case rows** (46 development, 17 holdout) by case and candidate and verifies 30 explicit T0/outcome probes across **all 15 cases**. The [probe file](probes.json) quotes the frozen T0 disconfirming evidence and frozen outcome verbatim; the assessment and short lesson are retrospective interpretations. The other 33 candidate-case rows remain in the denominator/audited join but do not receive a factor claim. The probes are selected contrasts, **not** a factor backtest or independent stock sample. Two initial PROMOTEs, both in development and both with later durability impairments, offer no reliable threshold calibration. Holdouts have zero PROMOTEs. No adjusted equity/benchmark/drawdown series exists in #261.

Separate outcomes: architecture demand can strengthen while a merchant supplier fails (D08 Asetek); a company can grow product revenue without funded profit (D03 Akoustis, H02 PacBio); initial profit can later reverse (D01 Applied Optoelectronics, D02 Q Technology, D05 Cognex). None of these alone establishes per-share total return.

## Proposed small v2 durability module, unvalidated

At T0, ask and document three separate questions, with **PASS / CONCERN / DATA-LIMITED** and dated primary evidence rather than a summed score:

1. **Retained company economics:** Are pricing, yield/margin, capacity response, concentration and substitutes consistent with an attributable incremental profit mechanism? A design win alone is insufficient.
2. **Funded conversion:** Can production and capital/working-capital needs plausibly convert into owner free cash without destructive financing? Report missing incremental ROIC and cash separately; do not mechanically veto capex.
3. **Per-share capture:** What ownership, dilution, valuation and benchmark-adjusted return bridge existed at T0, and what would break it? Missing adjusted prices/benchmark means DATA-LIMITED on equity, even with operating success.

These questions would flag known warnings in AAOI/Q Technology/Akoustis/Wolfspeed/PacBio, but **also** flag Qorvo, SK hynix, Ypsomed and Enphase despite later operating conversion. They therefore **fail as a demonstrated discriminating gate** on this corpus. They may structure prospective records under #325; adoption as a promotion threshold requires predeclared independent cases and later checkpoints. Initial economic capture and durability must be scored separately, with no inferred return from either.

## Rejected or unresolved factor rules

- **Reject:** automatic exclusion for high capex, customer concentration, falling margin, or absent isolated 5–15% segment disclosure. Each would catch a failure but also suppress real/missed conversion, and no defensible threshold can be estimated from two development PROMOTEs.
- **Reject:** scoring a theme win as company capture, broad group growth as architecture-specific materiality, or product sales as funded per-share capture.
- **DATA-LIMITED:** incremental ROIC, ASP trajectories, working-capital conversion, competitor capacity additions, acquisition-adjusted group economics, adjusted shareholder returns and drawdowns at comparable T0/3/6/12/18-month windows. The frozen packets do not form a consistently measured panel.
