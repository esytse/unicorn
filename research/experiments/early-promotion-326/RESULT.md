# #326 early-promotion falsification

**Status:** HISTORICAL / exploratory, 2026-09-25. **Inputs:** immutable [#261 predictions](../../backtests/261/PREDICTIONS.md), [outcomes](../../backtests/261/OUTCOMES.md), and [case packets](../../backtests/261/cases/). [The complete 34-row annotation](observations.json) quotes each EVIDENCE-BUILD T0 confirming/disconfirming bridge and the separate post-T0 outcome verbatim. `python3 scripts/early_promotion_experiment.py` verifies the 63-row frozen join and enumerates every affected row. The feature coding was done after outcomes were known; this is a challenge to proposed rules, **not** a blind or independent validation. No frozen label or live classification changes.

## Coding and denominators

There are **2 PROMOTE, 34 EVIDENCE-BUILD and 27 REJECT** candidate-case observations: development **2/25/19**, holdout **0/9/8**. A hypothetical change here retains both existing PROMOTEs and tests additions only from the 34 EVIDENCE-BUILD rows. REJECT comparators remain in the 63-row denominator but are not silently recast as early promotion candidates. Repeated issuers in distinct cases are separate observations, not independent companies. Excluded/contaminated candidates remain excluded.

The seven `YES/NO` T0 feature interpretations in the annotation are deliberately strict; **NO means not established in the frozen T0 packet**, not evidence of absence. Qualification (`Q`) means a named customer/socket or repeat customer sales for the specified engine. Production (`P`) means that engine was shipping or explicitly in mass production. Acceleration (`A`) means a comparable relevant product/order growth observation at T0; broad group growth is insufficient. Capacity (`C`), orders/backlog (`O`), attributable company materiality (`M`) and financial inflection (`F`) are kept distinct. Mixed product buckets are not credited to a narrower architecture. The quote, missing bridge, T0 concentration and valuation/late-stage caution appear on every row. `UNKNOWN` means no admissible valuation or concentration verdict. No post-T0 event supplies a T0 factor.

For example, SK hynix had a named H100 HBM3 mass-production socket (`Q,P`) but no frozen HBM acceleration, group-share/profit or valuation measurement (`A,M,F` unestablished). West had a qualified components portfolio, but no GLP-1-specific T0 socket or materiality (`Q,P` unestablished for this case). Micron had HBM2E shipments, but no named HBM3 socket at T0; its later HBM3E ramp cannot fill that gap. Cognex had logistics customer revenue growth >50% (`Q,P,A`) with lower logistics gross margin and unisolated group mix. PacBio had rising Sequel consumable sales (`Q,P,A`) with 34.9% gross margin and capital risk. Enphase had core microinverter revenue growth and profit (`Q,P,A,M,F`) but an already established engine and unknown warranty-adjusted cash/valuation. These are T0 interpretations, open to reclassification only with cited T0 evidence, not subsequent outcomes.

No rule here establishes pre-recognition equity alpha.

## False negatives and falsification

- **Clear operating miss:** SK hynix HBM3 was frozen EVIDENCE-BUILD despite named H100 mass production, then became >40% of quarterly DRAM sales with strong group earnings. `Q+P` recovers it, but measured acceleration, T0 valuation and adjusted equity performance are absent. Recovering it alone does not justify promotion.
- **Other operating conversion with caveats:** Cognex initial logistics materiality is a plausible development miss, but its specific profit and durability were impaired; Oclaro was a later-stage already-material engine. West later reached 17% of group quarterly GLP-1 sales yet had no GLP-specific T0 socket. Micron later produced material HBM revenue but its T0 HBM3 qualification and incremental profit were unestablished. Sunny, NeoPhotonics and other incumbents retain attribution, timing or market-recognition gaps. Moderna/BioNTech initial conversion followed an unforeseen pandemic shock; T0 commercial production was absent. None is quietly counted as a clean prospective equity win.
- **New adverse cases:** `Q+P` would promote PacBio despite visible gross-margin/funding risk and Enphase despite an established engine and missing warranty-adjusted cash/valuation. It also promotes Sunny despite explicit T0 rerating and Oclaro/Neo at already 47%/57% mix. `Q+P+A` preserves both funded/timing problems while losing SK hynix. `P+A+M` retrieves no early missed engine.

**Prospective comfort test:** Without outcome knowledge, `Q+P` could warrant faster, bounded follow-up on a named production socket. It is not enough to label the supplier an unrecognised, funded, durable shareholder opportunity. A hard financing veto would catch PacBio yet #327 showed capex/financing flags also accompany conversions; a valuation veto cannot be scored consistently here. The candidate rules either add adverse/late names or fail to recover the clear operating miss. **Conclusion: NO ROBUST IMPROVEMENT demonstrated; equity and market-recognition verdict DATA-LIMITED.** Keep v1 as the prospective benchmark. Named production, attributable profit and funded cash are separately recorded hypotheses under the experimental prospective protocol, not a new validated promotion gate.
