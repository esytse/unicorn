# #261 Candidate v2 rules — proposed before holdout opening

**Status:** PROPOSAL ONLY, written 25 September 2026 after D01–D10 outcome review. **Do not apply to H01–H05.** The holdout set runs the exact frozen `SPEC.md` v1. No live Unicorn methodology or portfolio conclusion changes here.

## Proposed changes for a separately registered future test

1. **Split early qualification from funded scale.** Record product qualification, repeat orders, unit gross margin/yield, capex, cash runway and eventual owner FCF as separate gates. Product sales alone never establish shareholder capture (Akoustis, Wolfspeed).
2. **Track durability after promotion.** Predefine quarterly customer/top-account share, ASP, product gross margin, utilisation and replacement architecture signals; evaluate initial conversion and 2–3-year durability independently (Applied Optoelectronics, Q Technology, Cognex).
3. **Add a parallel incumbent-upgrade lane.** An already meaningful product franchise can still gain architecture-specific incremental profit; require identifiable organic mix, margin and group FCF, plus T0 market-recognition test (Skyworks/Qorvo, Cognex, Arista, Vertiv). Keep the 5–15% new-engine lane as a separate hypothesis, not a universal exclusion.
4. **Pre-register attribution levels.** Label company-level, segment-level, product-level and architecture-specific evidence; do not assign all mixed advanced packaging, cloud, cooling or utility revenue to a nominated use case. Acquired business growth needs an organic/ownership bridge (TSMC/Amkor, Modine, Hubbell).
5. **Regime-shock sensitivity.** Report the frozen T0 case and a separately marked scenario for an unforeseen demand discontinuity; never retrofit the scenario into the original prediction (Moderna/BioNTech, D09 AI).
6. **Clean candidate discovery.** Capture search queries, timestamped admissible documents and later-result snippet exposure; exclude contaminated names from scored false negatives, and register bounded negative searches. A new prospective repeat is needed for Besi/Largan.
7. **Pre-arrange equity data.** Before any future T0 freeze, specify listing, adjusted price/dividends/corporate actions, benchmark, interval rules and missingness policy. Do not equate operating conversion with market recognition.

## Falsification criteria for a future v2 study

Pre-register new cases not used to derive these rules; report promotion precision on initial and durable economics, false-negative capture in both lanes, shareholder return versus benchmark and financing-adjusted FCF. Abandon or revise a rule if it improves apparent hits only by adding broad mature incumbents after the fact or relies on unattributable group sales. H01–H05 are **v1** holdouts, not a fair independent test of these proposed v2 changes.
