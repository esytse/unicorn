## Observations by chain link

- **Architecture and bottleneck:** 100G optics and dual cameras developed as expected; advanced packaging, RNA commercial manufacturing, AI infrastructure and grid equipment also strengthened. Theme correctness is weaker evidence than supplier-specific durable profit.
- **Company capture and materiality:** Applied Optoelectronics and Q Technology converted quickly, but reliance on concentrated customers and mix/capacity economics made capture unstable. Cognex logistics, Moderna/BioNTech vaccines and several incumbents delivered substantial later operating growth from EVIDENCE-BUILD/REJECT states. These are possible economic false negatives, conditional on attribution and the extraordinary pandemic demand shock.
- **Migration and integration:** TSMC integrated chiplet packaging internally; Besi (excluded) later secured hybrid-bonding orders. 100G architecture and smartphone-module pricing evolved. Direct-liquid Asetek was an instructive failed supplier while other broad cooling firms grew.
- **Capital and shareholder capture:** Akoustis and Wolfspeed demonstrate that product revenue can rise while negative gross margin, fab underutilisation and financing destroy shareholder value. Acquisitions (Resonant, Catalent, Oclaro) limit later standalone observation.
- **Timing and scope:** Oclaro's 100G revenue was already large at T0, so evidence-build may be late. NVIDIA was explicitly a core-platform comparator, not a forecast of weak shares. D09's late-November 2022 T0 and D07's pre-pandemic T0 expose how sudden demand regimes affect apparent false negatives.

## Answer to the research question

**INTERPRETATION:** v1 generated two early operating inflection picks in this development sample, both with later durability problems. It demonstrated a useful distinction between architecture demand and supplier economics, especially for Asetek, Akoustis and Wolfspeed. It has **not** shown reproducible equity asymmetry or a reliable transformation capture rate: the small selected sample, leaks, uncertain end-market attribution, no price series and possible missed incumbent captures prevent that conclusion. The holdouts must test whether any signal survives without altering v1.

**HYPOTHESIS:** require observable price/mix/yield and cash conversion after qualification, and run a separate incumbent-upgrade track so narrow 5–15% screens do not silently discard material existing suppliers. These hypotheses are written in `V2_PROPOSAL.md` and cannot be applied to the holdouts.
