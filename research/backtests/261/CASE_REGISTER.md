## Pre-registration caveats

- The architecture set itself is historically informed and therefore cannot prove a fully systematic strategy. The test asks whether #261 adds value **conditional on a genuine architecture transition being identified**.
- Cases must not be dropped because they produce no candidate.
- If T0 is too early/late to permit a fair reconstruction, retain the case and report the sensitivity; do not silently move the date.
- H01–H05 are holdouts. Do not perform candidate discovery or outcome reveal until all development predictions are frozen and development outcomes have been reviewed.
