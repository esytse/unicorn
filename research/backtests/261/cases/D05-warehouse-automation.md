# D05 — Warehouse automation scaling

**T0:** 1 July 2019. **Prediction:** FROZEN; dated evidence through T0 only.

## Architecture and scarcity

**FACT:** Cognex's 2018 filing describes retail/e-commerce distribution centres using vision and identification to scan, track and sort goods; logistics revenue grew more than 50%. KION/Dematic reported 2018 Supply Chain Solutions orders up 15.5% to €2.425bn and launched robotic piece picking. Teradyne acquired mobile-robot maker MiR in April 2018. [SRC-BT261-D05-001–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** As warehouses move from isolated conveyors/robots to fleets and high-throughput picking, reliable item identification, navigation, safe motion, installation and software orchestration become constraints. Orders and pilots can precede revenue; integration/deployment cost may absorb the value of commodity sensors and motors. Fleet-scale economics and merchant capture, rather than general e-commerce growth, are the test.

## Listed candidates

### Cognex (NASDAQ: CGNX) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** 2018 logistics customer revenue grew >50%, offsetting weak consumer electronics; group revenue $806.3m rose just 5%. Gross margin fell to 74.4% from 75.6%, primarily due to lower-margin logistics solutions and deployment services. The filing does not quantify logistics absolute revenue/profit. [SRC-BT261-D05-001]

**INTERPRETATION:** Vision/data capture is a strong physical throughput constraint with actual sales, but customer-specific integration costs threaten margin and logistics group materiality is undisclosed. **Disconfirming:** falling mix margin, volatile other end markets. **Missing bridge:** repeat distribution-centre installations -> logistics share and gross profit -> group operating leverage at attractive T0 valuation. **Next change:** reported logistics revenue share, standardised product margins and repeat multi-site orders. **Frozen state: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.**

### KION / Dematic (ETR: KGX) — REJECT for early supplier promotion / Medium

**FACT:** Its 2018 Supply Chain Solutions order intake was €2.425bn, up 15.5%, and the existing business integrates shuttles, picking, software and systems; adjusted EBIT margin was 8.8% versus 9.4% a year earlier. [SRC-BT261-D05-002]

**INTERPRETATION:** The integrator may capture the system budget but was already a large, established warehouse player; project timing, working capital and service burden weaken a scarce-component/early materiality case. **Disconfirming:** order-to-revenue lag and margin contraction. **Missing bridge:** incremental fleet/picking projects -> durable group profit beyond existing Dematic -> unrecognised equity. **Next change:** conversion of orders to cash and margin. **Frozen state: REJECT / Medium.**

### Teradyne / MiR (NASDAQ: TER) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low

**FACT:** Teradyne bought MiR in April 2018 for approximately $198m (cash and contingent fair value). MiR disclosed $12m 2017 revenue, more than triple 2016, and $5m Q1 2018 sales; those are pre-acquisition figures. Its 2018 industrial automation segment contains Universal Robots, MiR and Energid and cannot isolate warehouse robots. [SRC-BT261-D05-003–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** Autonomous transport is a fleet-scaling option but MiR's small sales are not proof of high-volume warehouse deployment or group transformation at a $2bn-plus test-equipment parent. **Disconfirming:** acquisition premium and manufacturing-heavy applications rather than named warehouse fleet economics. **Missing bridge:** multi-site logistics fleet orders -> MiR margins -> Teradyne materiality. **Next change:** distinct MiR/logistics sales and operating contribution. **Frozen state: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.**

### Zebra Technologies (NASDAQ: ZBRA) — REJECT for early D05 promotion / Medium

**FACT:** Its 2018 Enterprise Visibility & Mobility revenue was $2.795bn, up 15.8% reported/13.4% organic, spanning mobile computing, capture and many end markets. No warehouse-robotics-specific incremental profit was isolated. [SRC-BT261-D05-005]

**INTERPRETATION:** Essential data-capture infrastructure, but an established broad vendor with attribution and under-recognition unresolved. **Disconfirming:** hardware competition and end-market breadth. **Missing bridge:** warehouse fleet deployments -> incremental recurring capture/group profit. **Next change:** isolated warehouse software/services attach and margin. **Frozen state: REJECT / Medium.**

## Negative search and boundary

Searched robotic transport, item vision/ID, integrated systems, motion/safety components and private automation platforms. No listed niche actuator/safety part with verifiable <=T0 warehouse fleet production and financial materiality was recovered. Amazon's internal robot network and privately held system makers are architecture comparators, not public candidates. Later search results surfaced 2020+ Teradyne, Cognex and newer robotic-platform filings; excluded entirely from states. All four screened companies were listed at T0. A no-pick is possible; T0 prices/valuations were not recovered reliably.

## Frozen prediction

**No PROMOTE.** Cognex's logistics revenue signal is strongest, but margins moved adversely and its incremental group-profit bridge is missing. KION/Dematic and Zebra are already material incumbents; MiR is small within Teradyne and acquisition-confounded. **HYPOTHESIS:** integration, logistics vision and/or mobile fleets will scale, but the merchant margin may accrue differently from the most visible robot hardware. Outcome research follows the merged prediction, not this draft.
