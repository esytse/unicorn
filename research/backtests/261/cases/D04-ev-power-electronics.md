# D04 — EV high-efficiency power electronics

**T0:** 2 January 2019  
**Prediction:** FROZEN before outcome research. Only publications dated on or before T0 support the decisions below.

## Architecture and bottleneck at T0

**FACT:** Cree described SiC materials and power devices used in electric vehicles, inverters and charging-related applications. Its February 2018 investor presentation compared SiC efficiency, size and weight with silicon and proposed expansion of materials and devices. Infineon's November 2018 annual report described SiC MOSFET/module capability, high-volume manufacturing cost hurdles and a long-term Cree wafer-supply agreement. [SRC-BT261-D04-001–003]

**INTERPRETATION:** Increasing inverter switching efficiency and power density, including at higher vehicle voltages, creates demand for qualified SiC crystal growth, low-defect 150-mm wafers, device fabrication and automotive-grade module packaging. The material/yield bottleneck may be upstream of the branded inverter. Conventional silicon IGBTs remain an economically relevant substitute. This is an emerging transition, not proof that all EVs will adopt SiC.

## Contemporaneously listed supplier search

### Cree / Wolfspeed (NASDAQ: CREE) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** Wolfspeed FY2018 revenue was $328.6m, 22% of Cree's group revenue, versus $221.2m/15% in FY2017, with 48% segment gross margin. In September-quarter 2018 revenue reached $127.4m/31.2% of group revenue, up 93% year on year, but the segment combined SiC material, power devices and RF, and the March 2018 Infineon RF acquisition confounds organic growth. On 16 October 2018 Cree announced a >$85m long-term 150-mm SiC wafer-supply agreement with an unnamed leading power-device company. [SRC-BT261-D04-001, 004–005]

**INTERPRETATION:** Real contracted wafer demand, process capacity and a rapidly material segment make this the strongest upstream candidate. The specific EV SiC revenue/organic increment and valuation recognition are not isolated; at 31% segment mix this is already beyond the preferred early 5–15% discovery window. It does not earn a clean pre-inflection PROMOTE.

**Disconfirmation:** acquisition-driven RF revenue, multi-end-market material demand, capital intensity, defect/yield and customer concentration. **Missing bridge:** contracted SiC wafers -> EV-qualified volume at yields/returns -> incremental consolidated profit and FCF beyond RF/LED -> unrecognised equity asymmetry. **Next observable change:** disclose organic SiC material/power growth, customer production rather than reservation, capex returns and FCF. **Frozen state: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.**

### II-VI (NASDAQ: IIVI) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low

**FACT:** Its FY2018 filing describes SiC substrates and advanced semiconductor materials for power switching and automotive applications. Performance Products revenue rose 24% to $266.4m, but that segment contains several unrelated product lines and the filing does not isolate EV SiC sales or margins. [SRC-BT261-D04-006]

**INTERPRETATION:** A plausible second qualified crystal supplier and direct scarcity comparator, but segment-wide growth cannot be assigned to EV SiC. **Disconfirmation:** mixed RF/industrial end markets and broad portfolio; no customer production or vehicle-specific materiality. **Missing bridge:** automotive wafer qualification -> repeat substrate volume -> isolated group materiality/profit. **Next observable change:** separately quantified SiC power substrate orders and contribution. **Frozen state: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.**

### Infineon (ETR: IFX) — REJECT for early D04 promotion / Medium

**FACT:** November 2018 annual report describes CoolSiC MOSFETs, power-module manufacturing and a long-term Cree wafer agreement, while warning high-volume production at reasonable cost remained technically challenging. [SRC-BT261-D04-003]

**INTERPRETATION:** A credible, diversified incumbent capture layer that also illustrates wafer-supply dependency, but a new EV SiC engine large enough to transform the group and unrecognised at T0 is not demonstrated. **Disconfirmation:** silicon IGBT substitution and diversification. **Missing bridge:** distinct EV SiC module shipments -> group profit contribution -> unrecognised equity. **Next observable change:** automotive-qualified SiC production economics. **Frozen state: REJECT / Medium.**

### STMicroelectronics (NYSE: STM) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low

**FACT:** Its March 2018 20-F lists SiC MOSFETs among a broad power semiconductor portfolio, alongside silicon IGBTs, without EV SiC sales or customer-program economics. [SRC-BT261-D04-007]

**INTERPRETATION:** The technology is present; application-specific production and materiality are not verified. **Disconfirmation:** diversified group and silicon alternatives. **Missing bridge:** automotive SiC qualification -> shipment mix -> incremental group margin. **Next observable change:** disclosed customer EV ramp and SiC capacity/profit. **Frozen state: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.**

### ROHM (TSE: 6963) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low

**FACT:** An August 2018 company release presented SiC-based inverter technology and its Formula E module contribution. This is an engineering demonstration, without quantified mass-production EV programme or group-profit contribution. [SRC-BT261-D04-008]

**INTERPRETATION:** Credible alternative power-device/module supplier, but racing qualification is not proof of road-vehicle volume. **Disconfirmation:** process/automotive reliability and cost, existing silicon competition. **Missing bridge:** prototype/racing module -> production automotive socket -> group-material profit. **Next observable change:** OEM volume contract and separate SiC sales/yield. **Frozen state: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.**

## Negative search and boundary

Searched upstream substrates (II-VI and Cree), merchant power devices/modules (Infineon, ST, ROHM) and gate-driver/IGBT substitutes. No smaller listed pure-play with verifiable <=T0 EV production economics was recovered. Private or unlisted device makers are not scored. The search is bounded rather than exhaustive. Search results exposed post-T0 Cree FY2019 and II-VI December-2018-quarter filings, plus later automotive SiC deals; none was used for a frozen fact or state. T0 valuation and adjusted price evidence were not reconstructed, further limiting an equity promotion.

## Frozen prediction

**No PROMOTE.** SiC wafer scarcity and contracted demand are substantial; Cree is already a sizeable, mixed and partly acquisition-driven segment by T0. II-VI, ST and ROHM lack isolated EV production economics, and Infineon is a mature group. **HYPOTHESIS:** Cree's materials bottleneck could convert economically even if the equity case is too late or capital-intensive. This null-promotion outcome is retained as a possible false negative to test after the prediction merge. No v1 rule or current watchlist conclusion changes.

## Outcome reveal — 25 September 2026

The prediction was merged in PR #315 before any post-T0 outcome research. Frozen rows above are unchanged. Post-T0 sources below are registered separately.

**FACT — architecture:** Silicon-carbide power products and wafer materials achieved substantial production revenue. Wolfspeed's FY2023 power products were $408.9m, versus $132.8m in FY2021; materials were $349.3m versus $241.6m. Infineon's 2024 report describes SiC across EVs and fast charging, with capacity and qualified wafer sourcing still central. [SRC-BT261-D04-O01, O05]

**Cree/Wolfspeed — PARTIAL technical capture, FAILED durable shareholder capture / High:** FY2023 including RF reported $921.9m revenue, 30.3% gross margin and $380.6m continuing-operations operating loss. After RF classification as discontinued, the comparable FY2023 continuing revenue is $758.5m; FY2024 continuing revenue was $807.2m, gross profit $77.4m (~9.6% margin), with $124.4m fab underutilisation costs and $671.3m continuing operating cash outflow. These reporting bases must not be mixed as a growth series. Wolfspeed filed Chapter 11 on 30 June 2025. Its September reorganisation cancelled old shares and distributed new shares to former holders at 0.008352 per old share. [SRC-BT261-D04-O01–O03]

**INTERPRETATION:** The T0 wafer scarcity and device demand translated into sales, but scaling a new 200-mm fab destroyed gross-margin/cash conversion, and debt dilution/restructuring overwhelmed the equity. The frozen EVIDENCE-BUILD correctly withheld shareholder promotion, while the technology capture is real. The 2019-to-2025 adjusted shareholder return and interim drawdown remain unscored; the reorganisation demonstrates a severe impairment, not literally zero recovery.

**II-VI/Coherent — PARTIAL strategic validation, group materiality DATA-LIMITED / Medium:** Coherent's FY2023 filing discussed strategic alternatives for the SiC business. October 2023 agreements gave Denso and Mitsubishi Electric 12.5% each of the SiC subsidiary for a combined $1bn commitment, leaving Coherent 75%. This validates strategic value and external customer alignment, but neither EV-specific SiC revenue nor its consolidated profit bridge is isolated here. The 2022 Coherent acquisition confounds group comparisons. [SRC-BT261-D04-O04]

**Infineon — PARTIAL incumbent capture / Medium:** Its FY2024 report describes a developed SiC power portfolio, production expansion and EV use. This supports the mature-incumbent capture mechanism but does not isolate an EV SiC group-profit or T0 valuation surprise, so the REJECT for a clean early company transformation is not falsified. [SRC-BT261-D04-O05]

**ST and ROHM — DATA-LIMITED company-specific EV conversion / Low:** The registered evidence in this pass does not isolate comparable 2019–2024 EV SiC revenue, profit and equity bridges for these two. They are not silently scored as failures or successes. Their frozen EVIDENCE-BUILD states remain unresolved on economic conversion; later general automotive/SiC product listings alone would not suffice.

**Scoring:** Zero PROMOTEs, thus no promotion-precision denominator. The strongest architecture beneficiary identified at T0 produced material revenue but failed durable cash/shareholder capture. A v2 hypothesis is to test capacity/financing runway and fab utilisation *as a separate durability gate* even when wafer LTAs are real. This is an observation, not a change to frozen v1. The post-T0 business-combination and reporting restatement, limited competitor attribution and missing adjusted equity series constrain any stronger claim.
