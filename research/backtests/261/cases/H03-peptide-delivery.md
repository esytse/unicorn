# H03 — Incretin/peptide manufacturing and delivery scale

**T0:** 1 June 2022. **Phase:** PRE-OUTCOME PREDICTION. Do not assume later obesity-label decisions or demand levels.

## Architecture and constraint

**FACT:** Novo reported in December 2021 that a contract manufacturer stopped filling Wegovy syringes following cGMP issues. Lilly announced May 2022 Mounjaro approval for type-2 diabetes. Ypsomed's 25 May 2022 release recorded Delivery Systems CHF259m revenue (+22.7%) and new automated YpsoMate/pen assembly investment, but no GLP-1-specific device revenue. West's FY2021 filing described proprietary injectable containment/delivery components without a GLP-1 split. [SRC-BT261-H03-001–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** Peptide synthesis/formulation, sterile fill, reliable disposable injectors, elastomers and scale-qualified lines can constrain launch. A documented fill disruption is stronger than a generic demand narrative, but the bottleneck may reside with drug owners or contract manufacturers, not every device vendor.

## Frozen candidate states

- **Ypsomed (SIX: YPSN) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.** Delivery Systems reached 56% of group sales and contributed utilisation/profit; this is already an established broad drug-device platform. Disconfirmation: diabetes-pump losses, capital investment, no named incretin socket. Missing bridge: GLP-1-specific qualified recurring units -> incremental group FCF and T0 equity asymmetry. Next: named commercial contracts/volume and margin. [SRC-BT261-H03-003]
- **Novo Nordisk (CPH: NOVO-B) — REJECT for scarce *merchant supplier* promotion / Medium.** Wegovy fill disruption proves supply scarcity, but drug owner controls the initiating product and existing diabetes franchise; independent manufacturing-profit/T0 mispricing bridge absent. Disconfirmation: cGMP/supply delay. Next: restored supply and unit economics. This is not a forecast of weak medicine sales. [SRC-BT261-H03-001]
- **Eli Lilly (NYSE: LLY) — REJECT for merchant complement promotion / Medium.** Mounjaro's diabetes approval validates emerging peptide demand, but it is an initiating therapy within a large company, not an overlooked device/fill supplier. Disconfirmation: launch supply and payer access. Missing bridge: third-party scarce complement and group surprise valuation. Next: commercial dose demand/availability. [SRC-BT261-H03-002]
- **West Pharmaceutical Services (NYSE: WST) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.** Qualified injectable containment may gain volume, but product/customer and GLP-1 contribution cannot be assigned from the 2021 broad portfolio. Disconfirmation: supplier alternatives and capacity cost. Missing bridge: named high-value incretin components -> order/organic margin/FCF. Next: modality-specific materiality. [SRC-BT261-H03-004]

## Negative search / freeze

Surveyed peptide API capacity, sterile fill and device/needle/elastomer suppliers; no clean pre-T0 public pure-play supplier with confirmed GLP-1 capacity reservation and company-transforming economics surfaced. Ypsomed's segment is already >50% of group and cannot be mislabeled a 5–15% new GLP engine. Later reporting snippets were excluded. **No PROMOTE.** T0 valuation/adjusted prices unresolved. **HYPOTHESIS:** fill and device qualification may matter, but which merchant captures the bottleneck is unresolved.
