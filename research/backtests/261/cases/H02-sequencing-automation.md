# H02 — Sequencing and high-throughput lab automation

**T0:** 2 January 2018. **Phase:** PRE-OUTCOME PREDICTION. FY2017 reports filed in 2018 are excluded even when fiscal year ended before T0.

## Architecture and constraint

**FACT:** Illumina's Q3 2017 10-Q reported NovaSeq rollout, $451m quarterly consumables (+14%) and lower initial NovaSeq instrument margins. PacBio's Q3 Sequel consumables rose to $10.6m from $6.5m, while reported GM was 34.9% and Roche terminated a collaboration. Brooks FY2017 10-K described automated cold sample handling and a diversified semiconductor/life-science mix. [SRC-BT261-H02-001–003]

**INTERPRETATION:** Higher sequencing throughput pushes sample tracking/storage, robotic preparation, reproducible consumables and instrument utilisation. Workflow scale can be captured by installed-platform vendors, independent automation or consumables. Instruments alone do not guarantee high-margin repeat assays.

## Frozen candidate states

- **Illumina (NASDAQ: ILMN) — REJECT for early *automation supplier* promotion / Medium.** Strong installed-platform/consumable capture already established; NovaSeq is a product transition within a large incumbent. Lower introductory instrument margin and cannibalisation are disconfirmers. Missing bridge: a newly emerging, unrecognised automation profit engine distinct from its existing core. Next: installed-base consumable gross profit and high-throughput utilisation. [SRC-BT261-H02-001]
- **Pacific Biosciences (NASDAQ: PACB) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.** Sequel installed base raised consumables but 34.9% quarterly GM and Roche termination impair funded conversion. Disconfirmation: consumable cost, competing short reads and financing. Missing bridge: repeat high-throughput usage -> positive instrument/consumable unit economics and group FCF. Next: utilisation and margin after transition. [SRC-BT261-H02-002]
- **Brooks Automation (NASDAQ: BRKS) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.** Automated sample-management capabilities and life-science customer exposure are real, but the mixed portfolio and acquisition activity do not isolate sequencing-specific group profit. Disconfirmation: semiconductor cycle and pharma/biobank rather than sequencing demand. Missing bridge: sequencing lab placements -> repeat consumables/storage margin -> group materiality. Next: organic life-science orders/margins by workflow. [SRC-BT261-H02-003]

## Negative search / freeze

Surveyed sample storage, liquid handling, single-cell prep and installed sequencer consumables. Several specialised workflow innovators were private at T0; no additional public issuer with clean sequencing-specific 5–15% emerging company engine was recovered in this bounded pass. Later search snippets were excluded. **No PROMOTE.** The throughput architecture is real, but incumbent maturity and instrument margin/attribution block early company promotion. T0 adjusted equity/valuation unresolved. **HYPOTHESIS:** repeat consumables matter more than headline instrument placements.
