# D09 — Accelerator infrastructure before the generative-AI buildout

**T0:** 30 November 2022. **Prediction phase:** FROZEN before outcome inspection. This boundary does not assume later generative-AI demand or name subsequent winners.

## Architecture and constraints

**FACT:** NVIDIA reported $3.83bn data-centre Q3 FY2023 revenue (+31% y/y), production H100 samples and named cloud AI collaborations in its November 2022 release/10-Q. Vertiv's September 2022 backlog was $4.7bn (+46% from year-end 2021), reflecting broad critical infrastructure demand. Arista's September 2022 filing described adoption of 400GbE by large cloud customers. [SRC-BT261-D09-001–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** Scaling large-model compute requires high-current board/rack power conversion, network bandwidth, cooling, package capacity and reliable installed systems. GPU revenue is an architecture comparator, not a licence to assign all downstream demand to AI. At T0 the velocity and duration of any generative demand shock are unknown. Stacked-memory-specific discovery belongs to sealed H04 and is not undertaken here.

## Listed candidate states

### Vicor (NASDAQ: VICR) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** September 2022 10-Q described Advanced Products concentrated in data-centre/hyperscaler server-board/rack voltage distribution, but Q3 Advanced Products revenue fell 12.5% sequentially amid supply constraints and a ~$6m return authorisation. [SRC-BT261-D09-005]

**INTERPRETATION:** A focused power-density supplier with credible customer access, but weaker shipments and customer/qualification risk prevent a clean state-change promotion. **Disconfirmation:** return, single-customer dependence, vertically integrated alternatives. **Missing bridge:** accelerator power socket -> repeat high-margin volume/royalties -> company FCF without excess capacity cost. **Next state:** customer production, mix/margin and diversified repeat orders. **Frozen: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.**

### Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** $4.7bn backlog and 2022 inflation/execution pressure accompany a broad data-centre power/cooling platform. [SRC-BT261-D09-002] **INTERPRETATION:** Rack buildout can capture systems demand, but the backlog is not AI-specific and does not establish unrecognised profitable liquid/power margin. **Disconfirmation:** project inflation and legacy portfolio. **Missing bridge:** accelerator density -> incremental orders/margin/cash distinct from general colocation. **Next state:** backlog conversion with sustained margin and AI workload attribution. **Frozen: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.**

### Arista Networks (NYSE: ANET) — REJECT for clean early promotion / Medium

**FACT:** Its 2022 filings reported 400G cloud adoption and Q3 revenue of $1.177bn versus $749m a year earlier. [SRC-BT261-D09-003–004] **INTERPRETATION:** A profitable high-speed-network incumbent, but most T0 acceleration precedes the nominated generative shock and AI-specific group transformation/under-recognition is unisolated. **Disconfirmation:** customer concentration and Ethernet alternatives. **Missing bridge:** new model cluster topology -> incremental company profit beyond broad 400G cloud cycle -> T0 valuation asymmetry. **Next state:** differentiated AI-network deployments and profit. **Frozen: REJECT / Medium.**

### NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) — REJECT as scarce-complement supplier candidate / Medium

**FACT:** H100 was already sampling and data-centre revenue $3.83bn in the last reported quarter. [SRC-BT261-D09-001] **INTERPRETATION:** Essential demand/architecture comparator and potential system winner, but its core accelerators are the initiating technology, not an overlooked downstream physical complement; large established data-centre business and valuation not underwritten here. **Disconfirmation:** product-cycle uncertainty. **Missing bridge:** new demand -> incremental unrecognised equity case. **Next state:** commercial H100 scale and group margins. **Frozen: REJECT for D09 complement promotion / Medium.**

## Negative search and leakage

Searched board power, rack cooling, 400G switching and semiconductor packaging suppliers. No pre-T0 small listed supplier had both AI-cluster-specific customer qualification and isolated group-profit data in this bounded packet. Large foundry/package providers are architecture comparators but not scored without an isolated early company bridge. Later NVIDIA/Vertiv/Vicor/Arista search snippets appeared and were excluded. Memory stack suppliers were deliberately not researched: H04 remains sealed. T0 price/market recognition still needs a separate adjusted-series reconstruction.

## Frozen prediction

**No PROMOTE.** Real cloud AI/H100 deployment, but the specific generative-AI complement inflection is too early to verify economically. **HYPOTHESIS:** power/cooling/network constraints may migrate rapidly; broad system incumbents could outperform narrow parts. The late-November T0 itself is a sensitivity: a subsequent demand discontinuity cannot be assumed at freeze. Frozen v1 unchanged.
