# H01 — ADAS multi-sensor perception

**T0:** 3 January 2017. **Phase:** PRE-OUTCOME PREDICTION. Only the dated filings below published by T0 inform these states; the 2016 annual reports released in 2017 are inadmissible.

## Architecture and constraint

**FACT:** Mobileye's November 2016 Q3 release documented qualified OEM EyeQ vision shipments and $72.6m OEM quarterly sales. Autoliv's September 2016 filing described integrated radar, night vision and ADAS camera systems. ON Semiconductor owned Aptina automotive image sensors, but Q3 2016 Image Sensor Group revenue fell 9.4% y/y. [SRC-BT261-H01-001–003]

**INTERPRETATION:** Multi-sensor coverage requires calibrated radar/camera sensing, compute, fail-safe fusion and OEM qualification. Greater sensor count does not automatically create a new merchant monopoly; OEMs and Tier-1s may integrate. We do not assume a later autonomy deployment curve.

## Frozen candidate states

- **Mobileye (NYSE: MBLY) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.** EyeQ/OEM qualification and rising ASP already demonstrate capture, but the whole company is an established ADAS franchise rather than an unrecognised 5–15% *new multi-sensor* engine. Disconfirmation: competition, OEM insourcing and unit pricing. Missing bridge: incremental multi-sensor content and group FCF beyond single-camera base, plus T0 valuation. Next: multi-sensor production wins and higher per-vehicle economic content. [SRC-BT261-H01-001]
- **Autoliv (NYSE: ALV) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.** Radar and camera systems in an established Active Safety unit create an integration route; segment mix and parent-wide inflection from richer sensing remain unisolated. Disconfirmation: passive safety scale, software competition, OEM integration. Missing bridge: repeat fusion-platform orders -> incremental segment margin/parent FCF. Next: separately disclosed core radar/camera volume and margin. [SRC-BT261-H01-002]
- **ON Semiconductor (NASDAQ: ON) — REJECT / Medium.** Aptina ownership places sensors in the chain, but latest available Image Sensor Group revenue fell 9.4%, with no ADAS-only group profit. Disconfirmation: mix and commodity image sensors. Missing bridge: distinct multi-sensor automotive production/pricing inflection. Next: segment automotive sensor share/margin. [SRC-BT261-H01-003–004]
- **Ambarella (NASDAQ: AMBA) — REJECT / Medium.** Video processing sold chiefly to sports/IP/UAV and **automotive aftermarket** cameras, and six-month 2016 operating income fell from $45.2m to $10.6m. Disconfirmation: aftermarket is not OEM ADAS qualification and major-customer dependence. Missing bridge: qualified OEM perception socket -> material recurring company FCF. Next: OEM automotive production and profit disclosure. [SRC-BT261-H01-005]

## Negative search / freeze

Surveyed vision ASIC, radar, image sensor, camera SoC and private lidar routes; no other listed narrow multi-sensor supplier with <=T0 qualification, emerging group revenue and valuation evidence was recovered. Later acquisitions, product launches and outcomes seen in search snippets were excluded. **No PROMOTE.** The already material ADAS franchises make this a timing/recognition sensitivity. T0 adjusted equity series unrecovered. **HYPOTHESIS:** integration/qualification beats raw sensor count, conditional on OEM programme economics.
