# H05 — Solar inverter and system architecture scale

**T0:** 2 January 2015. **Phase:** PRE-OUTCOME PREDICTION. A fiscal 2014 10-K released in 2015 is inadmissible.

## Architecture and constraint

**FACT:** Enphase's November 2014 Q3 release reported $99.1m revenue (+60% y/y), 32.8% GAAP GM vs 28.1% and $0.8m GAAP net profit as module-level microinverters and monitoring scaled. SMA's November 2014 nine-month report documented a difficult price/volume cycle in established central/string inverters. Advanced Energy's Q3 2014 release and December 2014 disclosure reported solar inverter pressure and an evaluation of strategic alternatives. [SRC-BT261-H05-001–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** As residential/commercial solar grows, module-level conversion/monitoring and grid-compliant string/central equipment compete on yield, safety, cost and service. Growing installations do not guarantee any one architecture retains margin; inverter pricing and warranty obligations matter.

## Frozen candidate states

- **Enphase Energy (NASDAQ: ENPH) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.** Microinverters are already nearly the whole business, and quarterly operating economics improved. This is a qualified architecture, not a newly 5–15% company engine; longer-lived GM after warranty claims and T0 valuation are missing. Disconfirmation: string-inverter/optimiser substitution, manufacturing cost and warranty liabilities. Missing bridge: durable unit margin/FCF and unrecognised equity. Next: repeated profitable volume and warranty-adjusted cash margin. [SRC-BT261-H05-001–002]
- **SMA Solar Technology (FWB: S92) — REJECT for early new architecture promotion / Medium.** Large established inverter portfolio but the contemporaneous nine-month report indicates weak shipments and losses rather than new differentiated profit. Disconfirmation: price pressure/Europe demand. Missing bridge: material new product mix and margin turn. Next: organic orders and positive segment FCF. [SRC-BT261-H05-003]
- **Advanced Energy Industries (NASDAQ: AEIS) — REJECT / Medium.** Commercial/utility inverter business faced weak economics; management announced a strategic-alternatives review on 22 December 2014. Disconfirmation: potential exit. Missing bridge: a viable inverter business delivering incremental group FCF. Next: sale/restructure or funded positive margin. [SRC-BT261-H05-004–005]

## Negative search / freeze

Surveyed module microinverters, string/central inverters, panel optimisers and battery/grid integration. **SolarEdge was not publicly listed at T0**; its S-1 was first filed 18 February 2015, after T0, so it is excluded from the investable candidate denominator. That later filing is used only to verify listing chronology, never to construct a T0 prediction or score a false negative. [SRC-BT261-H05-006] **No PROMOTE.** T0 valuation/adjusted return series absent; Enphase profitability and the 5–15% heuristic create a timing sensitivity. **HYPOTHESIS:** module-level monitoring improves system yield but price/warranty risk can dominate shareholder capture.

## Post-freeze outcome reveal — 25 September 2026

**FACT:** Enphase lost $22.1m net in 2015 and $67.5m in 2016 after its 2014 near-break-even quarter, then reached ~$2.3bn revenue and ~$397m net profit in 2022 from microinverters and storage. SMA rebounded to €999.6m sales and positive EBIT in 2015 from €805.4m in 2014. Advanced Energy wound down its solar inverter business in 2015, projecting a $260–290m pre-tax charge. [SRC-BT261-H05-O01–O04]

**INTERPRETATION:** Enphase demonstrates both early shareholder-financing danger and later system-level capture, with a long gap between T0 and durable operating success; a 2015 PROMOTE would require a drawdown/dilution-aware thesis not in the packet. SMA's 2015 rebound is an incumbent cycle, not a proven new architecture win. Advanced Energy REJECT is validated. SolarEdge was unlisted at T0 and is not a false negative. Equity checkpoints unscored.
