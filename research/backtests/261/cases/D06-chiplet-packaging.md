# D06 — Chiplets and advanced packaging

**T0:** 2 January 2020. **Prediction phase:** FROZEN before outcome inspection. The 2019 fiscal year alone does not qualify a later-published filing.

## Architecture and bottleneck

**FACT:** Intel's FY2018 filing described EMIB bridges and Foveros logic stacking to mix process nodes in one package. TSMC's 2018 report described CoWoS integrating 7-nm compute and HBM2 in qualified production, InFO-oS multiple-chip production, and its proposed SoIC chiplet integration. [SRC-BT261-D06-001–002]

**INTERPRETATION:** Yield, fine-pitch interconnect, precise placement/bonding, substrate routing and package test become constraints as the package becomes a system. The value may remain captive at foundries/IDMs, shift to independent assembly/test or equipment, or be offset by capex and yield losses. Existing 2.5D production does not establish a new merchant bonding monopoly.

## Listed candidate states

### Amkor Technology (NASDAQ: AMKR) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** FY2018 10-K documented design wins in advanced flip-chip/TSV and wafer-level fan-out alongside 7–16nm chips, RF SiP and sensors. Its first-quarter 2019 release separately reported $422m advanced-product quarterly revenue but this aggregates many architectures. [SRC-BT261-D06-003–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** Qualified merchant packaging may capture the package step, but the mixed advanced revenue is already substantial and chiplet-specific margin/order inflection is absent. **Disconfirmation:** capacity utilisation, capital and foundry in-house integration. **Missing bridge:** chiplet package socket -> distinct high-yield advanced sales -> incremental group FCF/unrecognised valuation. **Next state:** separate HPC chiplet wins and advanced-product incremental margin. **Frozen: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.**

### Kulicke & Soffa (NASDAQ: KLIC) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low

**FACT:** Its FY2019 10-K (filed before T0) describes ball/wafer-level bonders and advanced packaging sold to IDMs/OSATs, without isolating chiplet fine-pitch orders. [SRC-BT261-D06-005]

**INTERPRETATION:** An equipment path exists, but established wire bonding is not proof of qualified hybrid bonding. **Disconfirmation:** process substitution and cyclicality. **Missing bridge:** fine-pitch tool qualification -> repeat chiplet orders -> material group profit. **Next state:** separately reported new bonding-tool customer volume and margins. **Frozen: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.**

### TSMC (TWSE: 2330) — REJECT for early merchant supplier promotion / Medium

**FACT:** CoWoS/HBM2 and multi-chip InFO were already in production by 2018. [SRC-BT261-D06-002] **INTERPRETATION:** A strong architecture validator with captive foundry/packaging integration; standalone chiplet profit, early 5–15% engine and T0 under-recognition are unisolated for this large incumbent. **Disconfirmation:** cannibalisation and capacity cost. **Missing bridge:** package-specific group profit and attractive unrecognised equity. **Next state:** disclosed advanced-packaging capacity/revenue/profit. **Frozen: REJECT / Medium.**

### Intel (NASDAQ: INTC) — REJECT for independent supplier promotion / Medium

**FACT:** Foveros and EMIB were internal package architectures announced in 2018. [SRC-BT261-D06-001] **INTERPRETATION:** Technical relevance does not prove external merchant capture or group transformation. **Disconfirmation:** internal integration and manufacturing execution. **Missing bridge:** volume production and external profitable package economics. **Next state:** commercial shipments and isolated package return. **Frozen: REJECT / Medium.**

## Negative search and leakage

Searched independent assembly/test, bonding equipment, foundry integration, substrate and inspection. No substrate/inspection issuer with verifiable <=T0 *chiplet-specific* qualification and group financial materiality was recovered in this bounded pass. Search results exposed later Besi hybrid-bonding revenue/orders, before a clean historical review. **Besi is excluded from scored candidates**; it cannot be counted as a clean v1 miss or retroactively promoted. Later Amkor/Intel filings and TSMC 2019 report published after T0 were excluded. T0 share-price and valuation checkpoints remain unrecovered.

## Frozen prediction

**No PROMOTE.** Chiplet packaging is demonstrable at T0, but merchant capture and early group materiality are unisolated. **HYPOTHESIS:** foundry-integrated production and independent assembly/test both benefit; tools must prove novel production qualification. Besi leakage weakens later aggregate coverage confidence. Frozen v1 unchanged.

## Post-freeze outcome reveal — 25 September 2026

**FACT:** Amkor explicitly attributed Q2 2024 growth to advanced packaging for premium phones and AI 2.5D, while the broad advanced bucket does not identify incremental chiplet profit. Kulicke & Soffa reported more than tenfold thermo-compression bonding growth over four years, without absolute TCB revenue. TSMC's 2024 report describes CoWoS growth driven by AI from 2023 and integrated capacity expansion. [SRC-BT261-D06-O01–O04, O06]

**INTERPRETATION:** The architecture and both captive and merchant paths converted technically. No scored candidate has a clean package-specific total-company FCF/valuation bridge; therefore no validated early PROMOTE. Besi's 26-system hybrid-bonding order in May 2024 demonstrates a consequential **coverage loss** after pre-freeze search leakage, not a scored v1 false negative. [SRC-BT261-D06-O05] Equity checkpoints unscored; see OUTCOMES and FAILURES.
