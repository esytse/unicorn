# D03 — 5G handset and network RF complexity

**T0:** 2 January 2018  
**Prediction phase:** FROZEN before outcome reveal  
**Architecture:** more bands, carrier aggregation, antenna paths and emerging 5G NR increase RF front-end and network test complexity.

## T0 architecture evidence and scarce complements

**FACT:** On 21 December 2017, 3GPP reported approval of the first implementable non-standalone 5G NR specification. This was not a completed standalone standard or a commercial-volume handset cycle. Qorvo's 2017 technical presentation showed RF front ends progressing from one antenna in single-carrier 3G to multiple paths under LTE carrier aggregation; Qualcomm described integration of PAs, switches, acoustic filters, tuners and tracking from modem to antenna. [SRC-BT261-D03-001–003]

**INTERPRETATION:** A credible constraint is simultaneous RF-band isolation and insertion-loss control in a limited handset footprint, alongside efficient high-power RF amplification and conformance/beamforming test for network equipment. Premium BAW/TC-SAW filters and qualified GaN power devices have process/yield scarcity; design software, test platforms and module integration are alternative capture layers. The LTE-Advanced upgrade was already raising RF content before 5G; attributing all future content to 5G would be false.

**HYPOTHESIS:** Suppliers with demonstrable production qualification and a small but emerging 5G-specific revenue engine could compound content gains. This requires evidence beyond prototypes or management's addressable-market forecasts. Incumbent integration by Qualcomm, Qorvo and Broadcom may capture or compress value available to smaller filter designers and manufacturers.

## Listed candidate search using only publications through T0

### Qorvo (NASDAQ: QRVO) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** In February 2017 Qorvo introduced a 5G RF front end combining BAW/TC-SAW/SAW filters, switches and multi-band PAs. Its 2017 investor materials described premium-filter improvement, next-generation integration and expanded module capacity. FY2017 revenue was $3.03bn (+16.2%); capex was $552.7m, up from $315.6m, partly to increase premium-filter capacity. Apple-related sales were 34% of group revenue. [SRC-BT261-D03-004–006]

**INTERPRETATION:** The manufacturing and integration response to RF complexity is real and economically meaningful, but the observed earnings already derive mostly from incumbent 4G smartphones. No 5G-specific revenue mix, incremental margin or overlooked group transformation is shown at T0.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** Heavy capex and Apple concentration; announced 5G product is not proof of scaled 5G shipments. LTE carrier aggregation could account for much of the current demand.

**Missing bridge:** 5G-specific qualified volume -> premium-filter yields/utilisation and pricing -> incremental group profit beyond existing 4G franchise -> unrecognised equity upside.

**Expected next observable state change:** disclosed 5G design wins and filter/module volume accompanied by sustained margin and return on new capacity.

**Frozen state:** **EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium**.

### Akoustis Technologies (NASDAQ: AKTS) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low

**FACT:** In June 2017 Akoustis acquired a 150-mm RF wafer fab; its September 2017 10-Q described prototype filters for LTE Band 41, radar and 5GHz Wi-Fi. A November announcement reported a first purchase order from a top-five smartphone OEM, explicitly for **prototypes**, not production. It had no commercial product revenue in its FY2017 filing. At 13 November, cash was $4.7m, expected to fund operations only through December 2017; the filing raised substantial doubt about going-concern capacity. [SRC-BT261-D03-007–009]

**INTERPRETATION:** Scarce single-crystal BAW process and a named-tier customer evaluation create a plausible small-company option, but this is an evaluation rather than an economic state change. LTE and Wi-Fi prototypes are not 5G sockets.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** no volume qualification or product margin, material cash burn, near-term financing requirement, dilution/manufacturing-yield risk and competing incumbents.

**Missing bridge:** prototype PO -> verified production qualification -> repeat volume at acceptable yield -> self-funded group profit without excessive dilution.

**Expected next observable state change:** a production purchase order and separately disclosed commercial filter revenue with yield/cash-runway evidence.

**Frozen state:** **EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low**.

### Resonant (NASDAQ: RESN) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low

**FACT:** Its September 2017 10-Q described an ISN filter-design/IP licensing model with TC-SAW and high-performance module design capabilities. Nine-month revenue was $482,000, primarily development fees; $261,000 was deferred. The company said customer designs could take 9–18 months before sampling and potential licensing receipts. It raised equity in 2017 and held $13.5m cash at September quarter end. [SRC-BT261-D03-010]

**INTERPRETATION:** An asset-light royalty layer could capture filter-design complexity, but development fees are not proof of recurring unit economics. Its designs could be absorbed by foundries or vertically integrated RF incumbents.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** no material royalty revenue, long qualification lag, repeated equity financing and no isolated 5G design win.

**Missing bridge:** licensed design -> manufactured filter in qualified handset/network product -> recurring unit royalties -> material profit without dilution.

**Expected next observable state change:** shipped customer product and reported recurring royalties rather than further design-fee-only growth.

**Frozen state:** **EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low**.

### MACOM (NASDAQ: MTSI) — REJECT for D03 pre-inflection promotion / Medium

**FACT:** Its FY2017 10-K listed GaN-on-silicon RF power products for wireless networks and 5G, but said the company had abandoned a GaN-on-SiC programme, was still trying to qualify its GaN-on-Si process/products, and was litigating with a GaN licensor. Optical networks represented 59% of revenue, while Networks growth was primarily acquisition and cloud-datacenter related. [SRC-BT261-D03-011]

**INTERPRETATION:** Base-station RF is relevant, but the revenue inflection cannot be attributed to 5G GaN and the manufacturing/IP bridge is unusually fragile.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** process qualification and patent litigation, acquisition-confounded growth, and limited 5G-specific materiality.

**Missing bridge:** GaN process qualification -> repeat 5G base-station volume -> group margin/cash generation distinct from optical/cloud businesses.

**Expected next observable state change:** independently quantified commercial 5G GaN shipments and stable yields/rights.

**Frozen state:** **REJECT / Medium**.

### Keysight Technologies (NYSE: KEYS) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** Keysight reported record 5G-related orders in fiscal Q2 2017 and said 5G, 4.9G and Wi-Fi R&D test drove Communications Solutions activity in fiscal Q4. Its FY2017 10-K nevertheless reported $3.2bn group revenue, flat Communications Solutions revenue and declining organic revenue, as smartphone-chain weakness offset strength in 5G and datacenters. Its $1.62bn Ixia acquisition confounds total-company growth. [SRC-BT261-D03-012–013]

**INTERPRETATION:** Test demand appeared before rollout and customer relationships are credible, but the 5G engine was not shown to be capable of transforming this diversified company at T0.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** no 5G test mix/margin disclosure; organic weakness and acquisition-driven reported growth.

**Missing bridge:** 5G test orders -> recurring production/conformance demand -> sizeable organic group profit -> unrecognised equity case.

**Expected next observable state change:** sustained organic Communications Solutions order/revenue growth attributable to 5G with operating leverage.

**Frozen state:** **EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium**.

### Cree / Wolfspeed (NASDAQ: CREE) — REJECT for D03 pre-inflection promotion / Medium

**FACT:** Cree's FY2017 10-K described GaN-on-SiC RF die, transistors and MMICs for telecom and defence, plus a custom foundry offering. Wolfspeed segment revenue was $221.2m or 15% of group revenue, but this combined SiC materials, power devices and RF devices; it did not isolate 5G RF. Competitors included MACOM, Qorvo, Mitsubishi and Sumitomo. [SRC-BT261-D03-014]

**INTERPRETATION:** A credible material/process supplier at a superficially attractive 15% segment mix, yet attributing that entire segment to 5G would misread the evidence. Competing SiC power and LED drivers also dilute group specificity.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** no 5G qualification or RF-specific revenue/margin split and multiple competing GaN processes/vendors.

**Missing bridge:** 5G GaN design wins -> RF-specific segment materiality -> group cash profit with sufficient pricing power.

**Expected next observable state change:** quantified telecom GaN production/customer mix and segment economics distinct from power materials.

**Frozen state:** **REJECT / Medium**.

### Skyworks Solutions (NASDAQ: SWKS) — REJECT for D03 pre-inflection promotion / Medium

**FACT:** Fiscal 2017 revenue was about $3.7bn (+11%) and operating cash flow $1.5bn. Its filings described broad RF modules, PAs and filters; 5G positioning was prospective, while the actual growth was already driven by mobile and IoT connectivity demand. [SRC-BT261-D03-015]

**INTERPRETATION:** This is a mature RF-content benchmark rather than a previously unrecognised 5G-specific new engine.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** no 5G-specific material revenue/qualification disclosed at T0; established business scale and customer concentration reduce transformation purity.

**Missing bridge:** new 5G content per handset -> additional company profit after mature-mobile baseline -> attractive unrecognised valuation.

**Expected next observable state change:** 5G-design production economics separately distinguishable from existing 4G mobile revenue.

**Frozen state:** **REJECT / Medium**.

## Negative and boundary search

- Qualcomm publicly launched a comprehensive modem-to-antenna RF platform in February 2017. It is a serious vertical-integration/substitution threat and an architecture comparator, but no evidence isolated an early 5G RF engine capable of transforming its already-large group, so it is not scored as an early supplier candidate. [SRC-BT261-D03-003]
- Broadcom's established acoustic-filter capability makes it an important incumbent comparator, but an isolated pre-T0 5G qualification or emerging group-material engine was not recovered. No hindsight inference is made from its later corporate history.
- RF test, GaN power, filtering and antenna integration were searched; no candidate is promoted solely because a 5G-addressable-market slide projected RF content growth. Other small/delisted suppliers may have been missed: the negative search is bounded, not exhaustive.
- General search returned documents published after T0 for Akoustis and Resonant. Their contents were excluded; every prediction fact above comes from independently dated <=T0 filings or releases. This boundary caveat must be carried into outcome scoring. No later performance evidence has been examined for this case.

## D03 frozen prediction

**No PROMOTE.** The standards milestone and filter/test scarcity are credible, but no screened issuer simultaneously meets the frozen production-state-change, potential group-materiality, economic-capture and under-recognition tests. The early/small candidates (Akoustis, Resonant) are still at prototype/design-fee stages with financing risk; Qorvo and Keysight have actual capacity/orders but already substantial RF/test businesses. The others lack a clean 5G-specific bridge.

**HYPOTHESIS:** Qorvo is the strongest operational architecture validator; Akoustis is the most asymmetric but lowest-confidence conditional lead if prototype qualification converts without ruinous dilution. This is not a PROMOTE or a 2026 buy recommendation. A null-promotion case is a meaningful test of whether v1 can avoid plausible-sounding 5G narratives.

## Outcome reveal — 25 September 2026

The prediction above was committed and merged in PR #313 before this post-T0 evidence was inspected. This section is append-only; frozen states and v1 rules are unchanged. Source IDs below resolve in `sources/source-register.md`. Later search-result leakage from the prediction phase remains a caveat for Akoustis/Resonant, although no post-T0 filing was used to choose their states.

### Architecture result

**FACT:** Qorvo subsequently described 5G mobile and base-station products, coexistence across low/mid/high bands, MIMO, additional receive paths and higher RF content. Keysight identified 5G design/manufacturing and O-RAN as test-demand drivers. The RF-complexity bottleneck strengthened, while the commercial upside was mixed with Wi-Fi 6, advanced 4G, optical backhaul, high-speed digital and customer cycles. [SRC-BT261-D03-O01, O05]

### Qorvo — PARTIAL, substantial economic conversion but not a clean early discovery

**FACT:** FY2021 revenue rose 24.0% to $4.015bn from $3.239bn, primarily on 5G mobile/base-station and Wi-Fi demand, partially offset by Huawei weakness. Gross margin rose to 46.9% from 40.8%, operating income to $906.6m from $423.2m, and operating cash flow to $1.302bn. FY2022 revenue grew to $4.646bn and gross margin to 49.2%, but China-based 5G handset weakness and ASP erosion affected inventory/capture. Apple still represented 30% of FY2021 revenue. [SRC-BT261-D03-O01–O02]

**INTERPRETATION (Medium-High on operating capture; Low on T0 asymmetry):** The frozen filter-capacity and integrated-front-end thesis converted strongly. But 5G-only incremental profit and whether 2018 equity already discounted the transition are unisolated; other product cycles contributed. EVIDENCE-BUILD is defensible for *pre-inflection unrecognised unicorn* selection but potentially too cautious for an architecture/capture investor. No precise equity return is claimed.

### Akoustis — FAILED economic/shareholder conversion, despite product sales

**FACT:** FY2024 RF-filter sales were $16.16m of $27.38m group revenue, but that filter line had a $4.59m gross loss; group gross loss was $0.69m and net loss $167.95m. Shares outstanding increased materially from roughly 19.4m at the November 2017 filing to 72.2m at June 2023. Akoustis filed Chapter 11 in December 2024. [SRC-BT261-D03-008, O03–O05]

**INTERPRETATION (High):** The prototype-to-volume link partly converted, but fab yield, capital needs, legal liabilities and financing overwhelmed shareholder economics. The frozen EVIDENCE-BUILD rather than PROMOTE avoided treating a top-five OEM *prototype* order as production proof. Do not infer that technical filters had no value or compute a total-return figure without a reliable adjusted series.

### Resonant — PARTIAL IP validation; not standalone recurring-profit conversion

**FACT:** Murata entered a 5G XBAR collaboration/licence in 2019 and expanded it in 2021, with prepaid royalties/fees. FY2021 revenue was $2.2m, mostly contract recognition from Murata, versus a $35.97m net loss; the filing discussed prospective sales-based royalties rather than showing them as a material recurring engine. Murata agreed to buy the remaining shares for $4.50 cash in 2022. [SRC-BT261-D03-O06–O07]

**INTERPRETATION (Medium):** The design-IP scarcity attracted a strategic acquirer, but the frozen design -> shipped filter -> recurring royalty -> material profit bridge was not demonstrated independently. An acquisition price is an equity outcome, not automatic validation of the original economic thesis; T0-to-exit return remains unscored.

### MACOM — REJECT for clean 5G GaN promotion not falsified

**FACT:** FY2020 telecom growth was driven in part by carrier optical semiconductors targeted at 5G, while FY2021 telecom revenue fell 10.1%; no 5G GaN product profit was isolated. MACOM bought Wolfspeed's RF business effective December 2023, a much later ownership change. [SRC-BT261-D03-O08–O09]

**INTERPRETATION (Medium):** MACOM participated in wider 5G infrastructure, but not via the T0-specific GaN-on-Si production bridge shown in the frozen case. Later acquisition cannot repair a missing T0 qualification.

### Keysight — PARTIAL, test demand converted without isolated 5G transformation

**FACT:** By FY2022 group revenue was $5.42bn; commercial-communications revenue grew 11% y/y, with wireless 5G development/manufacturing, O-RAN and high-speed digital demand among the reported drivers. [SRC-BT261-D03-O10]

**INTERPRETATION (Medium):** The T0 test-order signal pointed to real demand, but no disclosed 5G-specific revenue/profit share proves a stand-alone company-transforming new engine. EVIDENCE-BUILD was appropriately cautious on attribution and starting scale.

### Cree/Wolfspeed — REJECT not falsified for group transformation

**FACT:** Wolfspeed continued offering next-generation telecom RF devices but sold the RF business to MACOM effective December 2023 while focusing on SiC power/materials. [SRC-BT261-D03-O09, O11]

**INTERPRETATION (Medium):** The 5G RF exposure existed, but was not demonstrated to become a transforming profit engine for the original listed parent; ownership migrated. Sale value cannot be attributed exclusively to 5G.

### Skyworks — PARTIAL / possible economic false negative

**FACT:** FY2021 revenue grew 52.3% to $5.109bn and gross profit to $2.512bn (49.2% margin). The company identified the onset of 5G and Wi-Fi 6 upgrade cycles and increased next-generation content per device as material drivers. Its filing did not isolate a 5G-only revenue/profit segment. [SRC-BT261-D03-O12]

**INTERPRETATION (Medium on broad RF conversion; Low on false-negative equity conclusion):** The frozen REJECT for a *clean pre-inflection* D03 promotion may have been too strict about mature scale: a large incumbent can still enjoy a substantial architecture-driven earnings jump. Yet the precise 5G increment, starting valuation and shareholder return remain open, so this is a possible economic false negative, not a proven missed unicorn.

### Scoring and methodological observation

**No candidate was PROMOTE**, so D03 adds neither a success nor a failure to the promotion-precision denominator. It is not valid to call this a 100% accurate no-pick: incumbents Qorvo and Skyworks showed significant economic conversion, while the small-cap prototype Akoustis proved destructive to shareholders and Resonant's IP exit did not demonstrate recurring profit. The strongest inference is that a *zero-pick* can avoid a catastrophic false positive while missing material incumbent captures. The magnitude of any missed 2018 equity opportunity is DATA-LIMITED until adjusted 6/12/18/24/36-month prices, T0 valuation and drawdowns are reconstructed. Search-result contamination for Akoustis/Resonant reduces confidence in clean prospective candidate discovery even though the frozen decisions used only <=T0 evidence.

**HYPOTHESIS for eventual v2 (not a v1 amendment):** Test a separate 'mature incumbent with new architecture-driven operating leverage' route while continuing to demand product-specific attribution and valuation evidence; preserve a hard distinction between prototype orders, recurring revenue and profitable cash capture. Frozen v1 is unchanged.
