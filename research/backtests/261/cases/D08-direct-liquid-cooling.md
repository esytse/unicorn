# D08 — Direct liquid cooling at higher compute density

**T0:** 3 January 2022. **Prediction phase:** FROZEN before outcome inspection.

## Architecture and scarce complements

**FACT:** Asetek announced a 2020 HPE Apollo direct-liquid-cooling collaboration for HPC/AI, but its September 2021 update said it would discontinue cash-consuming HPC focus to prioritise the general data-centre market. Vertiv presented liquid-cooling projects/concepts at the 2021 OCP summit; its 2020 10-K and 2021 releases described a broad power/cooling data-centre business. [SRC-BT261-D08-001–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** Higher rack thermal flux makes direct-to-chip cold plates, leak-safe connections, coolant distribution units (CDUs), pumps, serviceability and heat rejection more demanding. Proof of prototype coolant flow is not proof of qualified fleet uptime and positive unit economics. Air, rear-door exchange and immersion compete; customers can integrate their own loop.

## Listed candidate states

### Asetek (OSE: ASETEK) — REJECT / Medium

**FACT:** HPE collaboration showed technical product relevance, but management's September 2021 guidance described a strategic HPC exit after inadequate returns and supply-chain pressure. [SRC-BT261-D08-001–002] **INTERPRETATION:** A valuable negative control: the earliest direct-liquid vendor itself signalled poor economics. **Disconfirmation:** demonstrated inability to turn HPC deployments into attractive cash returns. **Missing bridge:** newly funded general-data-centre design wins -> repeat profitable direct-liquid units. **Next state:** sustained data-centre segment gross profit/FCF rather than pilot announcements. **Frozen: REJECT / Medium.**

### Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** It sold broad data-centre power and thermal systems, with 2021 OCP liquid-cooling demonstrations and strong second-quarter 2021 group sales/backlog. No direct-to-chip product revenue/margin was separately reported. [SRC-BT261-D08-003–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** Channel, maintenance and power/cooling integration could capture deployments, but group sales already include established air/power offerings. **Disconfirmation:** inflation, project execution, competing cooling modes. **Missing bridge:** qualified CDU/cold plate fleet orders -> direct-liquid sales and incremental group cash profit -> T0 asymmetry. **Next state:** repeat liquid-cooling shipments, service attachment and segment margin. **Frozen: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.**

### Modine / Airedale (NYSE: MOD) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low

**FACT:** Modine owned Airedale, a long-standing commercial cooling business; dated pre-T0 source material in this pass does not isolate direct-to-chip CDU revenue or qualified high-density compute sockets. [SRC-BT261-D08-005]

**INTERPRETATION:** Heat-rejection/channel access offers optionality, but extrapolating later AI cooling economics backward is forbidden. **Disconfirmation:** traditional air-cooling/vehicle mix and no direct-liquid unit economics. **Missing bridge:** direct-liquid deployment -> group-material orders/margin/FCF. **Next state:** dated liquid-system customer qualification and disclosed revenue. **Frozen: EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.**

### nVent (NYSE: NVT) — REJECT / Low

**FACT:** Its 2020 10-K describes enclosures and electrical protection sold via data-centre contractor/OEM channels but no separately established direct-liquid CDU profit engine. [SRC-BT261-D08-006] **INTERPRETATION:** Adjacent distribution is not a bottleneck qualification. **Disconfirmation:** direct-liquid competitive positioning absent. **Missing bridge:** product socket -> repeat liquid flow/leak economics -> group profit. **Next state:** qualified liquid product shipments and margin. **Frozen: REJECT / Low.**

## Negative search and boundary

The 2019/2020 filings are dated before T0; the 2021 annual reports published in 2022 are excluded. Search snippets also exposed 2024–26 Modine/Vertiv/nVent results; none informs these states, and Modine's evidence gap is expressly preserved. ## Frozen prediction

**No PROMOTE.** T0 direct liquid cooling has technical deployment evidence but insufficient attributable merchant profit. Asetek's retrenchment is a meaningful disconfirming signal; Vertiv has the best integration option but no clean liquid-specific group bridge. **HYPOTHESIS:** the durable scarce layer might be certified systems/service rather than a standalone cold plate. Frozen v1 unchanged.
