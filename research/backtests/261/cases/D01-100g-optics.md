# D01 — 100G optical interconnect transition

**T0:** 4 January 2016  
**Prediction phase:** FROZEN before outcome reveal  
**Architecture:** hyperscale/networking transition toward 100G optical interconnect.

## T0 architecture evidence

**FACT:** LightCounting reported in March 2015 that Acacia saw strong 100G DWDM demand and planned silicon-photonics production ramp; Applied Optoelectronics and Innolight were benefiting from Web2.0/white-box demand. This supports a real bandwidth/optical transition before T0, but Acacia was not publicly listed at T0.

**INTERPRETATION:** The scarce complement was not generic bandwidth. The useful supplier search was high-speed optical transceivers/components plus the integration/manufacturing capability needed to qualify 100G products at scale.

## Listed candidate search using only <=T0 evidence

### Applied Optoelectronics (NASDAQ: AAOI) — PROMOTE / Medium

**FACT:** On 5 November 2015 AAOI said it had won two 100G design wins from two hyperscale datacenter customers. It explicitly linked rapid 100G market entry to vertically integrated 25G laser-diode and 100G light-engine manufacturing. Q3 revenue was $57.1m, +56% y/y.

**Inference:** This is close to the #261 desired pre-inflection shape: named state change (two hyperscale design wins), architecture-specific internal capacity/capture, small company base, and growth already accelerating while the 100G transition was described as only beginning.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** T0 disclosure did not yet quantify 100G revenue, unit ramp, customer concentration economics or durability. The thesis could fail through customer concentration, rapid optical ASP erosion or competing transceiver architectures.

**Missing bridge:** design wins -> 100G volume -> material revenue/margin contribution -> diversified hyperscale adoption.

**Frozen state:** **PROMOTE / Medium**.

### Oclaro (NASDAQ: OCLR) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** By the November 2015 Q1 FY2016 presentation, 100G transmission was $40.8m, 47% of quarterly revenue, up from $21.3m / 24% a year earlier. Datacom/client-side revenue was 54%. Management guided the following quarter to $88–94m revenue and positive adjusted EBITDA.

**Inference:** The 100G engine was already far beyond the #261 5–15% early-materiality window. This is strong bottleneck/capture validation but a later-stage turnaround rather than the clean pre-inflection shape sought by #261.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** total quarterly revenue had only recently recovered from the low-$80m range; 100G mix growth partly replaced declining 40G rather than automatically creating total-company growth.

**Missing bridge:** 100G mix -> sustained total-company growth, margin/EBITDA inflection and customer durability.

**Frozen state:** **EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium**.

### NeoPhotonics (NYSE: NPTN) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium

**FACT:** May 2015 results reported 57% of Q1 revenue from 100G and described leadership in 100G coherent receivers and narrow-linewidth tunable lasers following an acquisition.

**Inference:** This validates a genuine 100G component bottleneck, but at T0 the new engine was already majority revenue and the evidence was more telecom/coherent-oriented than a clean hyperscale datacenter qualification signal.

**Strongest disconfirming evidence:** high 100G exposure does not establish that hyperscale 100G was a new company-transforming engine rather than an already-recognised product mix.

**Missing bridge:** hyperscale-specific qualification/materiality and evidence of incremental group economics.

**Frozen state:** **EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium**.

### Lumentum (NASDAQ: LITE) — REJECT for D01 pre-inflection promotion / Medium

**FACT:** FY2015 Datacom represented 17.4% of revenue versus 14.3% in FY2014, while Optical Communications remained dominated by Telecom. Its 2015 filing described 10G/40G/100G technologies but did not provide a T0 100G hyperscale state-change comparable with AAOI's design wins.

**Inference:** Relevant supplier, but insufficient T0 evidence for a company-transforming 100G datacenter engine.

**Frozen state:** **REJECT / Medium**.

## Negative / boundary search

- **Acacia Communications:** architecture leader, but not publicly listed until May 2016; excluded from the T0 investable set.
- Larger/diversified optical suppliers are useful bottleneck comparators but fail the desired company-transformation purity test without stronger T0 materiality evidence.
- The scan does not assume every 100G supplier benefits: rapid ASP decline, integration and customer concentration are explicit falsifiers.

## D01 frozen prediction

The strongest v1 prediction is **AAOI PROMOTE**, because the evidence available before 4 January 2016 combined hyperscale 100G design wins, vertical integration in enabling laser/light-engine components and already-accelerating group revenue while management still described the 100G transition as beginning.

Oclaro and NeoPhotonics validate the architecture but appear later in the economic-conversion curve. Lumentum lacks a sufficiently specific T0 state change.
