# H04 — Stacked high-bandwidth memory for accelerators

**T0:** 3 January 2023. **Phase:** PRE-OUTCOME PREDICTION. H04 was deliberately sealed during D09; only <=T0 HBM source packets enter this freeze.

## Architecture and constraint

**FACT:** SK hynix announced in June 2022 that it mass-produced HBM3 for NVIDIA H100, with stacked memory bandwidth as the device-level constraint. Micron's June 2022 prepared remarks said HBM2E entered volume shipment for AI/ML workloads; its FY2022 10-K lists HBM2E but no HBM-only company profit. Samsung publicly described HBM-PIM and possible HBM3 applications in 2021. [SRC-BT261-H04-001–004]

**INTERPRETATION:** Stacking, thermal control, TSV/bond yield, test and DRAM die allocation can constrain accelerator bandwidth. A named production socket is strong company-capture evidence, but memory cycles, foundry/package integration and product revenue attribution still matter. Do not import later AI-demand magnitude or HBM3E disclosures.

## Frozen candidate states

- **SK hynix (KRX: 000660) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Medium.** Named HBM3/H100 production validates a direct supplier socket. Disconfirmation: DRAM cyclicality, customer concentration, capital intensity. Missing bridge: HBM share of total DRAM/company revenue and profitable FCF at T0, plus market recognition. Next: separately disclosed HBM mix/margins and multi-customer capacity reservations. [SRC-BT261-H04-001]
- **Micron (NASDAQ: MU) — EVIDENCE-BUILD / Low.** HBM2E volume shipments and AI/ML use prove relevance, but no T0 HBM3 named qualification or group-material emerging engine recovered. Disconfirmation: product generation gap and downcycle. Missing bridge: next-generation production socket -> material high-margin group sales/FCF. Next: HBM3 sample/qualification and HBM revenue split. [SRC-BT261-H04-002–003]
- **Samsung Electronics (KRX: 005930) — REJECT for early HBM-specific promotion / Low.** HBM technology R&D and broad memory manufacturing do not prove a new qualified HBM3 supplier engine, and group size obscures materiality. Disconfirmation: integration, competing memory allocations. Missing bridge: named commercial HBM3 socket and incremental group profit. Next: customer-volume qualification and HBM contribution. [SRC-BT261-H04-004]
