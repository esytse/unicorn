### Access and geography as a cross-cutting constraint

**FACT:** [BIS 16 November announcement](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/bis-issues-rule-addressing-remote-access-american-super-intelligence-technology) describes a new interim rule addressing remote access through third-country datacentres; the [rule text](https://www.bis.gov/media/documents/rin-0694-ak50.pdf) is the authority for scope/thresholds. [MOFCOM 9 November notice2026-58](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/ztfl/dwmygl/art/2026/art_312a647b76cb4eb1b98128c6d0e81ae5.html) continues suspension of specified2025 measures through 10 January2027. This is not a finding that every rare-earth restriction is lifted or that all remote cloud services are banned.

**INTERPRETATION:** distinguish physical capacity from capacity a particular customer can lawfully and economically access. Localization may create equipment demand and qualification costs, but can also duplicate capacity, restrict customers, increase capital intensity and destroy supplier rents. Test revenue geography, customer/end-user exposure, controlled inputs, qualified substitutes and funded new capacity in the affected underwrite. Do not assign a blanket China premium/discount or invent a policy probability. The supplied article is a discovery trigger; its other dramatic event/valuation claims were not imported as verified evidence.
