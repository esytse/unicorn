## Source-quality note

Developer timelines, projected plant costs and LCOE are not treated as established facts. Company sources are used primarily to establish contracts, technical demonstrations and declared relationships; financial materiality must be independently tested before portfolio promotion. Where company pages conflict on contract attribution, the disagreement is preserved and no revenue/backlog is inferred without the stronger primary evidence.
