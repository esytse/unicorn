## Source quality notes

Prefer, roughly in this order:

1. Regulatory filings and audited disclosures
2. Technical standards, peer-reviewed papers and official documentation
3. Company primary materials, with commercial claims treated cautiously
4. High-quality industry research and reputable journalism
5. Secondary summaries and community discussion for hypothesis generation only

Record disagreements between credible sources rather than deleting inconvenient evidence.
